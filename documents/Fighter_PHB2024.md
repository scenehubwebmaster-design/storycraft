# 🌿 The Druid
> **“The land remembers what men forget.”**

Druids are ancient guardians of nature’s balance, drawing upon the primal forces of the world — life, growth, and decay.  
Through communion with beasts, plants, and the elements, Druids wield their magic to heal, protect, and transform, becoming avatars of the natural order.

---

| **Trait** | **Details** |
|------------|-------------|
| **Primary Ability** | Wisdom |
| **Hit Point Die** | 1d8 per Druid level |
| **Saving Throws** | Intelligence, Wisdom |
| **Skill Proficiencies** | Choose 2: Arcana, Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Religion, or Survival |
| **Weapon Proficiencies** | Simple weapons |
| **Tool Proficiencies** | Herbalism Kit |
| **Armor Training** | Light Armor, Shields |
| **Starting Equipment** | Choose:<br>**A)** Leather Armor, Shield, Sickle, Druidic Focus (Quarterstaff), Explorer’s Pack, Herbalism Kit, and 9 GP<br>**B)** 50 GP |

> **Note:** Druids revere nature above all. Many guard ancient groves or sacred lands, while others wander as stewards of the wilds.

---

## 🌲 Becoming a Druid

### As a Level 1 Character
- Gain all traits from the **Core Druid Traits** table.  
- Gain the Druid’s level 1 features from the **Druid Features Table**.

### As a Multiclass Character
- Gain the following traits:  
  * Hit Point Die  
  * Training with Light Armor and Shields  
- Gain the Druid’s level 1 features, then see *Chapter 2: Multiclassing* for spell slot rules.

## 🌳 Class Features

### **Level 1: Spellcasting**
You have learned to channel nature’s energy through study and attunement to its primal rhythms.

- **Cantrips Known:** 2 (*Druidcraft*, *Produce Flame* recommended)  
- **Spell Slots:** See the Druid Features Table  
- **Prepared Spells:** Choose 4 Level 1 spells (*Animal Friendship*, *Cure Wounds*, *Faerie Fire*, *Thunderwave* recommended)  
- **Changing Spells:** You can change prepared spells after a Long Rest  
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Wisdom  
- **Spellcasting Focus:** Druidic Focus  

> **Example:**  
> *A Druid cups their hands; motes of light flicker between their fingers, blooming into fireflies that illuminate the grove.*

---

### **Level 1: Druidic**
You know **Druidic**, the secret language of Druids.  
You can leave hidden messages that only those fluent in Druidic can read.  
You also always have *Speak with Animals* prepared.

---

### **Level 1: Primal Order**
Choose your sacred calling:

- **Magician.** Learn one extra Druid cantrip. Add your Wisdom modifier to Arcana or Nature checks.  
- **Warden.** Gain proficiency with **Martial Weapons** and **Medium Armor**.

---

### **Level 2: Wild Shape**
As a Bonus Action, assume the form of a **Beast** you have learned (see Known Forms).

- **Uses:** 2; regain 1 on Short Rest, all on Long Rest  
- **Duration:** Hours equal to half your Druid level  
- **Known Forms:** 4 beasts (CR ≤ ¼, no Fly Speed). Recommended: *Rat*, *Riding Horse*, *Spider*, *Wolf*  
- **Temporary HP:** Gain Temp HP equal to your Druid level  
- **Game Stats:** Replaced by the Beast’s stat block except you retain Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, languages, and class features  
- **No Spellcasting** in form (but Concentration persists)  
- **Equipment:** Falls, merges, or is worn per DM discretion  

**Beast Shape Scaling**

| **Level** | **Known Forms** | **Max CR** | **Fly Speed** |
|------------|------------------|-------------|----------------|
| 2 | 4 | ¼ | No |
| 4 | 6 | ½ | No |
| 8 | 8 | 1 | Yes |

---

### **Level 2: Wild Companion**
You can expend a spell slot or Wild Shape use to cast *Find Familiar* (Fey spirit only, no material components).  
The familiar disappears after a Long Rest.

---

### **Level 3: Druid Subclass**
Choose a **Circle** that defines your approach to nature’s power:
- Circle of the Land  
- Circle of the Moon  
- Circle of the Sea  
- Circle of the Stars

---

### **Level 5: Wild Resurgence**
Once per turn, if you have no Wild Shape uses, you can regain one by expending a spell slot.  
Alternatively, you can convert one Wild Shape use into a 1st-level spell slot (usable once per Long Rest).

---

### **Level 7: Elemental Fury**
Choose one option:

- **Potent Spellcasting:** Add Wisdom modifier to damage of your Druid cantrips.  
- **Primal Strike:** Once per turn when you hit with a weapon or Beast form attack, deal +1d8 Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage.

---

### **Level 15: Improved Elemental Fury**
Your chosen Elemental Fury option improves:

- **Potent Spellcasting:** Increase spell range by 300 ft for cantrips with ≥10 ft range.  
- **Primal Strike:** Bonus damage increases to 2d8.

---

### **Level 18: Beast Spells**
You can now cast spells while in Wild Shape, except those requiring costly or consumed Material components.

---

### **Level 19: Epic Boon**
Gain an **Epic Boon feat** (see Chapter 5).  
> **Recommended:** *Boon of Dimensional Travel.*

---

### **Level 20: Archdruid**
You become one with nature itself.

- **Evergreen Wild Shape:** When you roll Initiative with no Wild Shape uses left, regain one.  
- **Nature Magician:** Convert Wild Shape uses into spell slots (2 spell levels per use; once per Long Rest).  
- **Longevity:** You age only one year for every ten that pass.

## 🌕 Druid Circles (Subclasses)

> Each Druid Circle represents a sacred philosophy of nature — earth, moon, sea, and stars — reflecting how Druids interact with the world’s primal forces.

---

### **Circle of the Land**
*“Celebrate connection to the natural world.”*

**Circle of the Land Spells**  
Choose a land type each Long Rest; you always have its spells prepared.

| **Land Type** | **3rd** | **5th** | **7th** | **9th** |
|---------------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Arid** | *Blur*, *Burning Hands*, *Fire Bolt* | *Fireball* | *Blight* | *Wall of Stone* |
| **Polar** | *Fog Cloud*, *Hold Person*, *Ray of Frost* | *Sleet Storm* | *Ice Storm* | *Cone of Cold* |
| **Temperate** | *Misty Step*, *Sleep*, *Shocking Grasp* | *Lightning Bolt* | *Freedom of Movement* | *Tree Stride* |
| **Tropical** | *Acid Splash*, *Ray of Sickness*, *Web* | *Stinking Cloud* | *Polymorph* | *Insect Plague* |

**Level 3: Land’s Aid**  
As a Magic Action, expend a Wild Shape use to cause flowers and thorns to erupt in a 10 ft radius within 60 ft.  
Each creature of choice makes a Con save, taking 2d6 Necrotic damage or half on success.  
One creature regains 2d6 HP. Damage/healing increases by 1d6 at levels 10 and 14.

**Level 6: Natural Recovery**  
Once per Long Rest, cast a Circle Spell without expending a slot.  
After a Short Rest, recover spell slots equal to half your Druid level (rounded up).  

**Level 10: Nature’s Ward**  
You gain immunity to Poisoned and Resistance based on land type:

| **Land** | **Resistance** |
|-----------|----------------|
| Arid | Fire |
| Polar | Cold |
| Temperate | Lightning |
| Tropical | Poison |

**Level 14: Nature’s Sanctuary**  
As a Magic Action, expend a Wild Shape use to summon spectral trees in a 15 ft cube within 120 ft.  
Allies gain Half Cover and your current Resistance.  
As a Bonus Action, move the cube 60 ft.

---

### **Circle of the Moon**
*“Adopt animal forms to guard the wilds.”*

**Level 3: Circle Forms**  
When you Wild Shape:  
- Max CR = Druid level ÷ 3  
- AC = 13 + Wisdom modifier (if higher)  
- Gain Temp HP = 3 × Druid level  

**Circle of the Moon Spells**

| **Level** | **Spells** |
|------------|------------|
| 3 | *Cure Wounds*, *Moonbeam*, *Starry Wisp* |
| 5 | *Conjure Animals* |
| 7 | *Fount of Moonlight* |
| 9 | *Mass Cure Wounds* |

**Level 10: Moonlight Step**  
Bonus Action teleport 30 ft; gain Advantage on next attack. Uses = Wisdom modifier (min 1), regain after Long Rest or by expending Level 2+ spell slots.

**Level 14: Lunar Form**  
Once per turn, deal +2d10 Radiant damage with Wild Shape attacks.  
When using Moonlight Step, teleport one willing creature within 10 ft with you.

---

### **Circle of the Sea**
*“Become one with tides and storms.”*

**Circle of the Sea Spells**

| **Level** | **Spells** |
|------------|------------|
| 3 | *Fog Cloud*, *Gust of Wind*, *Ray of Frost*, *Shatter*, *Thunderwave* |
| 5 | *Lightning Bolt*, *Water Breathing* |
| 7 | *Control Water*, *Ice Storm* |
| 9 | *Conjure Elemental*, *Hold Monster* |

**Level 3: Wrath of the Sea**  
Bonus Action: expend Wild Shape to manifest ocean spray in a 5 ft aura for 10 minutes.  
Each turn, choose a creature in the aura → Con save or take Cold damage (d6s = Wisdom mod) and pushed 15 ft.

**Level 6: Aquatic Affinity**  
Aura expands to 10 ft. Gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

**Level 10: Stormborn**  
While Wrath is active:  
- Gain a Fly Speed equal to Speed  
- Gain Resistance to Cold, Lightning, Thunder

**Level 14: Oceanic Gift**  
Manifest Wrath of the Sea around an ally within 60 ft.  
Optionally affect both yourself and the ally by expending 2 Wild Shape uses.

---

### **Circle of the Stars**
*“Harness secrets hidden in constellations.”*

**Level 3: Star Map**  
You possess a **Star Map** — a magical focus.  
While holding it, you have *Guidance* and *Guiding Bolt* prepared and can cast *Guiding Bolt* a number of times per Long Rest equal to Wisdom modifier.  

> **Star Map Forms:** Scroll, Stone Tablet, Engraved Crystal, Starry Hide, Ebony Codex, Glass Disk

**Level 3: Starry Form**  
Bonus Action: expend Wild Shape to assume a luminous astral form for 10 minutes.  
Sheds Bright Light 10 ft, Dim Light 10 ft. Choose a constellation:  

- **Archer:** As a Bonus Action, make a ranged spell attack (1d8 + Wisdom mod Radiant damage, range 60 ft).  
- **Chalice:** When you heal a creature, another creature within 30 ft regains 1d8 + Wisdom mod HP.  
- **Dragon:** Treat rolls of 9 or lower as 10 on Concentration or Int/Wis checks.

**Level 6: Cosmic Omen**  
After a Long Rest, roll a die:  
- **Even (Weal):** Add 1d6 to an ally’s D20 Test.  
- **Odd (Woe):** Subtract 1d6 from a foe’s D20 Test.  
Uses = Wisdom modifier, regain on Long Rest.

**Level 10: Twinkling Constellations**  
Your constellations improve:  
- Archer/Chalice = 2d8  
- Dragon grants Fly Speed 20 ft (hover)  
You may change constellations at the start of your turn.

**Level 14: Full of Stars**  
While in Starry Form, gain **Resistance** to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

# 🌾 Summary

> **Core Identity:** Nature’s guardian and primal spellcaster  
> **Primary Role:** Healer / Shapeshifter / Elementalist  
> **Complexity:** High  
> **Synergy With:** Rangers, Barbarians, Monks, and other Wisdom-based allies
