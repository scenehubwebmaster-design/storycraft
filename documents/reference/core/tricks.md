## Trick Effects (d100)

- **01–03**: Ages the first toucher  
- **04–06**: Object animates / animates nearby items  
- **07–10**: Asks 3 riddles; success spawns a reward  
- **11–13**: Grants resistance or vulnerability  
- **14–16**: Alters alignment/personality/size/appearance/sex  
- **17–19**: Transmutes substance (gold→lead, metal→crystal)  
- **20–22**: Creates a force field  
- **23–26**: Projects an illusion  
- **27–29**: Suppresses magic items temporarily  
- **30–32**: Enlarge/Reduce creature(s)  
- **33–35**: Magic mouth riddle/utterance  
- **36–38**: **Confusion** in 10-ft radius  
- **39–41**: Gives directions (true or false)  
- **42–44**: Grants a **wish** (dangerous, limited)  
- **45–47**: Flies about to avoid contact  
- **48–50**: **Suggestion** on viewers  
- **51–53**: Adjusts or negates gravity  
- **54–56**: Induces **greed**  
- **57–59**: Holds an imprisoned being  
- **60–62**: Locks/unlocks exits  
- **63–65**: Game of chance for a reward or info  
- **66–68**: Helps or harms certain creature types  
- **69–71**: **Polymorph** (1 hour)  
- **72–75**: Presents puzzle/logic test  
- **76–78**: Prevents movement (sticky/locking)  
- **79–81**: Vends coins/gems/map (real or false)  
- **82–84**: Summons or becomes a monster  
- **85–87**: **Suggestion** (alternate charm)  
- **88–90**: Wails loudly on touch (alarm)  
- **91–93**: Talks (nonsense/poetry/singing/spells)  
- **94–97**: **Teleport** elsewhere  
- **98–00**: Swap minds of two or more creatures |
