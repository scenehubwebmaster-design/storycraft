## Notes

- **Versatile.** Damage in parentheses is used when wielded with two hands.
- **Thrown (x/y).** Indicates the weapon can be thrown, with normal and long ranges.
- **Finesse.** Allows use of Dexterity for attack and damage rolls.
- **Heavy, Two-Handed, Reach.** See _Player’s Handbook_ for detailed rules.
- **Special.** Describes unique attack or usage conditions for the weapon.
