# 🧬 Halfling

> **Summary:**  
> Small in stature but large in spirit, Halflings are the heart of community and adventure alike. Their luck and charm carry them through peril where others might fall.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 2–3 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** Around 150 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Cheerful faces, curly hair, nimble movements, and keen eyes.

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Small |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |

---

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Brave
You have **Advantage** on saves to avoid or end the **Frightened** condition.

#### Halfling Nimbleness
You can move through the space of any creature larger than you (but not end your turn there).

#### Lucky
When you roll a natural `1` on a d20 test, you can reroll and must use the new result.

#### Naturally Stealthy
You can **Hide** even when only lightly obscured by a creature at least one size larger.

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Halflings bring optimism and courage to even the darkest journeys.  
> - Many believe a halfling’s “luck” is divine favor from Yondalla or Brandobaris.
