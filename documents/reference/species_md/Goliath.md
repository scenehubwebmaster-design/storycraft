# 🧬 Goliath

> **Summary:**  
> Towering descendants of giants, Goliaths carry elemental blessings in their blood. They are born of the mountains, embodying endurance, strength, and the unyielding will of the titans.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 7–8 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** Around 100 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Stone-carved skin patterns, elemental glow, and massive physiques.

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Speed** | 35 ft |

---

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Giant Ancestry
Choose one ancestral boon. You can use it PB times per **Long Rest**.

| **Giant Type** | **Power** |
|----------------|-----------|
| Cloud | `Teleport 30 ft` as a Bonus Action. |
| Fire | Add `1d10 Fire` damage on a hit. |
| Frost | Add `1d6 Cold` and reduce target’s Speed by 10 ft. |
| Hill | Knock a Large or smaller target **Prone** on a hit. |
| Stone | Reaction to reduce damage by `1d12 + Con mod`. |
| Storm | When damaged by a creature within 60 ft, deal `1d8 Thunder` back. |

#### Large Form
At level 5, you can grow to **Large size** for 10 minutes, gaining +10 ft Speed and **Advantage on Strength checks**.  
Usable once per **Long Rest**.

#### Powerful Build
You have **Advantage** on saves against the **Grappled** condition and count as one size larger for carrying capacity.

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Goliaths strive to honor their giant ancestors while forging independent destinies.  
> - They respect strength, challenge, and the thrill of overcoming the impossible.
