# 🧬 Tiefling

> **Summary:**  
> Descendants of fiends or shaped by infernal pacts, Tieflings carry the legacy of the Lower Planes. They stand between worlds — not inherently evil, but forever marked by it.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 4–7 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** About 100 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Horns, tails, pointed teeth, unusual eye colors, and skin tones from red to violet.

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Medium or Small |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |

---

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Darkvision
You can see in dim light within 60 ft as if it were bright light.

#### Fiendish Legacy
You inherit powers from one infernal lineage:

| **Legacy** | **Resistance** | **Cantrip** | **Level 3** | **Level 5** |
|-------------|----------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Abyssal** | Poison | `Poison Spray` | `Ray of Sickness` | `Hold Person` |
| **Chthonic** | Necrotic | `Chill Touch` | `False Life` | `Ray of Enfeeblement` |
| **Infernal** | Fire | `Fire Bolt` | `Hellish Rebuke` | `Darkness` |

You can cast each learned spell once per **Long Rest**, and your casting ability is **Int/Wis/Cha** (choose on creation).

#### Otherworldly Presence
You know the `Thaumaturgy` cantrip.

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Tieflings wrestle with the pull of their fiendish heritage and the desire for acceptance.  
> - Their power and charm often make them natural leaders, outcasts, or both.
