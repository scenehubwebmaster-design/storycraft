# 🧬 Orc

> **Summary:**  
> Born of divine endurance and primal strength, Orcs embody the will of Gruumsh — to survive, to strive, and to never yield.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 6–7 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** About 80 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Gray-green skin, tusks, broad shoulders, and eyes that burn with fierce vitality.

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |

---

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Adrenaline Rush
You can take the **Dash** action as a **Bonus Action**, gaining temporary HP equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**.  
Usable PB times per **Short or Long Rest**.

#### Darkvision
You can see in dim light within 120 ft as if it were bright light.

#### Relentless Endurance
When reduced to 0 HP (but not killed), drop to 1 HP instead.  
Usable once per **Long Rest**.

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Orcs balance primal instinct with hard-won discipline.  
> - They see endurance as the truest measure of honor.
