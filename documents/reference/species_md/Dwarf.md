# 🧬 Dwarf

> **Summary:**  
> Forged from stone and spirit by divine hands, Dwarves are enduring artisans, warriors, and builders. Their halls echo with the ring of hammers and songs of triumph against all odds.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 4–5 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** About 350 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Stocky build, robust constitution, thick beards, and eyes like polished gems.

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |

---

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Darkvision
You can see in dim light within 120 ft as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light.

#### Dwarven Resilience
You have resistance to `Poison` damage and **Advantage** on saves to avoid or end the **Poisoned** condition.

#### Dwarven Toughness
Your hit point maximum increases by 1, and increases by 1 each time you gain a level.

#### Stonecunning
As a **Bonus Action**, gain **Tremorsense (60 ft)** for 10 minutes while touching or standing on stone.  
Usable a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**, regaining all uses after a **Long Rest**.

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Dwarves value endurance, craftsmanship, and family above all.  
> - Their songs immortalize the courage of the small against the mighty.
