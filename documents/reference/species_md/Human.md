# 🧬 Human

> **Summary:**  
> Humans are the adaptable pioneers of the multiverse, defined by ambition and versatility. Found in every realm, they build, explore, and shape their fates with tireless resolve.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 4–7 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** Around 80 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Highly variable; humans display a vast range of colors, builds, and temperaments.

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Medium or Small (choose on creation) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |

---

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Resourceful
You gain **Heroic Inspiration** whenever you finish a **Long Rest**.

#### Skillful
You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.

#### Versatile
You gain one **Origin Feat** of your choice.  
> *Recommended:* `Skilled`

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Humans are relentless dreamers and doers; every generation reshapes the world anew.  
> - Their diversity makes them unpredictable and endlessly surprising.
