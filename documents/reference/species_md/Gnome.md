# 🧬 Gnome

> **Summary:**  
> Curious, clever, and irrepressibly inventive, Gnomes are small folk with a love for illusion, artifice, and the hidden workings of the world.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 3–4 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** Around 425 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Large eyes, pointed ears, nimble hands, and vivid hair colors.

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Small |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |

---

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Darkvision
You can see in dim light within 60 ft as if it were bright light.

#### Gnomish Cunning
You have **Advantage** on all **Intelligence**, **Wisdom**, and **Charisma** saving throws.

#### Gnomish Lineage
Choose one of the following lineages:

| **Lineage** | **Features** |
|--------------|--------------|
| **Forest Gnome** | You know `Minor Illusion`. You also always have `Speak with Animals` prepared, usable PB times per **Long Rest**. |
| **Rock Gnome** | You know `Mending` and `Prestidigitation`. You can spend 10 minutes using `Prestidigitation` to create a small clockwork device that performs a simple magical effect for 8 hours. You can maintain up to 3 at a time. |

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Gnomes delight in discovery and invention; they see magic as another form of tinkering.  
> - Their humor and mischief often conceal sharp intelligence and boundless creativity.
