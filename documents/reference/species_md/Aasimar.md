# 🧬 Aasimar

> **Summary:**  
> Aasimar are mortals touched by the divine. Whether descended from celestial beings or blessed by radiant power, they carry within them a spark of the heavens — a light that can heal, protect, or destroy the wicked.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 4–7 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** Up to 160 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Luminous eyes, metallic freckles, halos, or iridescent skin tones (silver, copper, opalescent green).

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Medium (4–7 ft) or Small (2–4 ft), chosen when selected |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |

---

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Celestial Resistance
You have resistance to both `Necrotic` and `Radiant` damage.

#### Darkvision
You can see in dim light within 60 ft as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light.

#### Healing Hands
As a **Magic Action**, you can touch a creature and restore hit points equal to your `Proficiency Bonus × d4`.  
Once used, this trait can’t be used again until after a **Long Rest**.

#### Light Bearer
You know the `Light` cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.

#### Celestial Revelation
At **character level 3**, you can use a **Bonus Action** to transform for 1 minute (or until you end it). Choose one form below.  
You can use this transformation once per **Long Rest**.

> **While transformed:**  
> Once per turn, when you deal damage with an attack or spell, you deal extra damage equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**.  
> The damage type depends on your Revelation form.

| **Form** | **Effect** |
|-----------|------------|
| **Heavenly Wings** | Spectral wings sprout; you gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. Damage type: Radiant. |
| **Inner Radiance** | Searing light shines from your eyes and mouth; you emit **Bright Light (10 ft)** and **Dim Light (10 ft)**. Each creature within 10 ft at the end of your turn takes Radiant damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus. |
| **Necrotic Shroud** | Your eyes turn black, and spectral, flightless wings appear. Each creature (except allies) within 10 ft must make a Charisma save (DC = 8 + Charisma mod + PB) or be **Frightened** until the end of your next turn. Damage type: Necrotic. |

---

## 🪄 Innate Magic
> **Cantrip:** `Light`  
> **Level 3:** Celestial Revelation (varies by form)  
> **Casting Ability:** Charisma  

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Aasimar are often drawn to acts of compassion, justice, or radiant vengeance.  
> - When your celestial nature manifests, your personality may shift — serene calm for Heavenly Wings, wrathful brilliance for Inner Radiance, or grim silence for Necrotic Shroud.
