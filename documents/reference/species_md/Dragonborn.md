# 🧬 Dragonborn

> **Summary:**  
> Descendants of dragons and blessed by draconic gods, Dragonborn carry the might and majesty of their progenitors. Their scales shimmer with ancestral hues, and their breath channels elemental fury.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 5–7 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** Around 80 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Dragonlike scales, horns, and reptilian eyes; coloration matches their draconic ancestry.

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Draconic Ancestry
Choose your lineage from the table below. It determines your **Breath Weapon**, **Damage Resistance**, and **appearance**.

| **Dragon Type** | **Damage Type** |
|------------------|----------------|
| Black | Acid |
| Blue | Lightning |
| Brass | Fire |
| Bronze | Lightning |
| Copper | Acid |
| Gold | Fire |
| Green | Poison |
| Red | Fire |
| Silver | Cold |
| White | Cold |

#### Breath Weapon
When you take the **Attack Action**, you can replace one attack with a magical breath in a **15 ft Cone** or **30 ft Line (5 ft wide)**.  
Each creature in that area must make a **Dexterity save** (DC = 8 + Con mod + PB).  
- On fail: takes `1d10` damage of your ancestry’s type.  
- On success: half as much.  
Damage increases to `2d10` (Lv 5), `3d10` (Lv 11), and `4d10` (Lv 17).  
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **Proficiency Bonus**, regaining all uses after a **Long Rest**.

#### Damage Resistance
You have resistance to your ancestral damage type.

#### Darkvision
You can see in darkness within 60 ft as if it were dim light.

#### Draconic Flight
At **character level 5**, you can use a **Bonus Action** to sprout spectral wings, granting a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed** for 10 minutes.  
You can’t use this again until after a **Long Rest**.

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Dragonborn often carry immense pride, ambition, and a code of honor tied to their lineage.  
> - The color of your scales may influence your temperament and elemental affinity.
