# 🧬 Elf

> **Summary:**  
> Born of magic and grace, Elves are immortalized by their poise and longevity. Created by Corellon, they embody the beauty and tragedy of lost divinity, ever adapting to new realms and roles.

---

## 🪞 Appearance and Lifespan
- **Average Height:** 5–6 feet  
- **Average Lifespan:** Around 750 years  
- **Distinctive Traits:** Pointed ears, ageless features, luminous eyes, and a serene aura.

---

## ⚙️ Species Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Creature Type** | Humanoid |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |

---

### 🧠 Core Abilities

#### Darkvision
You can see in dim light within 60 ft as if it were bright light.

#### Fey Ancestry
You have advantage on saves to avoid or end the **Charmed** condition.

#### Keen Senses
You gain proficiency in **Insight**, **Perception**, or **Survival**.

#### Trance
You don’t sleep. Four hours of meditation count as a **Long Rest**, and you remain aware of your surroundings.

#### Elven Lineage
You gain one of the following magical lineages:

| **Lineage** | **Level 1** | **Level 3** | **Level 5** |
|--------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Drow** | `Dancing Lights` | `Faerie Fire` | `Darkness` |
| **High Elf** | `Prestidigitation` (swapable each rest) | `Detect Magic` | `Misty Step` |
| **Wood Elf** | `Druidcraft` | `Longstrider` | `Pass without Trace` |

Each spell can be cast once per **Long Rest** without a slot, and uses **Int/Wis/Cha** (your choice) as the casting ability.

---

> **Roleplaying Notes:**  
> - Elves embody the patience of centuries and the weight of memory.  
> - Your lineage influences how your magic manifests — a dark shimmer for Drow, a gentle aura for Wood Elves, or a flash of intellect for High Elves.
