# D&D 5e Rest and Recovery Rules

## Short Rest

**Duration**: At least 1 hour
**Activities**: Eating, drinking, reading, tending wounds, light activity
**Cannot**: Marching, fighting, spellcasting, strenuous activity

### Benefits

- **Hit Dice Recovery**: Spend hit dice to regain hit points
  - Roll hit die (d6 for wizards, d8 for rogues, d10 for fighters, d12 for barbarians, etc.)
  - Add Constitution modifier to roll
  - Regain that many hit points
  - Can spend multiple hit dice (up to your maximum)
- **Class Features**: Some classes regain abilities
  - Warlock: Regain all Pact Magic spell slots
  - Fighter: Regain Second Wind (if expended)
  - Monk: Regain ki points (if any expended)
  - Wizard (Arcane Recovery): Once per day, recover spell slots equal to half wizard level (rounded up), max 5th level
  - Bard: Regain Bardic Inspiration uses (equal to Charisma modifier)
  - Druid (Natural Recovery): Once per day, recover spell slots (total levels = half druid level, rounded up)

### Limitations

- Must have at least 1 hit point to benefit
- Cannot regain more hit points than maximum
- Interruption: If more than 1 minute of strenuous activity, must start over

## Long Rest

**Duration**: At least 8 hours
**Activities**: Sleeping, light activity (reading, talking, eating, standing watch) for max 2 hours
**Cannot**: Marching, fighting, spellcasting, other strenuous activity for more than 1 hour

### Benefits

- **Hit Points**: Regain all lost hit points
- **Hit Dice**: Regain hit dice equal to half your total (minimum 1)
  - Example: Level 6 character regains 3 hit dice
- **Spell Slots**: Regain all expended spell slots
- **Class Features**: Regain all limited-use abilities
  - Wizard: Can change prepared spells
  - Cleric/Druid: Can change prepared spells
  - Warlock: Regain all Pact Magic slots (same as short rest)
  - Barbarian: Regain all Rage uses
  - Paladin: Regain all Lay on Hands points
  - Monk: Regain all ki points
  - Fighter: Regain Action Surge, Second Wind
  - Sorcerer: Regain Sorcery Points
  - Bard: Regain Bardic Inspiration uses
  - Rogue: No limited uses, but regains health/hit dice

### Limitations

- **Once per 24 hours**: Can't benefit from long rest more than once in 24-hour period
- **Minimum 1 HP**: Must have at least 1 hit point at start to benefit
- **Interruption**: If interrupted by combat or 1+ hour of strenuous activity, must start over
  - Can resume if interruption less than 1 hour

## Exhaustion

Exhaustion levels affect all activities:

### Level 1

- Disadvantage on ability checks

### Level 2

- Speed halved
- Disadvantage on ability checks

### Level 3

- Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws
- Speed halved
- Disadvantage on ability checks

### Level 4

- Hit point maximum halved
- All above penalties

### Level 5

- Speed reduced to 0
- All above penalties

### Level 6

- Death

### Recovering from Exhaustion

- **Long Rest**: Reduce exhaustion by 1 level (if you eat and drink)
- **Greater Restoration**: Removes 1 level
- Some items/spells: May remove exhaustion

### Causes of Exhaustion

- Going without food/water (multiple days)
- Extreme cold or heat
- Forced march beyond normal pace
- Some spells/abilities (e.g., Frenzy Barbarian)

## Food and Water

### Daily Requirements

- **Food**: 1 pound per day
  - Can go without food for 3 + Constitution modifier days (minimum 1)
  - After that: Gain 1 exhaustion level per day
- **Water**: 1 gallon per day (2 gallons in hot weather)
  - Can go without water for 1 day
  - After that: Gain 1 exhaustion level per day (2 levels in hot weather)

### Foraging

- **DC 10 Wisdom (Survival)** check while traveling
- Find food and water equal to 1d6 + Wisdom modifier pounds
- Takes 1 hour (slow travel pace)

## Sleep and Rest Requirements

### Sleep Deprivation

Optional rule (DM discretion):

- **No Long Rest**: After 24 hours without sleep, make DC 10 Constitution save
- Failure: Gain 1 exhaustion level
- Repeat every 24 hours without sleep (DC increases by 5 each time)

### Elves (Trance)

- **4 hours**: Elves enter trance instead of sleep
- Counts as 8-hour rest for long rest benefits
- Remain semiconscious (advantage on Perception checks)

### Warforged (Sentry's Rest)

- **6 hours**: Enter inactive state
- Remain conscious but have disadvantage on Perception checks
- Counts as long rest

## Healing During Rest

### Short Rest Healing

- **Hit Dice**: Only method during short rest
- **Healer's Kit**: Can stabilize dying creature (no hit points restored without feat)
- **Healer Feat**: Use healer's kit to restore 1d6 + 4 + creature's max hit dice

### Long Rest Healing

- **Automatic**: Regain all hit points
- **No Hit Dice Needed**: Full restoration regardless of hit dice remaining

## Interrupting Rest

### Short Rest

- **Combat**: Must start over
- **1 minute strenuous activity**: Must start over
- **Light activity**: Can continue (doesn't reset)

### Long Rest

- **Combat**: Must start over if fight lasts more than 1 hour total interruptions
- **1 hour strenuous activity**: Must start over
- **2 hours light activity**: Can continue (standing watch, etc.)
- **Casting spells**: Light activity unless combat/dangerous

## Resting in Armor

### Medium or Heavy Armor

Optional rule (DM discretion):

- Sleep in armor: Don't regain full benefit of long rest
- Regain only 1/4 hit dice instead of 1/2
- Or: Must make Constitution save or gain exhaustion level

### Light Armor

- No penalties
- Can sleep comfortably

## Downtime During Rest

### Activities (DM discretion)

- **Crafting**: Make progress on item creation
- **Research**: Study books, gather information
- **Training**: Learn new language or tool proficiency
- **Carousing**: Build relationships, gather rumors

## Special Rest Conditions

### Tiny Hut (Leomund's Tiny Hut)

- Creates safe area for long rest
- Protects from weather and intrusion
- Doesn't prevent sound/scent detection

### Magnificent Mansion (Mordenkainen's Magnificent Mansion)

- Safe extradimensional space
- Servants provide comfort
- Cannot be accessed from outside

### Rope Trick

- Hidden space for short rest
- Up to 8 creatures
- Lasts 1 hour (perfect for short rest)

## Resting in Dangerous Areas

### DM Guidelines

- **Random Encounters**: Roll during rest
- **Environmental Hazards**: Extreme temperature may prevent rest
- **Watch Rotation**: Party can take turns (2 hours light activity allowed)
- **Surprise**: Sleeping creatures have disadvantage on Perception

### Watch Schedule

Common rotation for 4-person party during 8-hour rest:

- Each character takes 2-hour watch
- Remaining 6 hours: Sleep/rest
- Watcher can perform light activity

## Hit Dice Recovery

### Long Rest Recovery

- Regain hit dice equal to **half total** (minimum 1)
- Choose which dice to regain if multiclassed
- Example: Level 8 Fighter/Wizard (5/3) regains 4 hit dice
  - Could regain 3 d10 (Fighter) + 1 d6 (Wizard)

### Full Recovery

- Takes **2 consecutive long rests** to regain all hit points and hit dice
- First rest: Regain all HP, half hit dice
- Second rest: Regain remaining hit dice

## Gritty Realism (Optional Rule)

Longer rest periods for grittier campaigns:

- **Short Rest**: 8 hours
- **Long Rest**: 7 days
- Makes resources more precious
- Encourages careful planning

## Healing Potions During Rest

### Short Rest

- Can drink healing potions
- Counts as light activity
- Doesn't interrupt rest

### Long Rest

- Healing potions have normal effect
- Not usually necessary (full HP recovery from rest itself)

## Rest and Spell Preparation

### Prepared Casters (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Wizard)

- **Long Rest**: Can change prepared spells
- Takes part of rest (flavor: morning prayers, studying spellbook)
- Number prepared: Class level + spellcasting modifier (minimum 1)

### Known Casters (Bard, Sorcerer, Ranger, Warlock)

- Cannot change spells during rest
- Only change on level-up

## Rest in Hostile Environments

### Underwater

- Difficult without Water Breathing or similar magic
- Must surface periodically (if breathing air)

### Extreme Heat/Cold

- May require Constitution saves
- Failure: Gain exhaustion
- Magic (Leomund's Tiny Hut) can protect

### Outer Planes

- Some planes prevent rest (e.g., Nine Hells, Abyss)
- DM discretion on whether long rest possible
