# D&D 5e Spellcasting Rules

## Spell Components

### Verbal (V)

- Must be able to speak clearly
- Chanting, speaking, or uttering
- Deafened doesn't prevent but Silenced does

### Somatic (S)

- Requires free hand to make gestures
- Can't cast if hands bound or wielding weapon/shield (unless War Caster feat or hand free)

### Material (M)

- Specific materials listed in spell description
- **Spell Component Pouch**: Contains all common materials
- **Spellcasting Focus**: Can replace material components (unless cost specified or consumed)
- **Cost/Consumed**: Must have exact material if spell lists gold cost or says "consumed"

## Casting Time

- **1 Action**: Most common, cast during your turn
- **1 Bonus Action**: Can still take normal action but no other bonus action spells (except cantrips)
- **1 Reaction**: Cast in response to trigger
- **1 Minute or Longer**: Requires concentration during casting (interrupted if damaged or fail Con save)
- **Ritual**: Add 10 minutes to casting time, no spell slot required (if class allows ritual casting)

## Spell Slots

### Spell Slot Table (by level)

- **Level 1**: 2 slots at 1st
- **Level 2**: 3 slots at 1st
- **Level 3**: 4 at 1st, 2 at 2nd
- **Level 5**: 4 at 1st, 3 at 2nd, 2 at 3rd
- **Level 9**: 4 at 1st-2nd-3rd-4th, 3 at 5th
- **Level 20**: 4 slots at each level 1st through 5th, 2 each at 6th-9th

### Cantrips

- No spell slot required
- Can cast at will
- Some scale with character level (not class level)

### Upcasting

- Cast spell using higher-level slot for additional effects
- Spell description specifies benefits

## Spell Attack Rolls

**Spell Attack Modifier**: Proficiency bonus + spellcasting ability modifier

**Ranged Spell Attack**: 1d20 + spell attack modifier

- Disadvantage if target within 5 feet (unless spell states otherwise)

**Melee Spell Attack**: 1d20 + spell attack modifier

## Spell Save DC

**Spell Save DC**: 8 + proficiency bonus + spellcasting ability modifier

Target rolls: 1d20 + relevant ability modifier + proficiency (if proficient)

- **Success**: Usually half damage or no effect
- **Failure**: Full effect

## Range

- **Self**: Affects only you
- **Touch**: Must touch creature/object
- **Ranged (X feet)**: Can target within distance
- **Sight**: Can target anything you can see
- **Special**: Defined in spell description

### Area of Effect Shapes

**Cone**: Originates from you, widens as it extends
**Cube**: Select point within range, cube extends from that point
**Cylinder**: Circle on ground, extends vertically
**Line**: Extends from you in straight line
**Sphere**: Expands from point you choose within range

## Duration

- **Instantaneous**: Effect happens immediately and ends
- **Concentration, up to X**: Must maintain concentration (see Concentration below)
- **X minutes/hours/days**: Lasts specified time
- **Until Dispelled**: Lasts until Dispel Magic or similar effect
- **Special**: Defined in spell description

## Concentration

**Rules**:

- Can only concentrate on one spell at a time
- Casting another concentration spell ends previous
- Taking damage: Make Constitution save (DC = 10 or half damage taken, whichever is higher)
- Incapacitated or killed: Concentration ends
- Environmental phenomena: DM may call for Constitution save

**Effects**:

- Maintain spell's effects for duration
- Some spells require action on each turn
- Movement doesn't break concentration

## Spell Schools

### Abjuration

- Protective magic, wards, barriers
- Examples: Shield, Counterspell, Protection from Evil

### Conjuration

- Summon creatures/objects, teleportation
- Examples: Misty Step, Conjure Animals, Find Familiar

### Divination

- Reveal information, scry, detect
- Examples: Detect Magic, Identify, Scrying

### Enchantment

- Charm, control minds
- Examples: Charm Person, Suggestion, Dominate Person

### Evocation

- Raw magical energy, damage
- Examples: Fireball, Lightning Bolt, Magic Missile

### Illusion

- Deceive senses, create false images
- Examples: Silent Image, Invisibility, Major Image

### Necromancy

- Manipulate life force, undead
- Examples: Animate Dead, Raise Dead, Vampiric Touch

### Transmutation

- Transform creatures/objects
- Examples: Polymorph, Haste, Enlarge/Reduce

## Spell Levels

- **Cantrips (0-level)**: No slot required, cast at will
- **1st-level**: Basic spells (Magic Missile, Cure Wounds)
- **2nd-level**: Moderate power (Hold Person, Invisibility)
- **3rd-level**: Significant power (Fireball, Counterspell)
- **4th-level**: Strong magic (Polymorph, Wall of Fire)
- **5th-level**: Very powerful (Cone of Cold, Raise Dead)
- **6th-9th level**: Epic magic, limited slots

## Spellcasting Ability

Each class uses different ability for spellcasting:

- **Intelligence**: Wizard, Artificer
- **Wisdom**: Cleric, Druid, Ranger
- **Charisma**: Bard, Paladin, Sorcerer, Warlock

## Prepared Spells vs Known Spells

### Prepared Casters (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Wizard)

- Know many spells (in spellbook or divine knowledge)
- Prepare limited number each long rest
- **Prepared**: Class level + spellcasting modifier (minimum 1)
- Can change prepared spells after long rest

### Known Spells (Bard, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock)

- Limited spells known
- Can't change easily (usually only on level-up)
- Always have access to known spells

## Ritual Casting

Classes with ritual casting (Wizard, Cleric, Druid, Bard):

- Cast spell with ritual tag without using spell slot
- Add 10 minutes to casting time
- Must have spell prepared or in spellbook (Wizard)

## Identifying Spells

**As it's being cast**:

- Reaction to make Arcana check (DC 15 + spell level)
- Success: Identify spell

**Already in effect**:

- Action to make Arcana check (DC 15 + spell level)
- Success: Identify spell

## Counterspell Rules

When creature casts spell:

- **Reaction**: Cast Counterspell
- **Level**: If spell is same or lower level than Counterspell slot used, it's countered
- **Higher level**: Make ability check (spellcasting ability + proficiency vs DC 10 + spell's level)

## Combining Spell Effects

- Effects of same spell cast multiple times don't combine
- Most potent effect applies while durations overlap
- Different spells: Effects combine if similar, or both apply if different

## Targets

- **Willing Target**: Chooses to be affected
- **Unwilling Target**: Can make save if spell allows
- **Self**: Always willing target
- **Object**: Usually no save unless worn/carried

## Spell Scrolls

- Use action to read scroll
- If spell on your class list and you can cast it: Automatically succeed
- If higher level than you can cast: Make ability check (spellcasting ability vs DC 10 + spell level)
- Failure: Scroll is consumed but spell doesn't take effect

## Long Rest Spell Slot Recovery

- Regain all expended spell slots
- Prepare new spells (for prepared casters)

## Short Rest Recovery

- **Warlock**: Regain all Pact Magic slots
- **Wizard (Arcane Recovery)**: Recover slots equal to half wizard level (rounded up)
- **Land Druid (Natural Recovery)**: Similar to Arcane Recovery

## Multiclass Spellcasting

**Spell Slots**: Combined from all classes using table
**Spells Known/Prepared**: Determined separately for each class
**Cantrips**: Know cantrips from all spellcasting classes

## Spell Damage Scaling

Many spells deal more damage when cast using higher-level slots:

- Usually stated as "When you cast this spell using a spell slot of Xth level or higher..."
- Cantrips: Scale with character level (not class level)
  - 5th level: +1 die
  - 11th level: +2 dice
  - 17th level: +3 dice

## Common Spell Mistakes to Avoid

1. Casting bonus action spell + regular spell on same turn (only cantrip allowed)
2. Forgetting concentration checks when damaged
3. Mixing up spell attack rolls and saving throws
4. Not having material components with cost
5. Forgetting somatic components when hands are full
6. Thinking you can split spell effects (you can't target same creature twice with Magic Missile unless it says so)

## Dispelling and Antimagic

**Dispel Magic**: Ends ongoing spell effects

- Automatic if 3rd-level or lower
- Ability check if higher level

**Antimagic Field**: Suppresses all magic within area

- Doesn't dispel, just suppresses
- Effects resume when no longer in field

**Counterspell**: Prevents spell from being cast
