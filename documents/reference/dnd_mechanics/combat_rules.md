# D&D 5e Combat Rules

## Action Economy

Each turn in combat, a creature can take:

- **1 Action**: Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use an Object
- **1 Bonus Action**: If a feature or spell grants it (e.g., offhand weapon attack, cunning action)
- **1 Reaction**: Opportunity attack, certain spells (Shield, Counterspell), readied actions
- **Movement**: Up to creature's speed (can be split before/after action)
- **Free Actions**: Drop an item, speak briefly, interact with one object

## Attack Rolls

**Melee Attack**: 1d20 + Strength modifier + proficiency bonus (if proficient)
**Ranged Attack**: 1d20 + Dexterity modifier + proficiency bonus (if proficient)

**Critical Hit**: Natural 20 - roll all damage dice twice
**Critical Miss**: Natural 1 - automatic miss

**Advantage/Disadvantage**: Roll 2d20, take higher/lower

## Armor Class (AC)

AC determines how hard it is to hit a creature:

- **Light Armor**: 11 + Dex modifier (Leather 11, Studded Leather 12)
- **Medium Armor**: 12-15 + Dex modifier (max +2) (Hide 12, Chain Shirt 13, Scale Mail 14, Breastplate 14, Half Plate 15)
- **Heavy Armor**: 14-18 (no Dex) (Ring Mail 14, Chain Mail 16, Splint 17, Plate 18)
- **Shield**: +2 AC
- **Unarmored**: 10 + Dex modifier (Monks/Barbarians have special calculations)

## Damage Types

- **Physical**: Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
- **Energy**: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Thunder
- **Magical**: Force, Necrotic, Poison, Psychic, Radiant

## Initiative

At start of combat:

1. Each creature rolls: 1d20 + Dexterity modifier
2. DM ranks all creatures from highest to lowest
3. Ties: higher Dexterity goes first, or simultaneous if equal

## Saving Throws

When forced to make a saving throw:
**DC**: 8 + proficiency bonus + ability modifier (for spells/abilities)
**Roll**: 1d20 + relevant ability modifier + proficiency (if proficient)

**Common Saves**:

- **Strength**: Resisting being moved, grappled
- **Dexterity**: Dodging area effects (Fireball, trap)
- **Constitution**: Resisting poison, disease, death
- **Intelligence**: Resisting mental illusions
- **Wisdom**: Resisting charms, fear
- **Charisma**: Resisting banishment, possession

## Cover

- **Half Cover**: +2 AC and Dex saves (low wall, furniture)
- **Three-Quarters Cover**: +5 AC and Dex saves (portcullis, arrow slit)
- **Total Cover**: Can't be targeted directly

## Combat Conditions

### Blinded

- Attack rolls: Disadvantage
- Attacks against: Advantage
- Can't see (auto-fail Perception checks relying on sight)

### Charmed

- Can't attack charmer or target with harmful abilities
- Charmer has advantage on social checks

### Deafened

- Can't hear (auto-fail Perception checks relying on hearing)

### Frightened

- Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while source in sight
- Can't willingly move closer to source

### Grappled

- Speed becomes 0
- Ends if grappler incapacitated or moved away

### Incapacitated

- Can't take actions or reactions

### Invisible

- Attack rolls: Advantage
- Attacks against: Disadvantage
- Can't be seen without magic (heavily obscured for purpose of hiding)

### Paralyzed

- Incapacitated, can't move or speak
- Auto-fail Str and Dex saves
- Attacks against: Advantage
- Hits within 5 feet: Critical hits

### Petrified

- Transformed to stone with possessions
- Incapacitated, can't move or speak
- Unaware of surroundings
- Auto-fail Str and Dex saves
- Attacks against: Advantage
- Resistance to all damage
- Immune to poison and disease

### Poisoned

- Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks

### Prone

- Disadvantage on attack rolls
- Melee attacks against: Advantage
- Ranged attacks against: Disadvantage
- Standing up costs half movement (or full movement if speed 0)

### Restrained

- Speed becomes 0
- Attack rolls: Disadvantage
- Dex saves: Disadvantage
- Attacks against: Advantage

### Stunned

- Incapacitated, can't move, can speak only falteringly
- Auto-fail Str and Dex saves
- Attacks against: Advantage

### Unconscious

- Incapacitated, can't move or speak, unaware
- Drop everything held, fall prone
- Auto-fail Str and Dex saves
- Attacks against: Advantage
- Hits within 5 feet: Critical hits

## Death and Dying

When dropped to 0 HP:

- **Instant Death**: Damage equals or exceeds HP maximum
- **Death Saves**: Roll 1d20 at start of each turn
  - **10+**: Success (3 successes = stabilized at 0 HP)
  - **1-9**: Failure (3 failures = death)
  - **Natural 20**: Regain 1 HP
  - **Natural 1**: Counts as 2 failures
  - **Taking damage**: 1 automatic failure (critical = 2 failures)

**Stabilized**: Stop making death saves but remain unconscious at 0 HP

## Two-Weapon Fighting

With light weapons in each hand:

- Main hand: Normal attack action
- Off-hand: Bonus action attack (no ability modifier to damage unless feature allows)

## Opportunity Attacks

When hostile creature you can see moves out of your reach:

- Use reaction to make one melee attack
- Doesn't provoke if they Disengage first

## Grappling

**Action**: Make special melee attack
**Contest**: Athletics vs Athletics or Acrobatics
**Success**: Target grappled (speed 0)
**Moving**: Drag at half speed (must be at least one size larger)
**Escape**: Action to repeat contest

## Shoving

**Action**: Make special melee attack
**Contest**: Athletics vs Athletics or Acrobatics
**Success**: Push 5 feet or knock prone

## Concentration

Some spells require concentration:

- Duration notes "Concentration, up to X minutes"
- Can only concentrate on one spell at a time
- Ends if: Cast another concentration spell, incapacitated, killed
- **Damage**: Make Constitution save (DC = 10 or half damage, whichever is higher)
- Fail save: Concentration broken, spell ends

## Resistance and Vulnerability

- **Resistance**: Take half damage
- **Vulnerability**: Take double damage
- **Immunity**: Take no damage

Multiple instances of same type don't stack.

## Healing

- **Hit Points Regained**: Immediately effective
- **Can't Exceed Maximum**: Extra HP lost
- **At 0 HP**: Regaining HP brings you back from unconscious

## Objects and Structures

**AC**: Usually 10-20 depending on material
**HP**: Varies by size and material
**Immunity**: Poison, psychic damage
**Damage Threshold**: Some objects ignore damage below threshold

## Range and Distance

**Melee Range**: Typically 5 feet (some reach weapons: 10 feet)
**Ranged Weapons**: Normal range (no penalty) / Long range (disadvantage)
**Spell Range**: Specified in spell description

## Visibility and Light

- **Bright Light**: Normal vision
- **Dim Light**: Lightly obscured (disadvantage on Perception relying on sight)
- **Darkness**: Heavily obscured (blinded condition for those without darkvision)

**Darkvision**: See in darkness as if dim light (black and white) up to specified range

## Underwater Combat

- Disadvantage on melee weapon attacks (unless weapon is dagger, javelin, shortsword, spear, trident)
- Ranged weapon attacks: Automatically miss beyond normal range
- Fire damage: Resistance
- Breath: Can hold for 1 + Con modifier minutes (minimum 30 seconds)
