# D&D Treasure Tables and Rewards

## Treasure by Challenge Rating

### CR 0-4 (Levels 1-4)

#### Individual Treasure

- **Copper**: 5d6 (17 cp average)
- **Silver**: 4d6 (14 sp average)
- **Gold**: 3d6 (10 gp average)
- **Magic Items**: 10% chance of common consumable (potion, scroll)

#### Hoard Treasure (Final Boss)

- **Copper**: 6d6 × 100 (2,100 cp)
- **Silver**: 3d6 × 100 (1,050 sp)
- **Gold**: 2d6 × 10 (70 gp)
- **Gems/Art**: 2d6 (7) pieces worth 10 gp each
- **Magic Items**:
  - 30% chance of 1d4 common items
  - 10% chance of 1 uncommon item

**Example Hoard**:

- 1,800 cp (18 gp)
- 900 sp (90 gp)
- 60 gp
- 5 gems worth 10 gp each (50 gp)
- Potion of Healing
- Bag of Tricks
- **Total: ~220 gp value**

### CR 5-10 (Levels 5-10)

#### Individual Treasure

- **Copper**: 4d6 × 100 (1,400 cp = 14 gp)
- **Silver**: 6d6 × 10 (210 sp = 21 gp)
- **Gold**: 3d6 × 10 (105 gp)
- **Platinum**: 3d6 (10 pp = 100 gp)
- **Magic Items**: 20% chance of uncommon consumable

#### Hoard Treasure

- **Copper**: 2d6 × 100 (700 cp = 7 gp)
- **Silver**: 2d6 × 1,000 (7,000 sp = 700 gp)
- **Gold**: 6d6 × 100 (2,100 gp)
- **Platinum**: 3d6 × 10 (105 pp = 1,050 gp)
- **Gems/Art**: 3d6 (10) pieces worth 50 gp each
- **Magic Items**:
  - 40% chance of 2d4 uncommon items
  - 20% chance of 1d4 rare items

**Example Hoard**:

- 2,100 gp in coins
- 1,050 pp (10,500 gp)
- 8 gems worth 50 gp each (400 gp)
- +1 Weapon (uncommon)
- Cloak of Protection (uncommon)
- Ring of Evasion (rare)
- **Total: ~14,000 gp value**

### CR 11-16 (Levels 11-16)

#### Individual Treasure

- **Silver**: 3d6 × 1,000 (10,500 sp = 1,050 gp)
- **Gold**: 1d6 × 100 (350 gp)
- **Platinum**: 1d6 × 10 (35 pp = 350 gp)
- **Magic Items**: 30% chance of rare consumable

#### Hoard Treasure

- **Gold**: 4d6 × 1,000 (14,000 gp)
- **Platinum**: 5d6 × 100 (1,750 pp = 17,500 gp)
- **Gems/Art**: 3d6 (10) pieces worth 250 gp each, 2d6 (7) worth 750 gp each
- **Magic Items**:
  - 50% chance of 2d4 rare items
  - 30% chance of 1d4 very rare items
  - 10% chance of 1 legendary item

**Example Hoard**:

- 14,000 gp in coins
- 17,500 pp (175,000 gp)
- 8 gems worth 250 gp (2,000 gp)
- 6 gems worth 750 gp (4,500 gp)
- +2 Weapon (rare)
- Mantle of Spell Resistance (rare)
- Rod of Absorption (very rare)
- **Total: ~200,000 gp value**

### CR 17+ (Levels 17-20)

#### Individual Treasure

- **Gold**: 2d6 × 1,000 (7,000 gp)
- **Platinum**: 8d6 × 100 (2,800 pp = 28,000 gp)
- **Magic Items**: 50% chance of very rare consumable

#### Hoard Treasure

- **Gold**: 12d6 × 1,000 (42,000 gp)
- **Platinum**: 8d6 × 1,000 (28,000 pp = 280,000 gp)
- **Gems/Art**: 3d6 (10) pieces worth 1,000 gp, 1d10 (5) pieces worth 2,500 gp, 1d4 art objects worth 7,500 gp
- **Magic Items**:
  - 60% chance of 2d4 very rare items
  - 40% chance of 1d4 legendary items
  - 20% chance of 1 artifact

**Example Hoard**:

- 42,000 gp in coins
- 280,000 pp (2,800,000 gp)
- 8 gems worth 1,000 gp (8,000 gp)
- 4 gems worth 2,500 gp (10,000 gp)
- 2 art objects worth 7,500 gp (15,000 gp)
- +3 Weapon (very rare)
- Cloak of Invisibility (legendary)
- Staff of the Magi (legendary)
- **Total: ~2,900,000 gp value**

## Loot Presentation

### Hidden Treasure

- **Behind False Wall**: DC 15 Investigation
- **Buried Underground**: DC 12 Perception or Investigation
- **Illusory Wall**: DC 15 Investigation or Arcana
- **Secret Compartment**: DC 13 Investigation
- **Magically Concealed**: Detect Magic or Dispel Magic

### Trapped Treasure

- **Poison Needle**: DC 15 Thieves' Tools or take 1d4 poison damage
- **Cursed Gold**: First person to touch makes DC 13 Wisdom save or cursed
- **Mimic**: Chest is actually a monster
- **Alarm**: Bell rings alerting nearby enemies

### Guarded Treasure

- **Monster Lair**: Creature guards treasure jealously
- **Locked Chest**: DC 15 Thieves' Tools or key required
- **Puzzle Lock**: Must solve riddle or mechanism
- **Elemental Guardian**: Summoned protector attacks thieves

## Non-Monetary Rewards

### Information

- Map to dungeon or treasure location
- Villain's plans or journal
- Historical records revealing secrets
- Blackmail material on NPC

### Reputation

- Title or rank in organization
- Fame in region (advantage on Charisma checks)
- Enemy of rival faction
- Legend status (bards sing of deeds)

### Property

- Deed to building or land
- Ship or vehicle
- Business (inn, shop, farm)
- Stronghold or fortress

### Allies

- Henchman or follower
- Favor owed by powerful NPC
- Alliance with faction
- Animal companion or mount

### Services

- Free healing or magic
- Transportation via teleportation circle
- Training in skill or tool
- Access to rare spell components

## Treasure Hoard Variety

Don't just pile coins! Mix it up:

- **50% Coins**: Easy to carry, universal value
- **20% Gems**: Concentrated wealth, easy transport
- **15% Art Objects**: Bulky but valuable
- **10% Magic Items**: Game-changing rewards
- **5% Trade Goods**: Silk, spices, wine (bulk value)

## Creating Memorable Treasure

Add story to loot:

- **Engraved Names**: Previous owner's name on item
- **Historical Significance**: Crown of ancient king
- **Unusual Materials**: Mithral, adamantine, dragon scales
- **Curse or Quirk**: Sword won't sheathe until blooded
- **Connected to Plot**: Villain's family heirloom
