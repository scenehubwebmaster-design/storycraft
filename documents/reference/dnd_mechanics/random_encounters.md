# D&D Random Encounters by Environment

## Forest Encounters

1. **Pixies** (1d4) - Playful but mischievous, may lead party astray or help
2. **Bandits** (2d4) - Demand toll or valuables, may ambush if refused

5. **Unicorn** (1) - Flees unless party is good-aligned and gentle
6. **Elk Herd** (2d6) - Peaceful, can be hunted for food
7. **Treant** (1) - Ancient protector, concerned about forest fires/logging

## Encounter Complications

Add these to make encounters more interesting:

### Environmental

- **Difficult Terrain**: Mud, ice, rubble (half movement)
- **Hazards**: Fire, poison gas, collapsing ceiling
- **Weather**: Rain (disadvantage Perception), fog (heavily obscured)
- **Darkness**: Dim or no light, advantage for creatures with darkvision

### Tactical

- **High Ground**: Ranged attackers on cliff or building (+2 AC, advantage)
- **Cover**: Trees, rocks, walls (+2 or +5 AC)
- **Ambush**: Enemies surprise party (surprised condition first round)
- **Reinforcements**: More enemies arrive on round 3 or when half killed

### Objectives

- **Rescue**: Hostages or prisoners to save
- **Escape**: Party must flee, not fight to death
- **Defense**: Protect location or NPC
- **Time Limit**: Event happens in X rounds (door closes, ritual completes)

### Social

- **Negotiation**: Enemies may parley instead of fight
- **Morale**: Enemies flee when half defeated
- **Bribery**: Enemies accept payment to leave
- **Witnesses**: NPCs watching, affects reputation
