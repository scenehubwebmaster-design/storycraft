# D&D NPC Archetypes and Personalities

## Quick NPC Generation

### The One-Line NPC

Format: **[Profession] + [Distinctive Feature] + [Want]**

Examples:

- **Blacksmith** with burn scars who wants revenge on dragon
- **Innkeeper** with mysterious accent who wants to leave town
- **Guard** with gambling problem who wants to pay off debt

## Voice and Mannerisms

### Physical Quirks

- **Fidgeting**: Taps fingers, twirls hair, adjusts glasses
- **Posture**: Slouches, stands ramrod straight, leans in close
- **Gestures**: Talks with hands, points constantly, crosses arms
- **Tics**: Sniffs frequently, clears throat, blinks rapidly
- **Movement**: Paces, rocks on heels, always in motion

### Speech Patterns

- **Verbose**: Uses flowery language, never uses one word when ten will do
- **Curt**: Speaks in clipped sentences, gets to point
- **Accented**: Distinct regional accent or foreign speech patterns
- **Formal**: Always uses proper titles and etiquette
- **Slang**: Heavy use of colloquialisms and local phrases
- **Repetitive**: Repeats key phrases, has verbal tics ("you see," "as it were")
- **Stuttering**: Struggles with certain sounds or words
- **Whispers**: Speaks quietly, leans in to be heard

## Character Archetypes

### The Mentor

**Role**: Guide, teacher, quest giver
**Traits**: Patient, wise, mysterious
**Secrets**: Has dark past, knows more than tells
**Goals**: Prepare heroes for greater threat
**Death**: Often dies to motivate heroes

**Examples**:

- Retired adventurer training new generation
- Wizard with forbidden knowledge
- Priest protecting ancient secret

### The Ally

**Role**: Helper, companion, resource
**Traits**: Loyal, capable, supportive
**Secrets**: May have hidden agenda
**Goals**: Support party mission (usually)
**Arc**: Proves loyalty or betrays at crucial moment

**Examples**:

- Skilled henchman for hire
- Local guide with insider knowledge
- Noble patron funding mission

### The Rival

**Role**: Competition, foil, occasional enemy
**Traits**: Skilled, proud, ambitious
**Secrets**: Respects party despite rivalry
**Goals**: Same objective as party, different methods
**Arc**: Becomes ally or enemy based on party actions

**Examples**:

- Rival adventuring party
- Competing merchant or thief
- Suitor for same NPC's affection

### The Villain

**Role**: Antagonist, obstacle, threat
**Traits**: Powerful, driven, cruel (or believes they're good)
**Secrets**: Weakness or vulnerable point
**Goals**: Conquer, destroy, or achieve twisted dream
**Arc**: Defeated, redeemed, or escapes for later

**Examples**:

- Dark lord seeking world domination
- Corrupt official abusing power
- Zealot convinced they're righteous

### The Innocent

**Role**: Victim, motivation, moral compass
**Traits**: Pure, vulnerable, trusting
**Secrets**: May be more than they seem
**Goals**: Survive, find safety, return home
**Arc**: Corrupted, saved, or tragically dies

**Examples**:

- Child lost in wilderness
- Peasant oppressed by tyrant
- Prisoner in villain's dungeon

### The Trickster

**Role**: Wild card, comic relief, chaos agent
**Traits**: Clever, unpredictable, amoral
**Secrets**: True motives unclear
**Goals**: Entertainment, profit, or hidden agenda
**Arc**: Helps or hinders based on whim

**Examples**:

- Fey creature playing games
- Con artist with heart of gold
- Prankster wizard testing party

## Personality Traits (Pick 2-3)

### Positive Traits

- **Brave**: Faces danger without hesitation
- **Compassionate**: Cares deeply about others' suffering
- **Honest**: Always tells truth, even when difficult
- **Loyal**: Stands by allies no matter what
- **Wise**: Offers sage advice and perspective
- **Optimistic**: Sees best in people and situations
- **Humble**: Doesn't boast, downplays achievements
- **Patient**: Willing to wait and work slowly
- **Generous**: Gives freely to those in need
- **Clever**: Solves problems with wit and creativity

### Negative Traits

- **Cowardly**: Avoids danger, abandons others
- **Cruel**: Enjoys others' suffering
- **Deceitful**: Lies habitually, hides truth
- **Selfish**: Puts own needs first always
- **Foolish**: Makes poor decisions, ignores advice
- **Pessimistic**: Expects worst, sees flaws in plans
- **Arrogant**: Boasts constantly, dismisses others
- **Impatient**: Rushes into action, can't wait
- **Greedy**: Hoards wealth and resources
- **Dull**: Lacks creativity, follows orders blindly

### Complex Traits

- **Suspicious**: Trusts no one, sees conspiracies everywhere
- **Ambitious**: Driven to succeed, may cut corners
- **Curious**: Must know everything, asks too many questions
- **Stubborn**: Won't change mind once decided
- **Idealistic**: Believes in causes, disappointed by reality
- **Cynical**: Expects betrayal and selfishness
- **Jealous**: Envies others' success and possessions
- **Protective**: Guards loved ones fiercely, sometimes too much

## Motivations

### Primary Desires

- **Power**: Control over others, political influence, magical strength
- **Wealth**: Gold, treasure, valuable items
- **Knowledge**: Secrets, lore, forbidden information
- **Love**: Romance, family, acceptance
- **Revenge**: Payback for past wrongs
- **Survival**: Stay alive, protect self and family
- **Fame**: Glory, recognition, legacy
- **Redemption**: Atone for past sins
- **Freedom**: Escape servitude, tyranny, or fate
- **Duty**: Fulfill obligation or destiny

### Fears

- **Death**: Own mortality or loved ones dying
- **Failure**: Not achieving goals or letting others down
- **Abandonment**: Being left alone or forgotten
- **Powerlessness**: Losing control of situation
- **Exposure**: Secrets revealed, reputation ruined
- **Change**: Loss of familiar and comfortable
- **The Unknown**: Mysterious threats and unexplained phenomena

## NPC Relationships to Party

### Attitude Ladder

1. **Hostile**: Actively working against party
2. **Unfriendly**: Distrustful, unhelpful
3. **Neutral**: Indifferent, business-like
4. **Friendly**: Helpful, welcoming
5. **Devoted**: Would sacrifice for party

### Shifting Attitudes

Party actions can move NPC up or down ladder:

- **Help NPC**: Move up 1 step
- **Betray or harm NPC**: Move down 2 steps
- **Save NPC's life**: Move up 2 steps
- **Threaten NPC**: Move down 1 step

## NPC Social Classes

### Peasantry

**Examples**: Farmers, laborers, servants
**Speech**: Simple, direct, local dialect
**Concerns**: Food, safety, taxes
**Interaction**: Deferential to nobility, wary of outsiders

### Merchants

**Examples**: Shopkeepers, traders, craftspeople
**Speech**: Practical, business-focused
**Concerns**: Profit, reputation, trade routes
**Interaction**: Friendly if profitable, shrewd negotiators

### Nobility

**Examples**: Lords, ladies, knights
**Speech**: Formal, educated, entitled
**Concerns**: Honor, lineage, political power
**Interaction**: Expect deference, value proper etiquette

### Clergy

**Examples**: Priests, monks, healers
**Speech**: Pious, philosophical
**Concerns**: Faith, souls, divine will
**Interaction**: Judgmental or compassionate depending on deity

### Scholars

**Examples**: Wizards, sages, librarians
**Speech**: Verbose, technical, condescending
**Concerns**: Knowledge, research, discoveries
**Interaction**: Helpful if party has information to trade

### Criminals

**Examples**: Thieves, smugglers, assassins
**Speech**: Coded, cautious, street smart
**Concerns**: Profit, avoiding law, reputation in underworld
**Interaction**: Transactional, untrustworthy but reliable if paid

## NPC Depth Layers

### Layer 1: Surface (First Impression)

What party sees immediately:

- Appearance
- Obvious emotion
- Initial statement

**Example**: Nervous guard at gate

### Layer 2: Presentation (Social Mask)

What NPC wants party to see:

- Professional role
- Public persona
- Stated goals

**Example**: Guard claims to be loyal to lord, doing his duty

### Layer 3: Truth (Real Motivation)

What NPC actually wants:

- Hidden agenda
- True feelings
- Secret goal

**Example**: Guard is paid by thieves guild, lets criminals through for bribe

## Random NPC Generator

Roll 1d6 for each category:

### Profession

1. **Craftsperson**: Blacksmith, carpenter, tailor
2. **Service**: Innkeeper, barmaid, stablehand
3. **Trade**: Merchant, peddler, moneylender
4. **Authority**: Guard, official, magistrate
5. **Knowledge**: Sage, priest, librarian
6. **Underworld**: Thief, smuggler, beggar

### Distinctive Feature

1. **Physical**: Scar, limp, unusual hair color
2. **Clothing**: Gaudy jewelry, tattered robes, armor
3. **Voice**: Accent, lisp, booming or whisper
4. **Possession**: Pet, weapon, strange artifact
5. **Smell**: Perfume, smoke, chemicals
6. **Behavior**: Nervous, overly friendly, suspicious

### Secret

1. **Criminal past**: Former thief, murderer, or fraud
2. **Hidden identity**: Spy, noble in disguise, lycanthrope
3. **Forbidden love**: Affair, secret family, unrequited
4. **Debt**: Owes money, made deal with devil, blackmailed
5. **Quest**: Searching for something, revenge mission
6. **Knowledge**: Knows crucial secret, witnessed crime

### Quirk

1. **Collector**: Obsessed with specific item type
2. **Superstitious**: Avoids specific numbers, colors, actions
3. **Addicted**: Gambling, alcohol, rare substance
4. **Phobia**: Heights, water, confined spaces
5. **Compulsive**: Must count, organize, clean
6. **Mnemonic**: Terrible memory, remembers everything, confabulates

## NPC Decision-Making

When NPC must choose action:

1. **What does NPC want?** (motivation)
2. **What does NPC fear?** (deterrent)
3. **What does NPC know?** (information)
4. **What can NPC do?** (capability)

Answer these = NPC's logical choice

## Memorable NPC Checklist

- [ ] Distinctive voice or speech pattern
- [ ] Visual feature party can remember
- [ ] Clear motivation
- [ ] At least one secret
- [ ] Connection to larger world or plot
- [ ] Emotional hook (party cares about outcome)
- [ ] Potential for multiple interactions (not one-off)

## NPC Party Dynamics

### The Questgiver

- Provides missions and rewards
- May have ulterior motives
- Trust increases with completed quests

### The Mentor

- Teaches skills or lore
- Unavailable when most needed (kidnapped, dead, busy)
- Returns for emotional climax

### The Rival

- Competes for same goals
- May become ally or enemy
- Reflects party's choices (what they could have been)

### The Innocent

- Needs protection
- Motivates heroic actions
- Death/corruption = major emotional moment

### The Betrayer

- Trusted ally who turns
- Foreshadowing in early interactions
- Reveal hits hard emotionally

### The Redeemed

- Former enemy seeks redemption
- Party must decide to trust
- Sacrifice cements redemption arc
