# D&D 5e Conditions - Complete Reference

## Overview

Conditions alter a creature's capabilities in a variety of ways. Each condition has specific mechanical effects that override normal rules. Some conditions are beneficial (like invisible), while most are detrimental.

**Important**: Multiple instances of the same condition don't stack—you're either affected or you're not. However, different conditions can apply simultaneously.

## All Conditions Alphabetically

### Blinded

A blinded creature can't see and automatically fails any ability check that requires sight.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- Attack rolls have **disadvantage**
- Attack rolls against the creature have **advantage**
- Automatically fails ability checks requiring sight (Perception checks to see, Investigation to read, etc.)

**Common Sources**:

- Darkness spell
- Blindness/Deafness spell
- Blinding gas or fog
- Creature abilities (troglodyte stench)
- Being in magical darkness without darkvision

**Duration**: Varies by source (1 minute for spells, permanent for some curses)

**Strategic Use**:

- Ranged attackers suffer greatly
- Melee attackers less affected but still hindered
- Forces reliance on hearing and touch
- Can combine with Silence for devastating effect

---

### Charmed

A charmed creature can't attack the charmer or target the charmer with harmful abilities or magical effects.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- Can't attack or harm the charmer
- Charmer has **advantage** on social interaction checks (Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation)
- No other combat penalties (can attack charmer's allies)

**Common Sources**:

- Charm Person spell
- Vampire's Charm ability
- Dryad's Fey Charm
- Succubus/Incubus abilities
- Friends spell

**Duration**: Usually concentration or until harmed by charmer/allies

**Strategic Use**:

- Turns one enemy neutral (won't attack you)
- Can give commands they'll follow if reasonable
- Breaks if charmer or allies attack them
- Social advantage for negotiation

**Important Limits**:

- Charmed creature still recognizes friends vs foes
- Won't do obviously suicidal actions
- Effects end after duration, may remember being charmed

---

### Deafened

A deafened creature can't hear and automatically fails any ability check that requires hearing.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- Can't hear anything
- Automatically fails ability checks requiring hearing (Perception checks to hear, Performance with instruments)
- **No combat penalties** (this condition has minimal mechanical impact)

**Common Sources**:

- Blindness/Deafness spell
- Thunderwave or thunder damage spells
- Proximity to very loud noises

**Duration**: Varies (1 minute for spells, longer for permanent damage)

**Strategic Use**:

- Limited combat impact
- Prevents hearing warnings or commands
- Can't detect invisible/hidden creatures by sound
- May not hear verbal spell components (DM discretion)

---

### Exhaustion

Exhaustion is measured in six levels. Each level adds cumulative penalties.

**Level 1**: Disadvantage on ability checks
**Level 2**: Speed halved (in addition to level 1)
**Level 3**: Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws (in addition to levels 1-2)
**Level 4**: Hit point maximum halved (in addition to levels 1-3)
**Level 5**: Speed reduced to 0 (in addition to levels 1-4)
**Level 6**: **Death**

**Common Sources**:

- No food (3+ days): 1 level per additional day
- No water (1+ days): 1 level per day (2 in hot weather)
- Barbarian Frenzy: 1 level after rage ends
- Extreme cold/heat: 1 level per failed Constitution save
- Forced march: 1 level after traveling beyond normal pace for hours
- Sickening Radiance spell: 1 level per failed save

**Recovery**:

- Long rest removes 1 level (if you ate and drank adequately)
- Greater Restoration removes 1 level
- Some magic items prevent exhaustion

**Strategic Use**:

- Devastating at higher levels
- Plan resource management (food, water, rest)
- Long-term consequences of pushing too hard

### Frightened

A frightened creature has disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of its fear is within line of sight.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- **Disadvantage** on ability checks while source visible
- **Disadvantage** on attack rolls while source visible
- **Can't willingly move closer** to the source of fear
- Can still move away or perpendicular
- Condition ends if source out of sight (can break line of sight)

**Common Sources**:

- Dragon's Frightful Presence
- Fear spell
- Cause Fear spell
- Wraith's Life Drain
- Specter's ability

**Duration**: Usually 1 minute or until source no longer visible

**Strategic Use**:

- Forces enemies to retreat
- Combos well with enclosed spaces (can't escape)
- Ranged attackers still hit hard despite disadvantage
- Break line of sight to end early

---

### Grappled

A grappled creature's speed becomes 0, and it can't benefit from any bonus to its speed.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- Speed becomes **0** (can't move)
- Can't benefit from speed bonuses
- No other penalties (can still attack, cast spells normally)
- **Ends if grappler is incapacitated** or effect removes you from reach

**How to Grapple**:

1. Use Attack action (replaces one attack)
2. Make contested check: Athletics vs target's Athletics or Acrobatics
3. Success = grappled
4. Requires free hand
5. Can only grapple creatures up to one size larger

**Escaping a Grapple**:

- Use action to make Athletics or Acrobatics check vs grappler's Athletics
- Or: Teleport/become incorporeal
- Or: Shove grappler away

**Strategic Use**:

- Lock down mobile enemies
- Combo with Shove (knock prone while grappled = devastating)
- Drag grappled creatures into hazards
- Prevent fleeing or repositioning

---

### Incapacitated

An incapacitated creature can't take actions or reactions.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- **Can't take actions** (includes Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, etc.)
- **Can't take reactions** (no opportunity attacks, Counterspell, Shield, etc.)
- Can still move (unless another condition prevents it)
- Concentration automatically ends if spellcaster

**Common Sources**:

- Stunned condition (includes incapacitated)
- Unconscious condition (includes incapacitated)
- Hypnotic Pattern spell
- Some poisons

**Duration**: Varies by source

**Strategic Use**:

- Completely removes threat from combat (can't attack or cast)
- Victim can still be moved by allies
- Combine with grapple to prevent movement

---

### Invisible

An invisible creature is impossible to see without the aid of magic or a special sense.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- Considered **heavily obscured** for purpose of hiding
- Attack rolls against the creature have **disadvantage**
- The creature's attack rolls have **advantage**
- Can still be detected by sound, smell, tracks

**Common Sources**:

- Invisibility spell
- Greater Invisibility spell
- Potion of Invisibility
- Creature abilities (imp, sprite)

**Duration**: Varies (1 hour for Invisibility, 1 minute for Greater Invisibility)

**Breaking Invisibility**:

- Invisibility spell ends if you attack or cast spell
- Greater Invisibility doesn't end on attack
- Physical attacks still reveal location via sound/impact

**Detection Methods**:

- Hearing (Perception checks to locate)
- See Invisibility spell
- Truesight
- Faerie Fire (reveals invisible creatures)
- Blindsight (detects without seeing)

**Strategic Use**:

- Massive advantage in combat (advantage on attacks, disadvantage to hit you)
- Perfect for scouting and stealth
- Combine with silence for ultimate stealth

---

### Paralyzed

A paralyzed creature is incapacitated and can't move or speak.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- **Incapacitated** (can't take actions or reactions)
- **Can't move or speak**
- Automatically fails **Strength** and **Dexterity** saving throws
- Attack rolls against creature have **advantage**
- Attacks from within 5 feet are **automatic critical hits**

**Common Sources**:

- Hold Person spell
- Ghoul's claws
- Carrion Crawler's tentacles
- Gelatinous Cube
- Some poisons

**Duration**: Varies (1 minute for spells, less for creature abilities)

**Strategic Use**:

- Devastating debuff (auto-crit from melee = huge damage)
- Combine with Rogue's Sneak Attack for massive damage
- Victim helpless, can be coup de graced
- Ends concentration on spells

---

### Petrified

A petrified creature is transformed, along with any nonmagical objects it's wearing or carrying, into solid stone.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- Weight increases by factor of ten
- **Incapacitated**, can't move or speak, unaware of surroundings
- Automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saves
- Attack rolls against creature have **advantage**
- **Resistance to all damage**
- Immune to **poison** and **disease** (existing poison/disease suspended)

**Common Sources**:

- Basilisk's gaze
- Medusa's gaze
- Flesh to Stone spell
- Cockatrice's bite

**Duration**: Usually permanent until Greater Restoration or similar magic

**Strategic Use**:

- Effectively removes creature from combat permanently
- Resistance to all damage makes statue hard to destroy
- Creatures can be shattered if damaged enough while petrified
- Moral quandary: Is destroying statue murder?

---

### Poisoned

A poisoned creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- **Disadvantage on attack rolls**
- **Disadvantage on ability checks**
- No effect on saving throws (common misconception)
- Stacks with other conditions

**Common Sources**:

- Poison damage effects (giant spider bite)
- Poisoned weapons
- Poisoned food/drink
- Ray of Sickness spell
- Cloudkill spell
- Stinking Cloud spell

**Duration**: Varies (1 minute to 24 hours depending on source)

**Immunity**:

- Dwarves have advantage on saves vs poison
- Monks (10th level) immune
- Many undead and constructs immune
- Protection from Poison spell grants immunity

**Strategic Use**:

- Broad debuff affecting combat and skills
- Common condition (many monsters inflict it)
- Often comes with poison damage as well
- Lesser Restoration or antitoxin can cure

---

### Prone

A prone creature's only movement option is to crawl (costs extra movement) or stand up (costs half movement).

**Mechanical Effects**:

- **Disadvantage on attack rolls**
- Attack rolls against creature have **advantage** if attacker within 5 feet
- Attack rolls against creature have **disadvantage** if attacker farther than 5 feet
- Standing up costs half movement speed

**Common Sources**:

- Shove attack (replaces one melee attack)
- Grease spell
- Thunderwave or other force effects
- Slippery surfaces (ice, oil)

**Duration**: Until creature stands up (costs half movement)

**Strategic Use**:

- Combo with grapple (can't stand while grappled!)
- Ranged enemies at disadvantage to hit
- Melee enemies at advantage (easy kills)
- Crawling costs extra movement (difficult terrain)

---

### Restrained

A restrained creature's speed becomes 0, and it can't benefit from any bonus to its speed.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- Speed becomes **0**, can't benefit from speed bonuses
- Attack rolls have **disadvantage**
- Attack rolls against creature have **advantage**
- **Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws** (Fireball, Lightning Bolt hurt more!)

**Common Sources**:

- Entangle spell
- Web spell
- Net weapon
- Grappler feat (restrain while grappling)
- Gelatinous Cube's Engulf
- Restrained by chains or rope

**Duration**: Varies (spell duration or until escape)

**Escaping Restraints**:

- Action to escape (Athletics or Acrobatics vs DC)
- Break restraints (attack or Strength check)
- Allies can help free you

**Strategic Use**:

- More severe than grappled (includes attack disadvantage and Dex save penalty)
- Combos with Dex-save spells (Fireball, Lightning Bolt)
- Prevents fleeing completely

---

### Stunned

A stunned creature is incapacitated, can't move, and can speak only falteringly.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- **Incapacitated** (can't take actions or reactions)
- **Can't move**
- Can speak only falteringly (DM discretion on verbal spell components)
- Automatically fails **Strength** and **Dexterity** saving throws
- Attack rolls against creature have **advantage**

**Common Sources**:

- Monk's Stunning Strike
- Stunning Fist ability
- Some legendary creature abilities
- Power Word Stun spell

**Duration**: Usually short (until end of attacker's next turn)

**Strategic Use**:

- Removes creature from combat for 1 round
- Auto-fail Str/Dex saves (Fireball, Lightning Bolt guaranteed damage)
- Brief but powerful debuff

---

### Unconscious

An unconscious creature is incapacitated, can't move or speak, and is unaware of its surroundings.

**Mechanical Effects**:

- **Incapacitated**, can't move or speak
- **Unaware** of surroundings
- Drops whatever it's holding and **falls prone**
- Automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saves
- Attack rolls against creature have **advantage**
- Attacks from within 5 feet are **automatic critical hits** (death save territory!)

**Common Sources**:

- Dropping to 0 hit points
- Sleep spell
- Knocked out (nonlethal damage)
- Some poisons or toxins

**Duration**: Until regains hit points or wakes up

**Waking Up**:

- Take damage (if stable at 0 HP, become conscious at 1 HP if healed)
- Allies use action to wake (DC 10 Medicine check or slap awake)
- 1d4 hours natural sleep

**Strategic Use**:

- Most vulnerable state (auto-crits from melee)
- Creatures at 0 HP and unconscious need healing fast
- Easy coup de grace opportunity

## Condition Interactions

### Advantage/Disadvantage Stacking

Remember: Multiple sources of advantage/disadvantage don't stack—you either have it or you don't.

**Example**: Invisible (advantage on attacks) + Prone target within 5 feet (advantage) = Still just advantage, not double advantage.

### Common Combos

**Grappled + Prone** = Can't stand up (movement 0), melee advantage against them, disadvantage on attacks
**Restrained + Fireball** = Disadvantage on Dex save means more damage
**Paralyzed + Rogue** = Auto-crit from within 5 feet = massive Sneak Attack damage
**Invisible + Hidden** = Attack with advantage, they attack with disadvantage, and they don't know where you are

## DM Tips for Conditions

1. **Track Clearly**: Use tokens, markers, or notes to show which creatures have which conditions
2. **Describe Vividly**: "You're paralyzed" vs "Your muscles lock up, refusing to obey your commands"
3. **Remind Players**: "You're frightened, so you have disadvantage on that attack"
4. **Duration Tracking**: Note when conditions end (end of caster's turn, save each round, etc.)
5. **Strategic Enemies**: Smart enemies target restrained/paralyzed creatures for easy kills
6. **Player Agency**: Let players be creative in causing or removing conditions
