# D&D Scene Description Guide for DMs

## The Five Senses Approach

### Vision (Primary Sense)

**Light and Shadow**:

- Torchlight flickers on damp stone walls
- Shafts of sunlight pierce through canopy
- Bioluminescent fungi cast eerie blue glow
- Darkness so complete even darkvision struggles

**Color and Texture**:

- Moss-covered ancient stones, vibrant green
- Rusted iron gates, flaking orange-brown
- Polished marble floors, veined with gold
- Rough-hewn cave walls, jagged and gray

**Movement and Details**:

- Dust motes dance in sunbeams
- Banners sway in unfelt breeze
- Water drips rhythmically from stalactites
- Shadows shift as if alive

### Sound (Atmosphere Builder)

**Ambient Sounds**:

- Dripping water echoes in cavern
- Wind whistles through broken windows
- Distant thunder rumbles overhead
- Chains rattle in unseen chambers

**Action Sounds**:

- Footsteps crunch on gravel
- Armor clinks with each step
- Pages rustle as you turn them
- Bones crack underfoot

**Living Sounds**:

- Birds chirp in forest canopy
- Rats scurry in walls
- Breathing of large creature ahead
- Whispers in unknown language

### Smell (Emotional Trigger)

**Pleasant**:

- Fresh bread from nearby bakery
- Wildflowers in meadow
- Clean mountain air
- Incense in temple

**Unpleasant**:

- Rot and decay of corpses
- Sulfur from volcanic vents
- Mold and mildew in damp dungeon
- Unwashed bodies in crowded tavern

**Neutral/Informative**:

- Salt air from nearby ocean
- Smoke from campfires
- Leather and oil (armory)
- Herbs and potions (alchemist)

### Touch (Immediate Connection)

**Texture**:

- Smooth, cold marble under fingers
- Rough, warm bark of ancient tree
- Slimy walls of sewer passage
- Soft, dusty velvet curtains

**Temperature**:

- Icy wind bites exposed skin
- Humid heat makes clothes cling
- Cool mist settles on face
- Radiating warmth from forge

**Physical Sensation**:

- Vibration through floor (approaching footsteps)
- Sticky cobwebs brush face
- Sharp pain from thorn bush
- Gentle breeze ruffles hair

### Taste (Rarely Used but Powerful)

**Environmental**:

- Metallic tang of blood in air
- Bitter dust on lips
- Salt spray from ocean
- Sweet smell of rot (taste and smell connected)

**Deliberate**:

- Rich stew at tavern
- Bitter potion burns throat
- Sweet healing potion
- Stale water from dungeon fountain

## Pacing Your Descriptions

### Opening a Scene (20-30 seconds)

**Full Sensory Description**:

```
"You push open the heavy oak doors. The great hall stretches before you,
sixty feet to the far wall where a throne sits empty. Shafts of colored
light stream through stained glass windows, painting the dusty floor in
crimson and gold. The air smells of old stone and decay. Your footsteps
echo in the silence, and you notice massive tapestries hanging from the
walls, their colors faded by time. At the far end, behind the throne,
you glimpse a doorway leading into darkness."
```

**Key Elements**:

- Size/scale of space
- 2-3 visual details
- 1 other sense (smell, sound)
- Point of interest (doorway, throne)
- Atmosphere (silent, echoing)

### During Action (5-10 seconds)

**Quick, Focused Description**:

```
"The goblin raises a rusty shortsword, its jagged teeth bared in a snarl."
```

**Only Essential Details**:

- What's attacking/acting
- Immediate threat
- One memorable detail

### Investigating (10-15 seconds)

**Reward Curiosity**:

```
"You examine the bookshelf more closely. Most books are rotted beyond reading,
but one leather-bound tome seems intact. Its cover bears a symbol you recognize
from your studies—the mark of the necromancer Thraxis."
```

**Key Elements**:

- What they asked about
- New information revealed
- Plot hook or clue
- Optional: Require check for more

## Setting Tone and Mood

### Horror/Dread

**Focus On**:

- What you _don't_ see (shadows, darkness)
- Unnatural silence or sounds
- Decay and corruption
- Isolation and vulnerability

**Example**:
"The corridor stretches ahead, torchlight dying after thirty feet. You hear
nothing—not even your own breathing echoes back. The air is thick, heavy,
like it resists your passage. Deep scratches mar the stone walls, four parallel
gouges that descend into the darkness ahead."

### Wonder/Mystery

**Focus On**:

- Beauty and unusual elements
- Magical phenomena
- Ancient or alien architecture
- Hidden secrets and puzzles

**Example**:
"The chamber defies geometry. The ceiling seems impossibly distant, lost in
swirling mist that glows with its own light. Floating platforms drift in
the air, connected by bridges of solidified light. At the center, a crystal
obelisk rotates slowly, humming with power."

### Action/Urgency

**Focus On**:

- Movement and danger
- Clear tactical information
- Time pressure
- Immediate threats

**Example**:
"The ceiling cracks. Chunks of stone rain down around you. Twenty feet ahead,
the exit door is closing—it's at ten feet now, then five. The floor tilts,
sending you sliding toward the pit that just opened behind you."

### Comfort/Safety

**Focus On**:

- Warmth and light
- Friendly faces
- Food and rest
- Absence of danger

**Example**:
"The inn's common room is exactly what you need—a roaring fire, the smell of
fresh bread and roasted meat, and the friendly chatter of other travelers.
The halfling innkeeper waves you toward a table, already bringing mugs of ale."

## Show, Don't Tell

### Bad (Telling)

"The room is scary."
"The NPC is suspicious."
"The monster is dangerous."

### Good (Showing)

"Your torch reveals three skeletons slumped against the wall, their empty
eye sockets seeming to watch your every move."

"The merchant's eyes dart to the door, his hand moving unconsciously toward
the dagger at his belt. 'I don't know anything,' he says, too quickly."

"The dragon's claw is the size of your torso. A single tooth is as long as
your forearm. It hasn't noticed you yet, but when it shifts in sleep, the
ground trembles."

## Environmental Storytelling

### Use Details to Reveal History

**Bad**: "This room was a bedroom."

**Good**: "A four-poster bed sits against the far wall, its canopy rotted
to tatters. A child's wooden toy lies on the floor—a horse, one leg broken
off. The single window looks out over what might once have been gardens,
now overgrown with thorns."

**Revealed**:

- Child lived here
- Long abandoned
- Once wealthy (four-poster bed)
- Nature reclaiming space

### Foreshadowing Through Description

**Current Scene**:
"The altar is pristine, recently cleaned. Fresh wax drips from black candles.
Someone has been here, and recently."

**Payoff Later**:
Party realizes cultists are still active, not ancient history.

## Combat Descriptions

### Opening Round (Set the Scene)

```
"The orcs spot you. The largest—eight feet of muscle and scarred green flesh—
roars a challenge. His companions fan out, axes raised. The narrow canyon means
they can't surround you, but there's nowhere to retreat. Twenty feet separate
you. Initiative!"
```

**Include**:

- Enemy numbers and types
- Terrain advantages/disadvantages
- Distance
- Immediate tactical situation

### During Combat (Quick and Clear)

```
"Your sword bites deep into the orc's shoulder. He staggers back, black blood
spraying, but stays standing—barely."
```

**Include**:

- Whether hit/miss
- Damage severity (bloodied, barely hurt)
- Enemy reaction
- Keep it brief (5 seconds)

### Killing Blows (Make Them Memorable)

```
"Your arrow takes the dragon in the eye. It rears back, roaring in agony,
then crashes to the ground. The earth shakes with the impact. For a moment,
nothing moves. Then slowly, the massive chest stops rising. Silence."
```

**Include**:

- Dramatic impact
- Environmental reaction
- Moment of uncertainty
- Confirmation of death

## NPC Dialogue and Description

### Memorable NPCs Need Physical Details

**Bad**: "The guard asks your business."

**Good**: "A one-eyed guard leans on his spear, bored expression on his
scarred face. 'State your business,' he drawls, barely looking at you."

**Elements**:

- One striking physical detail (one eye)
- Body language (leaning, bored)
- Voice/manner (drawling)
- Emotional state (barely interested)

### Dialogue Tags That Show Emotion

**Neutral**: Said, asked, replied
**Emotional**:

- Whispered, hissed, growled (anger/threat)
- Stammered, muttered, mumbled (fear/uncertainty)
- Shouted, bellowed, roared (anger/command)
- Purred, cooed, breathed (seduction/intimacy)

### Action During Dialogue

```
"'I didn't see nothing,' the thief says, eyes darting to the window.
His hand creeps toward his belt knife. 'Best you leave, friend. Before
there's trouble.'"
```

**Shows**:

- Planning to flee (window)
- Ready for violence (knife)
- Lying (nervous behavior)
- Threatening (implied threat)

## Revealing Information Gradually

### Layer Your Descriptions

**First Glance** (Free, automatic):
"You enter a library. Bookshelves line the walls, floor to ceiling."

**Casual Investigation** (No check, just looking):
"Most books are about arcane theory. One shelf holds books about dragons
specifically."

**Active Search** (DC 12 Investigation):
"Behind a false book spine, you find a hidden compartment containing a journal."

**Deep Study** (DC 15 Investigation, takes time):
"The journal reveals the owner was researching a ritual to control dragons.
The last entry, dated three months ago, mentions 'the final component' with
coordinates to a mountain cave."

## Describing Magic

### Make It Visual and Unique

**Generic**: "He casts Fireball."

**Specific**: "The wizard traces a symbol of flame in the air. It hangs there,
glowing brighter, then streaks toward the orcs like a shooting star. The explosion
is deafening, a sphere of fire that consumes everything in twenty feet."

### Different Casters, Different Aesthetics

**Wizard**: Precise, scholarly, glowing runes
**Sorcerer**: Innate, explosive, wild energy
**Warlock**: Dark, unsettling, otherworldly
**Cleric**: Divine light, sacred symbols, prayers
**Druid**: Natural, growing, transforming

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Too Much Detail**: Descriptions over 30 seconds lose attention
2. **No Sensory Variety**: All vision, no smell/sound/touch
3. **Passive Voice**: "The door is opened" vs "You open the door"
4. **No Clear Information**: Pretty words but players don't know what's there
5. **Repetitive Phrases**: Same descriptions every room
6. **Ignoring Player Actions**: They searched specifically, give specific results
7. **Info-Dumping**: Massive paragraphs without player interaction

## Quick Reference: Description Template

1. **Size/Scale**: How big is this place?
2. **Light**: How well can they see?
3. **Notable Features**: What stands out? (2-3 things)
4. **Other Sense**: One smell, sound, or tactile detail
5. **Mood**: What emotion should players feel?
6. **Exits/Threats**: Where can they go? What's dangerous?

**Example**:
"A vast chamber [size], lit by phosphorescent moss [light]. Broken statues
of forgotten gods line the walls, their faces chipped away [feature]. The air
smells of damp earth and something sweet—rot [smell]. This place feels wrong,
sacred but violated [mood]. Three exits: the way you came, a crumbling staircase
leading up, and a dark tunnel descending into earth [exits]."
