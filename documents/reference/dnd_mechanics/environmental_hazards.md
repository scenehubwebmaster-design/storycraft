# D&D Environmental Hazards and Traps

## Traps

### Simple Traps (CR 1-4)

#### Pit Trap

**Trigger**: Step on false floor
**Effect**: DC 10 Dexterity save or fall 10 feet (1d6 bludgeoning)
**Detection**: DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) or Investigation
**Disable**: DC 10 Thieves' Tools check

#### Dart Trap

**Trigger**: Trip wire or pressure plate
**Effect**: +8 to hit, 1d4 piercing damage
**Detection**: DC 12 Perception
**Disable**: DC 12 Thieves' Tools

#### Falling Net

**Trigger**: Pull hidden cord
**Effect**: DC 12 Dexterity save or restrained
**Detection**: DC 10 Perception
**Disable**: DC 10 Thieves' Tools or Strength check to break net

#### Poison Needle

**Trigger**: Opening lock without disabling trap
**Effect**: DC 12 Constitution save or 1d4 poison damage + poisoned 1 hour
**Detection**: DC 15 Perception
**Disable**: DC 15 Thieves' Tools

#### Swinging Blade

**Trigger**: Step on pressure plate
**Effect**: +10 to hit, 2d6 slashing damage
**Detection**: DC 12 Perception
**Disable**: DC 15 Thieves' Tools or jam mechanism

### Moderate Traps (CR 5-10)

#### Spiked Pit

**Trigger**: False floor collapses
**Effect**: DC 13 Dex save or fall 20 feet (2d6 bludgeoning) onto spikes (2d6 piercing)
**Detection**: DC 13 Perception
**Disable**: DC 15 Thieves' Tools

#### Fire Jet

**Trigger**: Pull lever or step on plate
**Effect**: DC 13 Dex save or 3d6 fire damage (half on success)
**Detection**: DC 12 Perception (scorch marks)
**Disable**: DC 15 Thieves' Tools

#### Lightning Arc

**Trigger**: Touch metal object
**Effect**: DC 15 Dex save or 4d6 lightning damage (half on success)
**Detection**: DC 14 Arcana (magical energy)
**Disable**: DC 18 Thieves' Tools or Dispel Magic

#### Poison Gas

**Trigger**: Open chest or door
**Effect**: 10-foot cube, DC 14 Con save or 4d6 poison + poisoned 1 hour
**Detection**: DC 13 Perception (smell)
**Disable**: DC 16 Thieves' Tools or hold breath

#### Rolling Sphere

**Trigger**: Remove idol from pedestal
**Effect**: DC 15 Dex save or 4d10 bludgeoning + knocked prone
**Detection**: DC 13 Perception (Indiana Jones style)
**Disable**: Can't disable, must dodge or outrun

### Complex Traps (CR 11+)

#### Crushing Walls

**Trigger**: Enter room
**Effect**: Walls close 10 feet per round, DC 20 Athletics to hold open or escape
**Detection**: DC 15 Investigation (pressure plates)
**Disable**: DC 20 Thieves' Tools on mechanism

#### Disintegration Ray

**Trigger**: Cross threshold
**Effect**: +12 to hit, 10d6 + 40 force damage
**Detection**: DC 17 Investigation (magical sensor)
**Disable**: DC 20 Thieves' Tools or Dispel Magic (DC 18)

#### Sphere of Annihilation

**Trigger**: Touch specific object
**Effect**: Anything that enters space is destroyed (no save)
**Detection**: DC 18 Arcana (void in reality)
**Disable**: Control talisman or Mage Hand to manipulate

#### Collapsing Ceiling

**Trigger**: Weight on floor exceeds limit
**Effect**: DC 15 Dex save or 6d10 bludgeoning + buried (restrained, no actions)
**Detection**: DC 14 Perception (cracks in ceiling)
**Disable**: DC 18 Thieves' Tools or support pillars

## Natural Hazards

### Falling

**Damage**: 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet fallen
**Maximum**: 20d6 (200 feet or more)
**Save**: None, but Feather Fall, Slow Fall (Monk), or similar can reduce
**Landing**: Prone unless Acrobatics check (DC 15)

**Special Cases**:

- **Water**: Half damage if water at least 10 feet deep and successful DC 15 Athletics check
- **Snow**: Half damage if deep snow (5+ feet)
- **Branches**: DC 10 Acrobatics to catch branch, stop fall
- **Falling Objects**: Same damage, creatures below make DC 15 Dex save

### Suffocation

**Holding Breath**: 1 + Constitution modifier minutes (minimum 30 seconds)
**After Breath Runs Out**:

- Drop to 0 hit points
- Can't regain hit points or be stabilized until can breathe again

**Drowning**: Same as suffocation, but underwater

### Extreme Cold

**Unprotected**: DC 10 Constitution save per hour
**Failure**: 1 level of exhaustion
**Protection**: Cold weather clothing or resistance to cold = advantage
**Extreme Cold** (below 0°F): DC 10 Con save every 10 minutes

**Icy Water**: 1 minute submerged = DC 10 Con save or gain 1 exhaustion

### Extreme Heat

**Temperature 100°F+**: DC 10 Constitution save per hour
**Failure**: 1 exhaustion level
**No Water**: Automatic failure
**Heavy Armor or Clothing**: Disadvantage on save

**Boiling Water/Lava**:

- **Boiling Water**: 1d6 fire damage per round
- **Lava**: 10d10 fire damage on contact, 1d10 within 10 feet

### Starvation

**Without Food**: 3 + Constitution modifier days (minimum 1)
**After**: 1 exhaustion level per day
**Half Rations**: Count as full day every 2 days

### Dehydration

**Without Water**: 1 day
**Hot Weather**: Half day
**After**: 1 exhaustion level per day (2 in hot weather)

### Quicksand

**Entering**: DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) to notice before stepping in
**Sinking**: 1d4 feet per round, restrained
**Escape**: DC 12 Strength (Athletics) to pull self out or ally pulls with rope
**Submerged**: Begin suffocating

### Avalanche

**Warning**: DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) to hear rumbling
**Escape**: DC 15 Dexterity save to dodge out of path
**Failure**: 4d10 bludgeoning + buried under snow
**Buried**: Restrained, blinded, can't breathe
**Escape**: DC 15 Athletics to dig self out or allies dig (10 feet per minute)

### Forest Fire

**Smoke Inhalation**: DC 12 Constitution save or 1d6 poison damage + choking
**Flames**: DC 12 Dexterity save or 3d6 fire damage
**Spread**: Fire spreads 30 feet per round

### Thunderstorm

**Lightning**: Tallest object in area

- **Metal Armor**: +4 bonus to be struck
- **Effect**: +10 to hit, 6d10 lightning damage (DC 15 Dex save half)
  **Heavy Rain**: Disadvantage on Perception (Wisdom), extinguishes flames
  **Wind**: Disadvantage on ranged attacks, difficult terrain

### Earthquake

**Each Round**:

- DC 15 Dexterity save or knocked prone
- Buildings may collapse (4d10 bludgeoning + buried)
- Fissures open (DC 12 Dex save or fall in)

### Acid

**Weak Acid** (vinegar): 1 damage
**Moderate Acid** (stomach acid): 2d6 damage
**Strong Acid** (black dragon breath): 4d6 damage
**Immersion**: Damage each round

## Magical Hazards

### Antimagic Field

**Effect**: Suppresses all magic within 10-foot radius
**Duration**: Permanent until dispelled or trigger removed
**Result**: Magical items cease functioning, spells don't work

### Unhallow

**Effect**: Area desecrated, undead have advantage, turning undead disadvantage
**Detection**: DC 15 Religion check
**Removal**: Hallow spell or destroy source

### Curse

**Trigger**: Touch cursed item, enter cursed area
**Effect**: Varies (disadvantage on saves, can't heal, transformation)
**Removal**: Remove Curse, Greater Restoration, or quest

### Planar Portal

**Trigger**: Enter area or speak command word
**Effect**: Transported to another plane
**Detection**: DC 15 Arcana check
**Control**: Command word or destroy portal anchor

### Symbol Spell

**Trigger**: Read, touch, or approach symbol
**Effect**: Varies (death, discord, fear, insanity, pain, sleep, stunning)
**Detection**: DC 15 Investigation or Arcana
**Disable**: Dispel Magic (DC 15) or rogue DC 15 Thieves' Tools

### Glyph of Warding

**Trigger**: Enter area or manipulate object
**Effect**: Stores spell, released on trigger
**Detection**: DC 15 Investigation
**Disable**: Dispel Magic (DC 15)

## Disease

### Common Diseases

#### Sewer Plague

**Transmission**: Contact with filth, rodents
**Save**: DC 11 Constitution after 1d4 days
**Effect**: 1 exhaustion level, regain only half hit points from rest
**Recovery**: Two consecutive successful saves (daily)

#### Sight Rot

**Transmission**: Swamp water, contaminated food
**Save**: DC 15 Constitution after 1d3 days
**Effect**: Disadvantage on Wisdom checks and saves, blindness after 30 days
**Recovery**: Magic only (Lesser Restoration)

#### Cackle Fever

**Transmission**: Inhale spores, contact with infected
**Save**: DC 10 Constitution immediately
**Effect**: Uncontrollable laughter, disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks
**Recovery**: DC 13 Constitution save after each long rest (3 successes cures)

#### Mummy Rot (Curse)

**Transmission**: Touch of mummy
**Save**: DC 12 Constitution
**Effect**: Can't regain hit points, max HP reduced by 10 (3d6) every 24 hours
**Recovery**: Remove Curse spell

## Trap Design Guidelines

### Trap Elements

1. **Level Appropriate**:

   - CR 1-4: 1d6 to 2d6 damage
   - CR 5-10: 3d6 to 5d6 damage
   - CR 11+: 6d6+ damage

2. **Detection and Disarming**:

   - Easy: DC 10-12
   - Moderate: DC 13-15
   - Hard: DC 16-18
   - Very Hard: DC 19+

3. **Multiple Solutions**:

   - Disarm with Thieves' Tools
   - Detect and avoid
   - Tank the damage
   - Use magic (Dispel, Detect Magic)
   - Environmental interaction (jam mechanism, break trigger)

4. **Consequences**:
   - Alert enemies
   - Slow party down
   - Drain resources (HP, spells)
   - Create interesting choices

### Trap Complications

Add these for memorable traps:

- **Reset**: Trap resets after 1 minute
- **Multiple Triggers**: Several ways to activate
- **Chain Reaction**: Triggers other traps
- **False Trigger**: Obvious trap hides real danger
- **Guarded**: Monster immune to trap guards area
- **Magical**: Can't be disarmed with mundane tools

## Environmental Storytelling

Use hazards to:

- Show dungeon's age (collapsed tunnels, rust)
- Reveal history (trap types indicate builders)
- Create tension (ticking clock, rising water)
- Encourage clever play (use environment against enemies)
- Reward exploration (side path avoids hazard)
