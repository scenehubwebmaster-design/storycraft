# Random Encounters

Random encounters deliver the unexpected: urgency, atmosphere, resource pressure, and occasional help.

<!-- chunk:start -->
## When to Check

- Long, uneventful journeys
- During short/long rests
- When the party draws attention
- To prod momentum when players stall
<!-- chunk:end -->

**Procedure:** Roll d20 at reasonable intervals (e.g., every 4–8 in-game hours). On **18+**, an encounter occurs; roll on a suitable table.

## Example: Sylvan Forest Encounters (1d12 + 1d8)

```table
| Roll | Encounter |
|------|-----------|
| 2 | 1 displacer beast |
| 3 | 1 gnoll pack lord and 2d4 gnolls |
| 4 | 1d4 gnolls and 2d4 hyenas |
| 5 | Grove of burned trees; tracks lead to gnolls |
| 6 | 1 giant owl |
| 7 | Ivy‑covered elven statue |
| 8 | 1 dryad (50%) or 1d4 satyrs (50%) |
| 9 | 1d4 centaurs |
| 10 | 2d4 elf scouts (horn may call allies) |
| 11 | 2d4 pixies (50%) or 2d4 sprites (50%) |
| 12 | 1 owlbear |
| 13 | 1d4 elk (75%) or 1 giant elk (25%) |
| 14 | 1d4 blink dogs |
| 15 | Glowing berry plant (temporary invisibility) |
| 16 | Elven tune on the breeze |
| 17 | 1d4 faerie dragons (orange 75%, blue 25%) |
| 18 | 1 elf druid (indifferent; friendly vs. gnolls) |
| 19 | 1 treant (stance depends on party) |
| 20 | 1 unicorn |
```
