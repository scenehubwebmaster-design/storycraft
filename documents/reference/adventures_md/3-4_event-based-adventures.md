# Event-Based Adventures

Event-based play centers on **what** people do and **when**, more than **where**.

<!-- chunk:start -->
## Build Around a Villain

Create a clear antagonist with motives and a **timeline** of actions (assuming no interference). Then anticipate reactions when heroes intervene.

## Event Patterns (Villain Actions)

```table
| d6 | Pattern | Summary |
|----|---------|---------|
| 1 | Big Event | Plans ripen at a fixed time (festival, eclipse) |
| 2 | Crime Spree | Acts escalate in boldness and horror |
| 3 | Growing Corruption | Influence spreads (armies, cults, plague) |
| 4 | One and Done | Single deed; villain evades consequences |
| 5 | Serial Crimes | Repeated acts follow a pattern |
| 6 | Step by Step | Specific sequence toward a goal |
```

## Mysteries

Define the **victim**, a **closed circle of suspects**, and **redundant clues** (verbal and physical). False leads are fair; dead ends are not.
<!-- chunk:end -->

<!-- chunk:start -->
## Intrigue

Track **influence** with NPCs/factions. Some scenarios have many villains—or none at all—focused on favors, prestige, and leverage.
<!-- chunk:end -->
