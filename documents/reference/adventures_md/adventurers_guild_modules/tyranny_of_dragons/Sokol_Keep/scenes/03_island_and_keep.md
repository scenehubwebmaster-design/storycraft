## Inhabitants
- **Harae** (LG spirit of Tyr) — wants **justice**, aids respectful parties.
- **Servants**: **Darvag**, **Shandra**, **Rorin**; scared but cooperative; provide **keys** to interior doors.

## Search Highlights
- **Igan’s Quarters**: book **Moonsea Cults** (Thorass), **curses book**, **holy symbol of Tyr**, **5 pp** (theft angers Harae).
- **Lighthouse**: magical beacon intact but **disabled**.
- **East Tower**: hidden **Tyr shrine** with **Igan’s journal** (final page = **pass phrase**).
- **West Tower**: broken floor; stone slab with handprint; pass phrase **“Toe Mono, Toe Megalo.”** Wrong words trigger **lightning stun**.
