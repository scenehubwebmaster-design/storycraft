## Areas & Hazards
- **Entrance**: muddy floor, severed rope, brass doors.
- **Crypt**: calcified bones (taking trinkets angers **Harae**).
- **Vestibule of Barnacles**: **animated armor** ambush.
- **Sinkhole**: algae-slick slope, **DC 13 Acrobatics** to cross; **ghouls** lurk; circular vent high wall.
- **Cells**: stuck doors (**DC 15 Str**).
- **Jade Idol** (boss room): glowing idol of **Dagon**; altar grants **advantage vs. Turn Undead** until destroyed or suppressed by **Harae**.

## Combat Package
- **Zombies**: **Igan** (on altar) + a dead guardsman.
- **Skeletons**: archers from watery ledge.
- **Ghouls**: from sinkhole, arrive round 2.

## Clues
- **Igan** stabbed in back near altar; **Grim** guilty.
- Idol valuable but ominous; destroying altar removes protection.

## Outcome
- Capture/surrender **Grim** & soldiers in adjacent area; recover **Igan**; restore beacon.

## Mini Q&A
**Q:** What breaks the altar effect? **A:** A bludgeoning hit > **5 dmg** or **Harae** suppresses end of R1 (if friendly).  
**Q:** Who is the idol? **A:** **Dagon**, the Prince of the Depths.
