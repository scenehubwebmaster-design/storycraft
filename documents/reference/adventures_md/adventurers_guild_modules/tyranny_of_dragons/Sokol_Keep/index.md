# Overview

- **Hook**: The **beacon of Sokol Keep** has gone dark; the **garrison is missing**. Rumors suggest **ancient treasure** beneath the keep.
- **Start**: The **Laughing Goblin** tavern, docks of **Phlan**.
- **Investigation**: Talk to locals (dockworkers, mercenaries), visit **House Sokol** office and **Black Fist** post, take **Karst's ferry** to **Thorn Island**.
- **Revelation**: **Igan Sokol** found a **hidden shrine of Tyr** and, with help of **Harae** (a spirit), uncovered a **subterranean temple (Jade Temple)**.
- **Crime**: **Sergeant Grim** murdered Igan for the **treasure**, awakening **undead guardians** and trapping Grim's cohort below.
- **Climax**: In the **Jade Temple**, defeat undead, expose Grim and return with **Igan’s body**. Restore the beacon and bring culprits to **justice**.

## Quick Timeline
1. Beacon goes dark (2 days ago).  
2. Tavern altercation prompts investigation.  
3. Permits obtained; **ferry** to island.  
4. Search Keep → **East Tower shrine** → **journal & book** lead to **West Tower**.  
5. **Pass phrase** opens trap door to **Jade Temple**.  
6. **Undead fight** around **Jade Idol**; find **Igan’s corpse**.  
7. Apprehend **Grim & Black Fist** survivors; return to Phlan.

## Success States
- Beacon restored; Igan’s body recovered; Grim arrested or defeated; lighthouse secured.

## Failure States
- Party wiped; Grim escapes; undead spread; political fallout with House Sokol / Black Fist.

## Player-Facing Summary (handout-ready)
A storm-tossed lighthouse has **fallen silent**. **Phlan** whispers of **ghosts** and **lost treasure** under **Sokol Keep**. Find what snuffed the **beacon**, and bring the truth to light.

## Mini Q&A
**Q: Where does the adventure start?** A: The **Laughing Goblin** in **Phlan**.  
**Q: Who turned off the beacon?** A: Indirectly **Harae**, to attract help after **Grim** murdered **Igan**.  
**Q: How to reach the dungeon?** A: **West Tower** → stone slab with handprint; say **“Toe Mono, Toe Megalo.”**
