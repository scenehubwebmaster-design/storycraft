## Mission Overview





















Drums in the Marsh


For the past several nights, drums have been heard on the wind, coming from the Twilight Marsh. Each morning, more turn up missing from the nearby farms, leaving only muddy, clawed footprints heading towards the marsh. Can you help the find the lost and bring justice to the abductors? An adventure for 1st-4th level characters.
Adventure Code: DDEX1-7
