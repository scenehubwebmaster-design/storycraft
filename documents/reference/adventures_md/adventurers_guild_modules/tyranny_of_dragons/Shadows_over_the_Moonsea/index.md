# Overview
A ghost ship surfaces every four nights, razing coastal sites. Survivors speak of the **eyes of the dracolich**. Doomguide **Yovir Glandon** hires adventurers to investigate.

## Phases
1. Investigate Phlan: madman, clues, Black Fist.
2. Road travel: merchant encounter, wolves, ferry.
3. Island investigation: infernal folk, secrets, sabotage.
4. Night assault: Cult of the Dragon strike.
5. Finale aboard Ixas' ship as the crew of the **Audacity** return from the Hells.

## Core Mystery
The fisherfolk long ago traded souls with infernals, dooming pirates of the **Audacity**. Now cult wizard **Ixas** hunts lore and masks his raid as ghost activity.

## Timeline
- Day 1,5,9,13: coastal attacks
- Day 15: hook in graveyard
- Day 16: road travel
- Day 17: island arrival / night attack
- Finale: boarding Ixas’ vessel

## Mini Q&A
**Q:** Why every four days? **A:** Corresponds to moon phase shifts.  
**Q:** Is there a dracolich? **A:** Dracolich iconography; undead theatrics mask Cult activity.
