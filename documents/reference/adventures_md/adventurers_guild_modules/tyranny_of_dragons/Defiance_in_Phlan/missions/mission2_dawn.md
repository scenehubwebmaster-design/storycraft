## Hook
Millivent Moss begs rescue for family kidnapped by goblins near peat bog.

## Tracking
- **Wis (Survival)** adjusts goblin count in lair.
- Swampland difficulty; returning raiders imminent.

## Lair highlights
- **2A** arrow slits; alarm rope.
- **2B** tripwire falling-rock trap (can disable rope network).
- **2C** goblins + wolves in cages; goblins release wolves.
- **2D** Boss: **Gorrunk** (bugbear) + goblins; prisoner cages.

## Allies
- Caged **Chaab** (Zhentarim) rewards gems (100 gp) + potion of climbing; tips on Cult dealings.

## Mini Q&A
**Q:** How to free prisoners? **A:** Keys on Gorrunk or **DC 15** lockpick.
**Q:** Are more goblins coming? **A:** Yes — escape quickly.
