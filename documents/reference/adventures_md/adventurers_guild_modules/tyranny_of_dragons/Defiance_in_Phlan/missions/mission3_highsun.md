## Hook
**Brother Keefe** (Kelemvor priest) fears draconic necromancy in Welltran Crypt.

## Gem puzzle
Place tiny gems by **chromatic** correspondence with mural elements:
- white (cold), blue (lightning), red (fire), green (poison), black (acid).

## Chambers
- **Honor Guard**: draconic-skulled skeletons animate if throne meddled.
- **Reanimation Lab**: potion fumes reanimate skeletons; tomes dissolve outside.
- **Teleport Circle**: 4 glyphs empower zombie constructs; mar glyphs to disable.

## Mini Q&A
**Q:** Drink potion? **A:** DC 20 Con save vs 6d6 poison; don’t.
**Q:** Circle destination? **A:** Linked elsewhere; strong magic needed.

## Faction
Keefe is **Lords’ Alliance**; suspects **Welcomers** meddling in dragon lore.
