## Hook
Freona’s daughter **Reece** fetches PCs for a “coin for easy job” Harper meet.

## Beats
- Hooded Harper agent hires PCs to pose as merchant + hirelings.
- Exchange fake diamonds for dragon egg.
- Plant **silver tracking pin** on seller.

## Key checks
- **DC 10 Deception** to maintain identity.
- **DC 10 Sleight of Hand** to plant pin.

## Barn encounter
- Elf **spy** + 3 **guards**; egg is **fake** (flash-blind if broken).
- **Welcomers** thieves ambush post-deal.

## Outcomes
- Payment varies by pin success & egg retrieval.
- Potion of healing possible reward.

## Mini Q&A
**Q:** Can we just hand off the egg to thieves? **A:** Yes; speeds mission; may cause later ambush.
**Q:** Where to deliver? **A:** Tea Kettle stables.
