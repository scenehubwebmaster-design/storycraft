# Overview

- Five time-of-day missions in Phlan, each sourced from **Madame Freona’s Tea Kettle**.
- Touchpoints for factions: **Harpers**, **Zhentarim**, **Lords’ Alliance**.
- Cult of the Dragon interest rising; draconic artifacts and necromancy appear.

## Mission beats (very short)
- **Deepnight**: fake dragon egg trade; plant magical tracking pin.
- **Dawn**: goblin raid; rescue peat bog prisoners before reinforcements.
- **Highsun**: crypt delving; draconic necromancy; teleport circle.
- **Afternoon**: (in original placement) further faction hooks (not included due to truncation).
- **Evening**: Wrap at Freona’s; rumor circulation.

## Success states
- Evidence of cult activity acquired; innocents rescued; necromantic site disrupted.

## Failure states
- Prisoners slain or overwhelmed by returning goblins; necromantic circle intact.

## Player-facing summary
Phlan simmers with faction politics while **dragon cult** stirrings rise. Work quietly from **Madame Freona’s Tea Kettle** to foil cult interests, rescue innocents, and uncover draconic necromancy.
