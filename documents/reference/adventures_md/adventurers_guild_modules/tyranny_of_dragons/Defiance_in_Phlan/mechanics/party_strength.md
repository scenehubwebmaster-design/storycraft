Refer to APL (Average Party Level) guidance:
- 3–4 PCs at APL: Weak
- 3–4 PCs above APL: Average
- 5 PCs at APL: Average
- 5 PCs above APL: Strong
- 6–7 PCs at APL: Strong
- 6–7 PCs above APL: Very Strong

Each mission sidebar offers encounter dials.
