# Overview

Undead stir beneath **Valhingen Graveyard**. The faithful of **Kelemvor** seek help clearing catacombs and discovering the cause.

A **Red Wizard** (Rorreth Monforoth) seeks maps & access to the **Pool of Radiance**.

## Arc
1. Meet Doomguide Yovir Glandon at Valhingen.
2. Explore upper & lower catacombs; environmental hazards.
3. Discover clues about Pool of Radiance.
4. Confront Rorreth & accomplices.

## Success
- Identify necromancer source.
- Recover maps.
- Report back to Yovir.

## Failure
- Rorreth escapes with incriminating intel (intended).
- Excessive theft from dead angers clergy.

**Q:** Where? **A:** Beneath Valhingen Graveyard.
