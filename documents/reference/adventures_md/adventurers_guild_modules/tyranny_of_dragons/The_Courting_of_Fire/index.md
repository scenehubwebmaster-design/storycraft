# Overview

A fugitive cultist **Spernik** flees Phlan and descends into the ruined **Temple of the Scale** seeking a draconic relic to court the favor of red dragon **Scorlworyx**. A multipart key behind trapped shrines protects the relic.

## Arc
1. Investigate thefts, track Spernik (Phlan → mountains).
2. Drive off kobold guards; delve temple.
3. Retrieve four key fragments (white, black, blue, green shrines).
4. Confront Spernik and open reliquary.
5. Face wight **Scalebinder Skovac**.

## Success
- Capture/kill Spernik.
- Recover stolen tomes.
- Return relic for study.

## Failure
- Spernik escapes with relic; cult alliance strengthened.

## Mini Q&A
**Q:** Who is the dragon? **A:** **Scorlworyx**, an ancient red.  
**Q:** What’s the relic? **A:** A massive red scale with runic plating.  
**Q:** Do kobolds help? **A:** Mostly ambush; may turn on Spernik.
