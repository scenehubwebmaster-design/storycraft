# Chapter 3: Creating Adventures (Introduction)

Creating adventures is one of the great joys of being a Dungeon Master. Adventures are **stories** with a beginning, middle, and end—and each adventure contributes an episode to your larger **campaign**.

<!-- chunk:start -->
## Elements of a Great Adventure

1. **A Credible Threat** — A villain, monster, faction, or force with clear goals the heroes can discover and thwart.
2. **Familiar Tropes with Clever Twists** — Use classic fantasy elements, but subvert a detail or expectation.
3. **Clear Focus on the Present** — Let history color the world, but keep the action in the here and now.
4. **Heroes Who Matter** — Player decisions must change outcomes; avoid railroading.
5. **Something for All Player Types** — Mix **exploration**, **social interaction**, and **combat**.
6. **Surprises** — Sparingly applied twists, reveals, and odd pairings keep the table alert.
7. **Useful Maps** — Geography that invites decisions (branching corridors, multiple approaches).
8. **Published Adventures** — Remix hooks, villains, and maps to fit your campaign.
<!-- chunk:end -->

> *Design for agency first. If choices don’t matter, tension can’t land.*
