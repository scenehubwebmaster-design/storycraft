# Overview
A rich semantic adventure module: Spires of Sunder.

## Synopsis
PCs uncover an arcane disturbance centered on a relic tied to ancient planar spires.

## Themes
- planar resonance
- fractured oaths
- elemental corruption

## Success

## Failure
Factional agents seize it first, escalating threats.
