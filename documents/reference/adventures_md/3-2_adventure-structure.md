# Adventure Structure

Every adventure flows through **beginning**, **middle**, and **end**—with clear stakes and evolving pressure.

<!-- chunk:start -->
## The Beginning: Hooks

Hooks ignite curiosity and give characters a reason to act.

- Threat appears (dragon at the gate; assassins in an alley)
- Accidental discovery (forbidden text; hidden door)
- Urgent plea (desperate noble; cursed village)
- Strange opportunity (mysterious map; prophetic dream)

Keep the opening **focused** and end the first session with a strong aim.
<!-- chunk:end -->

<!-- chunk:start -->
## The Middle: Momentum & Agency

- **Villains advance** their plans; heroes respond and reshape the board.
- **Stakes evolve** with revelations, betrayals, and shifting alliances.
- **Choices matter**; design encounters with multiple viable approaches.
<!-- chunk:end -->

<!-- chunk:start -->
## The End: Climaxes with Consequences

A great climax reflects player decisions, risks failure, and changes the world—even if threads remain for future play.
<!-- chunk:end -->

```table
| Act | Focus                                   | DM Questions                                 |
|-----|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Beginning | Hook & immediate aim               | Why should they care right now?              |
| Middle | Rising pressure & meaningful choices | How do choices reshape the threat?           |
| End | High-stakes resolution                  | What changes after success/failure/partial?  |
```
