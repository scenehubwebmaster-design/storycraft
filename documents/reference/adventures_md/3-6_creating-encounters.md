# Creating Encounters

Encounters are the **scenes** that form your adventure. Each should have a clear objective and meaningful consequences for success, failure, or partial success.

<!-- chunk:start -->
## Common Encounter Objectives

- **Make Peace:** Mediate a conflict between two sides.
- **Protect an NPC/Object:** Guard someone or something under threat.
- **Retrieve an Object:** The item matters more than defeating foes.
- **Run a Gauntlet:** Survive traversal hazards under pressure.
- **Sneak In:** Avoid detection; complications if exposed.
- **Stop a Ritual:** Fight through defenders and disrupt magic (often on a timer).
- **Take Out a Single Target:** Break through minions to defeat the linchpin.
<!-- chunk:end -->
