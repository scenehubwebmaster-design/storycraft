
### Spellcasting Focus
You may use an **Arcane Focus** as your spellcasting focus for Warlock spells.

## ⚡ Level 2 — Magical Cunning

You can perform an **esoteric rite** for 1 minute.  
At the end of the ritual, you regain **expended Pact Magic spell slots** equal to **half your maximum (rounded up)**.

> Once used, you must finish a **Long Rest** before using it again.

---

## 👁️ Level 3 — Warlock Subclass

Choose your **Patron subclass**, representing the being that granted your power:
- **Archfey Patron**
- **Celestial Patron**
- **Fiend Patron**
- **Great Old One Patron**

Each subclass grants features at specific Warlock levels, detailed later in this section.

---

## 💪 Level 4 — Ability Score Improvement

You gain one **Ability Score Improvement (ASI)** or a **Feat** of your choice.  
You gain this feature again at levels 8, 12, and 16.

---

## 📞 Level 9 — Contact Patron

You can now directly commune with your patron.

You always have the spell *Contact Other Plane* prepared.  
When you use it to contact your patron:
- You **cast it without expending a slot**, and  
- You **automatically succeed** on the saving throw.

> Once used, you must finish a Long Rest before using it this way again.

---

## 🔮 Level 11 — Mystic Arcanum

Your patron grants you a powerful magical secret — an **Arcanum**.

Choose one **6th-level Warlock spell**.  
You can cast this spell once per Long Rest **without expending a spell slot**.

As you gain levels, you receive more Arcanum spells:
- Level 13 → 7th-level spell  
- Level 15 → 8th-level spell  
- Level 17 → 9th-level spell  

> You regain all uses of *Mystic Arcanum* after a Long Rest.  
> You may replace one Arcanum spell with another of the same level when you level up.

---

## 🌠 Level 19 — Epic Boon

You gain an **Epic Boon Feat** (see Chapter 5).  
*Recommended:* Boon of Fate.

---

## 🌌 Level 20 — Eldritch Master

When you use **Magical Cunning**, you regain **all expended Pact Magic spell slots** instead of half.

# 🌌 **Warlock Subclasses**

Each subclass represents the patron who grants the Warlock’s powers and defines their unique magical style.

---

## 🌿 **Archfey Patron — Bargain with Whimsical Fey**

Your pact ties you to the capricious powers of the **Feywild**, such as the Queen of Air and Darkness, Titania, or the Prince of Frost.

### Archfey Spells

| **Warlock Level** | **Spells** |
|--------------------|------------|
| 3 | Calm Emotions, Faerie Fire, Misty Step, Phantasmal Force, Sleep |
| 5 | Blink, Plant Growth |
| 7 | Dominate Beast, Greater Invisibility |
| 9 | Dominate Person, Seeming |

### Level 3 — Steps of the Fey
You can cast *Misty Step* a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (min 1).  
When you cast it, choose one:
- **Refreshing Step:** Gain `1d10` Temp HP or grant it to an ally within 10 ft.  
- **Taunting Step:** Nearby enemies make a Wisdom save or have **Disadvantage on attacks** against others until your next turn.

### Level 6 — Misty Escape
You can cast *Misty Step* as a **Reaction** when you take damage.  
You also gain:
- **Disappearing Step:** Turn invisible until your next turn.  
- **Dreadful Step:** Foes within 5 ft of where you left or arrived (your choice) take `2d10` Psychic damage (Wis save for half).

### Level 10 — Beguiling Defenses
You are **immune to the Charmed condition**.  
When hit by an attack, you can **halve the damage** and force the attacker to make a Wisdom save or take Psychic damage equal to what you took.  
> Recharge: Long Rest or expend a Pact slot.

### Level 14 — Bewitching Magic
After casting an **Enchantment or Illusion** spell using a spell slot, you may immediately cast *Misty Step* **without expending a slot**.

---

## ☀️ **Celestial Patron — Call on the Power of the Heavens**

Your pact draws from the Upper Planes — beings of radiant virtue such as couatls, unicorns, or empyreans.

### Celestial Spells

| **Warlock Level** | **Spells** |
|--------------------|------------|
| 3 | Aid, Cure Wounds, Guiding Bolt, Lesser Restoration, Light, Sacred Flame |
| 5 | Daylight, Revivify |
| 7 | Guardian of Faith, Wall of Fire |
| 9 | Greater Restoration, Summon Celestial |

### Level 3 — Healing Light
You gain a pool of `d6s` equal to 1 + your Warlock level.  
As a Bonus Action, heal a creature within 60 ft, spending dice up to your Charisma modifier.  
Regain all dice after a Long Rest.

### Level 6 — Radiant Soul
Gain **Resistance to Radiant damage**.  
Once per turn, add your **Charisma modifier** to a spell’s **Radiant or Fire damage**.

### Level 10 — Celestial Resilience
When you use **Magical Cunning** or finish a rest, you and up to 5 allies gain Temp HP equal to your **Warlock level + CHA modifier** (half that for allies).

### Level 14 — Searing Vengeance
When you or an ally within 60 ft would make a Death Save, unleash radiant power:
- Regain HP = ½ your max HP  
- End *Prone* condition  
- Enemies in 30 ft take `2d8 + CHA` Radiant damage and are **Blinded** until end of turn  
> Recharge: Long Rest.

---

## 🔥 **Fiend Patron — Deal with the Lower Planes**

Your pact draws upon the infernal might of the Lower Planes. You may serve demons, devils, or other fiends such as **Asmodeus**, **Demogorgon**, or **Orcus**.

### Fiend Spells

| **Warlock Level** | **Spells** |
|--------------------|------------|
| 3 | Burning Hands, Command, Scorching Ray, Suggestion |
| 5 | Fireball, Stinking Cloud |
| 7 | Fire Shield, Wall of Fire |
| 9 | Geas, Insect Plague |

### Level 3 — Dark One’s Blessing
When you or an ally within 10 ft reduces an enemy to 0 HP, you gain Temp HP = **Charisma modifier + Warlock level**.

### Level 6 — Dark One’s Own Luck
When you make an ability check or saving throw, add `1d10` to the roll after seeing it.  
Uses = CHA modifier (min 1), regained on Long Rest.

### Level 10 — Fiendish Resilience
Choose one **damage type (not Force)** whenever you finish a rest.  
You gain **Resistance** to that damage until you choose a new one.

### Level 14 — Hurl Through Hell
Once per turn when you hit with an attack, force the target to make a CHA save or be banished through a nightmare dimension.  
The creature takes `8d10` Psychic damage (unless a Fiend) and returns **Incapacitated** until your next turn.  
> Recharge: Long Rest or expend a Pact slot.

---

## 👁️ **Great Old One Patron — Unearth Forbidden Lore**

Your patron is an unfathomable being from beyond reality — perhaps **Cthulhu**, **Tharizdun**, or the **Dark Hunger Hadar**.

### Great Old One Spells

| **Warlock Level** | **Spells** |
|--------------------|------------|
| 3 | Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Phantasmal Force, Tasha’s Hideous Laughter |
| 5 | Clairvoyance, Hunger of Hadar |
| 7 | Confusion, Summon Aberration |
| 9 | Modify Memory, Telekinesis |

### Level 3 — Awakened Mind
As a Bonus Action, form a **telepathic bond** with a creature within 30 ft.  
You and the target can communicate telepathically while within miles equal to your CHA modifier.  
Lasts for minutes equal to your Warlock level.

### Level 3 — Psychic Spells
When you cast a Warlock spell that deals damage, you can change the damage type to **Psychic**.  
You can also cast **Enchantment or Illusion** spells **without Verbal or Somatic components**.

### Level 6 — Clairvoyant Combatant
When you use *Awakened Mind*, the target must make a Wisdom save.  
On failure, it has **Disadvantage** on attacks against you, and you have **Advantage** against it.  
> Recharge: Short/Long Rest or spend a Pact slot.

### Level 10 — Eldritch Hex
You always have *Hex* prepared.  
When cast, the target also has **Disadvantage on saving throws** of the chosen ability.

### Level 10 — Thought Shield
Your thoughts cannot be read unless you allow it.  
You gain **Resistance to Psychic damage**, and when a creature deals Psychic damage to you, it takes the **same amount**.
