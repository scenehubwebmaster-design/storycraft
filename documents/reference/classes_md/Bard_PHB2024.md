# 🎵 The Bard

> **“Through rhythm, rhyme, and resonance, the multiverse itself sings.”**

Bards weave the Words of Creation into their art — channeling the primal music that shaped the cosmos. They inspire, deceive, and heal with their songs and gestures, turning performance into power.

---

| **Trait**                | **Details**                                                                                                   |

| **Weapon Proficiencies** | Simple weapons                                                                                                |

| **Starting Equipment**   | Choose:<br>**A)** Leather Armor, 2 Daggers, Musical Instrument, Entertainer’s Pack, and 19 GP<br>**B)** 90 GP |

---

> **Flavor:**  
> Bards invoke magic through _music, dance,_ and _verse._ They believe the multiverse was spoken into being — and fragments of that power echo still. Through art, they harness these Words of Creation to inspire allies and manipulate reality.

---

## ⚔️ Becoming a Bard

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all traits from the **Core Bard Traits** table.
- Gain the **Bard's Level 1 features** listed in the Bard Features table.

### As a Multiclass Character

- Gain the following from Core Bard Traits:
  - Hit Point Die
  - Proficiency in one skill of your choice
  - Proficiency in one musical instrument
  - Light armor training
- Gain the Bard's **Level 1 features**.

## 🎭 Level Features

### **Level 1: Bardic Inspiration**

You can inspire others through performance.  
As a **Bonus Action**, a creature within 60 ft. who can see or hear you gains one Bardic Inspiration die (d6).

- **Use:** Add the die to one failed d20 Test within the next hour.
- **Uses:** Equal to your **Charisma modifier** (min 1), regained on a Long Rest.
- **Scaling:**
  - Level 5 → d8
  - Level 10 → d10
  - Level 15 → d12

---

### **Level 1: Spellcasting**

You learn to cast spells through your art.

- **Cantrips Known:** 2
- **Spell Slots:** See Bard Features table
- **Prepared Spells:** Choose 4 Bard spells (e.g., _Charm Person_, _Healing Word_)
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Charisma
- **Spellcasting Focus:** Musical Instrument

> **Example:**  
> _A bard strums a lute as threads of golden light swirl around an ally, knitting their wounds._

---

### **Level 2: Expertise**

Choose 2 skill proficiencies to gain Expertise (double proficiency bonus).  
At level 9, choose 2 more.

### **Level 2: Jack of All Trades**

Add half your proficiency bonus to any ability check that doesn’t already use it.

---

### **Level 3: Bard Subclass**

Choose a Bardic College:

- **College of Dance**
- **College of Glamour**
- **College of Lore**
- **College of Valor**

Each provides subclass features at specified levels.

---

### **Level 4, 8, 12, 16: Ability Score Improvement**

Gain an **ASI** or a **Feat** of your choice.

---

### **Level 5: Font of Inspiration**

Regain Bardic Inspiration uses after a **Short or Long Rest**.  
You may also expend a spell slot to regain one use.

---

### **Level 7: Countercharm**

When you or a creature within 30 ft. fails a save vs. _Charmed_ or _Frightened_, use your **Reaction** to let them reroll with Advantage.

---

### **Level 10: Magical Secrets**

When preparing new spells, you can select from the **Bard, Cleric, Druid, or Wizard** spell lists.  
These count as Bard spells for you.

---

### **Level 18: Superior Inspiration**

When you roll Initiative, if you have fewer than two Bardic Inspiration dice, regain enough to have two.

---

### **Level 19: Epic Boon**

Gain an **Epic Boon feat** (see Chapter 5).

> Recommended: _Boon of Spell Recall._

---

### **Level 20: Words of Creation**

You have mastered the **Words of Life and Death.**

- Always have _Power Word Heal_ and _Power Word Kill_ prepared.
- Each can target **a second creature** within 10 ft. of the first.

## 🎓 Bardic Colleges

### **College of Dance**

_Move in harmony with the cosmos._

**Level 3: Dazzling Footwork**

- Gain **Unarmored Defense** (10 + DEX + CHA).
- **Dance Virtuoso:** Advantage on Charisma (Performance) checks when dancing.
- When you use Bardic Inspiration, you may also make an **Unarmed Strike** (DEX-based).

**Level 6: Inspiring Movement**

- When an enemy ends its turn within 5 ft., use Reaction and Bardic Inspiration to move (and one ally can move half speed too) without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

**Level 14: Leading Evasion**

- When you succeed on a Dexterity save for half damage, take none.
- Allies within 5 ft. also gain this benefit.

---

### **College of Glamour**

_Weave beguiling Fey magic._

**Level 3: Beguiling Magic**

- Always have _Charm Person_ and _Mirror Image_ prepared.
- After casting an Enchantment or Illusion spell, one creature within 60 ft. must succeed on a Wisdom save or be **Charmed or Frightened** for 1 minute.

**Level 3: Mantle of Inspiration**

- Bonus Action: expend Bardic Inspiration to grant Temporary HP to allies (2× die roll) and allow them to move without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

**Level 6: Mantle of Majesty**

- Always have _Command_ prepared.
- Bonus Action: cast _Command_ without a slot; creatures Charmed by you automatically fail saves.

**Level 14: Unbreakable Majesty**

- Bonus Action: assume a majestic aura for 1 minute.
- When hit, attacker must make CHA save vs. your Spell DC or miss instead.

---

### **College of Lore**

_Plumb the depths of knowledge._

**Level 3: Bonus Proficiencies**

- Gain proficiency in 3 additional skills.

**Level 3: Cutting Words**

- Reaction: expend Bardic Inspiration to reduce enemy attack, ability check, or damage roll by the die value.

**Level 6: Magical Discoveries**

- Learn 2 spells from _Cleric_, _Druid_, or _Wizard_ lists. Always prepared.

**Level 14: Peerless Skill**

- When you fail an ability check or attack roll, expend Bardic Inspiration to add the die to the result.
- If still fails, die is not expended.

---

### **College of Valor**

_Sing the deeds of heroes._

**Level 3: Combat Inspiration**

- Affected allies can use your Bardic Inspiration die for:
  - **Defense:** Add die to AC vs. one attack (Reaction).
  - **Offense:** Add die to damage on a hit.

**Level 3: Martial Training**

- Gain proficiency with **Medium Armor**, **Shields**, and **Martial Weapons**.
- You can use a weapon as a **Spellcasting Focus.**

**Level 6: Extra Attack**

- Attack twice when taking the Attack action.
- Can replace one attack with a Bard cantrip.

**Level 14: Battle Magic**

- After casting a spell (casting time 1 action), make a weapon attack as a **Bonus Action.**

# 🎤 A Bard’s Repertoire

> _“Are your verses hymns to the gods, bawdy tavern songs, or laments for the fallen?”_

When you play a Bard, think about your artistic style and what themes inspire your performances — beauty, joy, loss, or rebellion.
