# 🗡️ The Rogue
> **“Quick hands, quicker mind — and gone before you knew I was there.”**

Rogues rely on **cunning, stealth, and precise strikes** to outwit and outmaneuver their foes. Whether slipping past guards, picking locks, or striking unseen from the shadows, a Rogue’s skill lies in being exactly where the enemy least expects.  
Some began their careers as thieves or assassins; others use their wit to fight injustice. Whatever their path, **no one matches a Rogue’s resourcefulness.**

## 🎭 Becoming a Rogue

### As a Level 1 Character
- Gain all traits listed in **Core Rogue Traits**.  
- Gain the **Rogue’s level 1 features** listed in the Rogue Features Table.

### As a Multiclass Character
- Gain: Hit Point Die, proficiency in one Rogue skill, Thieves’ Tools proficiency, and Light Armor training.  
- Gain the Rogue’s level 1 features.

## 💀 Class Features

### **Level 1: Expertise**
Gain **Expertise** in two of your skill proficiencies.  
(*Sleight of Hand* and *Stealth* recommended.)  
At **Level 6**, gain Expertise in two more skills.

---

### **Level 1: Sneak Attack**
Once per turn, deal **extra damage** when your attack roll has **Advantage** using a Finesse or Ranged weapon.

- **Damage:** Starts at `1d6` and scales with level.  
- You may also Sneak Attack if an **ally** is within 5 ft of the target and you don’t have Disadvantage.  
- The extra damage type matches your weapon’s.

---

### **Level 1: Thieves’ Cant**
You learn **Thieves’ Cant** — a secret code of slang and symbols — and one additional language of your choice.

---

### **Level 1: Weapon Mastery**
Choose two weapons with which you’re proficient to gain their **Mastery Properties** (e.g., Daggers and Shortbows).  
Change choices after a Long Rest.

---

### **Level 2: Cunning Action**
As a **Bonus Action**, you can **Dash**, **Disengage**, or **Hide**.

---

### **Level 3: Rogue Subclass**
Choose a **Rogue Subclass**, such as:
- **Arcane Trickster** – Stealth and magic intertwined  
- **Assassin** – Deadly precision and disguise  
- **Soulknife** – Psionic power and silent strikes  
- **Thief** – Master of agility and magical treasures

---

### **Level 3: Steady Aim**
As a **Bonus Action**, gain **Advantage** on your next attack roll — but your **Speed becomes 0** until end of turn.

---

### **Level 4: Ability Score Improvement**
Increase an ability score or gain a feat of your choice.  
You gain this feature again at levels 8, 10, 12, and 16.

---

### **Level 5: Cunning Strike**
Enhance your Sneak Attacks with tactical effects.  
When you deal Sneak Attack damage, you can forgo some of your Sneak Attack dice to apply one of the following effects:

| **Effect** | **Cost** | **Description** |
|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Poison** | 1d6 | Target makes CON save or is *Poisoned* for 1 min (repeat save each turn). Requires a Poisoner’s Kit. |
| **Trip** | 1d6 | Target (Large or smaller) makes DEX save or is *Prone*. |
| **Withdraw** | 1d6 | Move up to half Speed immediately without provoking Opportunity Attacks. |

---

### **Level 5: Uncanny Dodge**
When you can see an attacker, use your **Reaction** to **halve** the attack’s damage.

---

### **Level 7: Evasion**
When subjected to an effect allowing a **DEX save for half damage**, take **no damage** on a success and **half** on a failure.

---

### **Level 7: Reliable Talent**
When making an ability check with a proficient skill/tool, treat rolls of **9 or lower as 10**.

---

### **Level 11: Improved Cunning Strike**
You can apply **two Cunning Strike effects** at once, paying their die cost separately.

---

### **Level 14: Devious Strikes**
Gain new Cunning Strike options:

| **Effect** | **Cost** | **Description** |
|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Daze** | 2d6 | CON save or target can only move, act, or take a bonus action next turn (choose one). |
| **Knock Out** | 6d6 | CON save or target is *Unconscious* for 1 minute (save ends). |
| **Obscure** | 3d6 | DEX save or target is *Blinded* until end of its next turn. |

---

### **Level 15: Slippery Mind**
Gain proficiency in **Wisdom** and **Charisma saving throws.**

---

### **Level 18: Elusive**
No attack roll has Advantage against you unless you are **Incapacitated.**

---

### **Level 19: Epic Boon**
Gain an **Epic Boon feat** (recommended: *Boon of the Night Spirit*).

---

### **Level 20: Stroke of Luck**
When you **fail a d20 Test**, you can treat the roll as a **20**.  
> Once per Short or Long Rest.

## 🎩 Rogue Subclasses

---

### 🌀 **Arcane Trickster**
> *“A bit of magic never hurt a good heist.”*

#### **Level 3: Spellcasting**
You gain limited spellcasting using **Wizard spells**.

- **Cantrips:** Learn *Mage Hand* and 2 more Wizard cantrips (*Mind Sliver*, *Minor Illusion* recommended).  
- **Prepared Spells:** Begin with 3 Level 1 Wizard spells (*Charm Person*, *Disguise Self*, *Fog Cloud* recommended).  
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Intelligence  
- **Focus:** Arcane Focus  

#### **Level 3: Mage Hand Legerdemain**
You can cast *Mage Hand* as a **Bonus Action**, and make it **Invisible**.  
You may perform **Sleight of Hand** checks through it.

#### **Level 9: Magical Ambush**
If you’re **Invisible** when casting a spell on a creature, it has **Disadvantage** on the saving throw.

#### **Level 13: Versatile Trickster**
When using your **Trip** Cunning Strike, you may also apply it to another creature within 5 ft of your spectral *Mage Hand*.

#### **Level 17: Spell Thief**
When a creature casts a spell targeting you, use a **Reaction** to force an **INT save (DC = 8 + DEX mod + Prof)**.  
On failure, the spell is **negated** and you **learn it for 8 hours**.  
The original caster cannot cast it again until that time passes.

> *Once per Long Rest.*

---

### 🕯️ **Assassin**
> *“A single breath, a single strike — and the job is done.”*

#### **Level 3: Assassinate**
- **Initiative Advantage:** Gain Advantage on Initiative rolls.  
- **Surprise Strikes:** During the first round of combat, gain Advantage on attack rolls against creatures who haven’t acted yet.  
If your Sneak Attack hits that round, add **extra damage equal to your Rogue level.**

#### **Level 3: Assassin’s Tools**
Gain proficiency with **Disguise Kit** and **Poisoner’s Kit**.

#### **Level 9: Infiltration Expertise**
- **Masterful Mimicry:** Mimic speech or handwriting after 1 hour of study.  
- **Roving Aim:** Your Speed is not reduced to 0 when using *Steady Aim*.

#### **Level 13: Envenom Weapons**
When you apply the **Poison** Cunning Strike, the target also takes **2d6 Poison damage** on failed saves (ignores resistance).

#### **Level 17: Death Strike**
When you hit with Sneak Attack in the **first round**, target must make a **CON save (DC = 8 + DEX mod + Prof)** or the attack’s total damage is **doubled**.

---

### 🧠 **Soulknife**
> *“The mind cuts deeper than steel.”*

#### **Level 3: Psionic Power**
You gain **Psionic Energy Dice** to fuel special powers.

| **Rogue Level** | **Die Size** | **Number of Dice** |
|------------------|--------------|--------------------|
| 3 | d6 | 4 |
| 5 | d8 | 6 |
| 9 | d8 | 8 |
| 11 | d10 | 8 |
| 13 | d10 | 10 |
| 17 | d12 | 12 |

- **Psi-Bolstered Knack:** Add a Psionic die to a failed skill/tool check (expend only if it succeeds).  
- **Psychic Whispers:** Telepathically link with creatures (within 1 mile) for hours equal to die roll.

#### **Level 3: Psychic Blades**
Manifest **Psychic Blades** as weapons:
- Damage: 1d6 Psychic  
- Range: 60/120 ft  
- Properties: *Finesse, Thrown, Vex*  
After your Attack Action, you may make a **Bonus Action** attack with a second blade (1d4 Psychic).

#### **Level 9: Soul Blades**
Gain:
- **Homing Strikes:** Add Psionic die to missed attack rolls.  
- **Psychic Teleportation:** Throw a blade and teleport up to `10 × die roll` feet.

#### **Level 13: Psychic Veil**
As a **Magic Action**, become **Invisible for 1 hour** or until you attack or force a save.  
> Recharge on Long Rest or expend 1 Psionic Die to reuse.

#### **Level 17: Rend Mind**
When Sneak Attacking, force **WIS save (DC = 8 + DEX mod + Prof)** or target is **Stunned (1 min)**.  
> Recharge on Long Rest or expend 3 Psionic Dice.

---

### 🪙 **Thief**
> *“Locks, ledges, and loot — my kind of adventure.”*

#### **Level 3: Fast Hands**
As a **Bonus Action**, you may:
- Use *Sleight of Hand* (pick locks, disarm traps, pick pockets), or  
- Take the **Utilize** or **Magic** action to use a magic item.

#### **Level 3: Second-Story Work**
Gain:
- **Climb Speed** equal to your Speed.  
- Use **Dexterity** instead of Strength for Jump distance.

#### **Level 9: Supreme Sneak**
Gain a new Cunning Strike option:

| **Effect** | **Cost** | **Description** |
|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Stealth Attack** | 1d6 | If you are Hidden and have the Invisible condition, this attack does not end your invisibility if you end your turn behind ¾ or Total Cover. |

#### **Level 13: Use Magic Device**
Gain enhanced magic item mastery:
- **Attune to 4 items** at once.  
- **Save Charges:** On a d6 roll of 6, do not expend a magic item’s charges.  
- **Use Spell Scrolls:** Cast any scroll using INT.  
  - *Cantrips or 1st-level spells* = automatic success.  
  - Higher-level scrolls: succeed INT (Arcana) check `DC 10 + spell level`, or scroll disintegrates.

#### **Level 17: Thief’s Reflexes**
During the **first round of combat**, take **two turns** —  
your normal Initiative, and again at Initiative **–10**.

# 🗡️ Summary

> **Core Identity:** Agile tactician and master of precision  
> **Primary Role:** Damage dealer, skill expert, and infiltration specialist  
> **Complexity:** Moderate to High  
> **Synergy With:** Bards, Fighters, Monks, Rangers
