# ✨ The Cleric
> **“Your faith is your shield, your voice the conduit of the divine.”**

Clerics are miracle-workers who draw power from the gods and channel it through will and devotion.  
Each Cleric serves as both mortal and vessel—bridging the material and the divine through prayer, conviction, and radiant purpose.

---

## 🕊️ Core Cleric Traits

| **Trait** | **Details** |
|------------|-------------|
| **Primary Ability** | Wisdom |
| **Hit Point Die** | 1d8 per Cleric level |
| **Saving Throws** | Wisdom, Charisma |
| **Skill Proficiencies** | Choose 2: History, Insight, Medicine, Persuasion, or Religion |
| **Weapon Proficiencies** | Simple weapons |
| **Armor Training** | Light and Medium Armor, Shields |
| **Starting Equipment** | Choose:<br>**A)** Chain Shirt, Shield, Mace, Holy Symbol, Priest’s Pack, and 7 GP<br>**B)** 110 GP |

> **Note:** A Cleric’s power is granted through divine favor rather than arcane study, and thus their strength comes from faith rather than formula.

---

## 🙏 Becoming a Cleric

### As a Level 1 Character
- Gain all traits listed in the **Core Cleric Traits** table.  
- Gain the Cleric’s level 1 features from the **Cleric Features Table**.

### As a Multiclass Character
- Gain the following traits from **Core Cleric Traits**:  
  * Hit Point Die  
  * Training with Light & Medium Armor and Shields  
- Gain the Cleric’s **level 1 features**, then see *Chapter 2: Multiclassing* to determine available spell slots.

## 🕯️ Class Features

### **Level 1: Spellcasting**
Through prayer and devotion, you have learned to channel divine energy into miracles.

- **Cantrips Known:** 3 (see Cleric Spell List)  
  *Recommended:* *Guidance*, *Sacred Flame*, *Thaumaturgy*  
- **Spell Slots:** See table above  
- **Prepared Spells:** Choose 4 level 1 spells (*Bless*, *Cure Wounds*, *Guiding Bolt*, *Shield of Faith* recommended).  
- **Changing Prepared Spells:** After each Long Rest, replace any prepared spells.  
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Wisdom  
- **Spellcasting Focus:** Holy Symbol  

> **Example:**  
> *A Cleric raises a holy emblem toward a wounded ally; golden light washes over the battlefield, sealing their wounds.*

---

### **Level 1: Divine Order**
Choose one sacred role to define your devotion:

- **Protector.** Gain proficiency with **Martial Weapons** and **Heavy Armor**.  
- **Thaumaturge.** Learn one additional Cleric cantrip.  
  In addition, you gain a bonus to Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) checks equal to your Wisdom modifier (min +1).

---

### **Level 2: Channel Divinity**
Channel the raw energy of your faith to produce miraculous effects.

- **Uses:** 2; regain 1 after a Short Rest, all after a Long Rest.  
- **Save DC:** Equals your Spell Save DC.  
- **Divine Spark (Magic Action):**  
  Heal or harm a creature within 30 ft.  
  Roll 1d8 + Wisdom modifier → heal or deal Necrotic/Radiant damage (target saves Con, ½ damage on success).  
  *Scales:* +1d8 at Lv 7 (2d8), Lv 13 (3d8), Lv 18 (4d8).  
- **Turn Undead (Magic Action):**  
  Present your Holy Symbol; each Undead within 30 ft makes a Wisdom save or becomes **Frightened and Incapacitated** for 1 minute, fleeing from you. Ends early if it takes damage or you are Incapacitated.

---

### **Level 3: Cleric Subclass**
Choose a divine domain:
- **Life Domain** – Healers and restorers  
- **Light Domain** – Radiant truth and revelation  
- **Trickery Domain** – Deception and subversion  
- **War Domain** – Divine might and courage

---

### **Level 4, 8, 12, 16: Ability Score Improvement**
Gain an **Ability Score Improvement** or **Feat** for which you qualify.

---

### **Level 5: Sear Undead**
When you use *Turn Undead*, roll a number of d8s equal to your Wisdom modifier (min 1d8).  
Each Undead that fails its save takes Radiant damage equal to the total.  
This does not end the turning effect.

---

### **Level 7: Blessed Strikes**
Choose one of the following:

- **Divine Strike:** Once per turn when you hit with a weapon attack, deal +1d8 Necrotic or Radiant damage.  
- **Potent Spellcasting:** Add your Wisdom modifier to damage dealt by your Cleric cantrips.

---

### **Level 10: Divine Intervention**
As a Magic Action, choose a Cleric spell of 5th level or lower (no Reaction spells) and cast it **without expending a spell slot** or Material components.  
Can’t use again until after a Long Rest.

---

### **Level 14: Improved Blessed Strikes**
Your chosen Blessed Strike option improves:

- **Divine Strike:** Extra damage = 2d8.  
- **Potent Spellcasting:** When you deal damage with a cantrip, grant yourself or an ally within 60 ft Temporary HP equal to 2 × Wisdom modifier.

---

### **Level 19: Epic Boon**
Gain an **Epic Boon feat** (see Chapter 5).  
> **Recommended:** *Boon of Fate.*

---

### **Level 20: Greater Divine Intervention**
Your connection to your deity deepens.  
You may choose *Wish* as your Divine Intervention spell.  
After doing so, you can’t use Divine Intervention again until you finish **2d4 Long Rests.**

## 🛕 Cleric Subclasses — Divine Domains

> Each domain reflects a different aspect of divine power — healing, radiance, mischief, or warfare.  
> Choose a Domain at Level 3 to determine your subclass features.

---

### **Life Domain**
*“Soothe the hurts of the world.”*

**Level 3: Disciple of Life**  
When you cast a healing spell with a slot, the target regains + (2 + spell level) HP.

**Life Domain Spells**

| **Cleric Level** | **Spells Always Prepared** |
|------------------|-----------------------------|
| 3 | *Aid*, *Bless*, *Cure Wounds*, *Lesser Restoration* |
| 5 | *Mass Healing Word*, *Revivify* |
| 7 | *Aura of Life*, *Death Ward* |
| 9 | *Greater Restoration*, *Mass Cure Wounds* |

**Level 3: Preserve Life**  
As a Magic Action, expend a use of Channel Divinity to restore HP equal to 5 × Cleric level, divided among Bloodied creatures within 30 ft. No creature can be restored beyond half its maximum HP.

**Level 6: Blessed Healer**  
When you heal others, you also regain HP = 2 + spell slot level.

**Level 17: Supreme Healing**  
Whenever you roll healing dice, use the maximum value on each die.

---

### **Light Domain**
*“Bring light to banish darkness.”*

**Level 3: Light Domain Spells**

| **Cleric Level** | **Spells Always Prepared** |
|------------------|-----------------------------|
| 3 | *Burning Hands*, *Faerie Fire*, *Scorching Ray*, *See Invisibility* |
| 5 | *Daylight*, *Fireball* |
| 7 | *Arcane Eye*, *Wall of Fire* |
| 9 | *Flame Strike*, *Scrying* |

**Level 3: Radiance of the Dawn**  
As a Magic Action, expend Channel Divinity to emit divine light in a 30 ft radius. Dispels magical darkness and deals (2d10 + Cleric level) Radiant damage (½ on save).

**Level 3: Warding Flare**  
Reaction to impose Disadvantage on a creature’s attack within 30 ft. Uses = Wisdom modifier (min 1), recharged after Long Rest.

**Level 6: Improved Warding Flare**  
Regain uses after Short Rest. When used, target gains Temp HP = 2d6 + Wisdom modifier.

**Level 17: Corona of Light**  
Emit sunlight for 1 minute. Enemies in bright light have Disadvantage on saves against your Fire or Radiant effects. Uses = Wisdom modifier (min 1), recharge on Long Rest.

---

### **Trickery Domain**
*“Make mischief and challenge authority.”*

**Level 3: Blessing of the Trickster**  
Grant Advantage on Stealth checks to yourself or a creature within 30 ft until Long Rest or reused.

**Level 3: Invoke Duplicity**  
Bonus Action: Create an illusory duplicate within 30 ft (using Channel Divinity). Illusion lasts 1 minute and grants Advantage on attacks when you and it are both adjacent to a target. You can cast spells from its space and move it 30 ft as a Bonus Action.

**Trickery Domain Spells**

| **Cleric Level** | **Spells Always Prepared** |
|------------------|-----------------------------|
| 3 | *Charm Person*, *Disguise Self*, *Invisibility*, *Pass Without Trace* |
| 5 | *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Nondetection* |
| 7 | *Confusion*, *Dimension Door* |
| 9 | *Dominate Person*, *Modify Memory* |

**Level 6: Trickster’s Transposition**  
When you move your illusion via *Invoke Duplicity*, you can teleport to swap places with it.

**Level 17: Improved Duplicity**  
Your illusion grants Advantage to allies attacking creatures within 5 ft. When it ends, you or a creature within 5 ft regains HP = your Cleric level.

---

### **War Domain**
*“Inspire valor and smite foes.”*

**Level 3: Guided Strike**  
When you or an ally within 30 ft misses an attack, expend Channel Divinity to grant +10 to the roll (using your Reaction if it’s not your turn).

**War Domain Spells**

| **Cleric Level** | **Spells Always Prepared** |
|------------------|-----------------------------|
| 3 | *Guiding Bolt*, *Magic Weapon*, *Shield of Faith*, *Spiritual Weapon* |
| 5 | *Crusader’s Mantle*, *Spirit Guardians* |
| 7 | *Fire Shield*, *Freedom of Movement* |
| 9 | *Hold Monster*, *Steel Wind Strike* |

**Level 3: War Priest**  
Bonus Action: Make one weapon attack or Unarmed Strike. Uses = Wisdom modifier  (min 1), regain on Short or Long Rest.

**Level 6: War God’s Blessing**  
Expend Channel Divinity to cast *Shield of Faith* or *Spiritual Weapon* without using a spell slot. These castings last 1 minute without Concentration.

**Level 17: Avatar of Battle**  
You gain **Resistance** to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

# ⚖️ Summary

> **Core Identity:** Divine spellcaster and servant of higher powers  
> **Primary Role:** Support / Healer / Radiant Damage  
> **Complexity:** Moderate  
> **Synergy With:** Paladins, Bards, Warlocks, and other Wisdom-based classes
