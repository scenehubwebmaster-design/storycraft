# 🏹 The Ranger
> **“Between civilization and the wild, I stand guard.”**

Far from the bustle of cities, Rangers keep their endless vigil amid forests, mountains, and plains.  
Trained to hunt monsters and track prey with the precision of a predator, Rangers are the ultimate wilderness scouts — equally skilled with blade, bow, and primal magic.  
Their purpose: to protect the balance of nature and safeguard the realms from the forces that would destroy it.

## 🗺️ Becoming a Ranger

### As a Level 1 Character
- Gain all the traits listed in **Core Ranger Traits**.
- Gain all level 1 features listed in the **Ranger Features Table**.

### As a Multiclass Character
- Gain **Hit Point Die**, proficiency with **Martial Weapons**, **Light and Medium Armor**, and **Shields**.
- Gain **one skill proficiency** from the Ranger skill list.
- Gain the Ranger’s level 1 features.

## 🌿 Class Features

### **Level 1: Spellcasting**
You channel nature’s essence to cast spells.

- **Prepared Spells:** Begin with 2 Level 1 spells (*Cure Wounds*, *Ensnaring Strike* recommended).  
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Wisdom  
- **Focus:** Druidic Focus (e.g., sprig of mistletoe)  
- **Changing Spells:** Replace one prepared spell after each Long Rest.  

---

### **Level 1: Favored Enemy**
You always have *Hunter’s Mark* prepared.  
You can cast it **twice per Long Rest** without using a spell slot.  
This number increases with level (see table).

---

### **Level 1: Weapon Mastery**
Choose two weapon types you’re proficient with to gain their **mastery properties** (e.g., Longbow, Shortsword).  
You may change these choices after a Long Rest.

---

### **Level 2: Deft Explorer**
Your travels have hardened and taught you well.

- **Expertise:** Gain Expertise in one skill you’re proficient with.  
- **Languages:** Learn two new languages.

---

### **Level 2: Fighting Style**
Gain one **Fighting Style feat** (see Chapter 5).  
> **Optional:** *Druidic Warrior* – learn two Druid cantrips (*Guidance*, *Starry Wisp* recommended), which count as Ranger spells.

---

### **Level 3: Ranger Subclass**
Choose a Ranger subclass:
- **Beast Master** – primal bond with an animal companion  
- **Fey Wanderer** – blessed by the magic of the Feywild  
- **Gloom Stalker** – ambusher from the Shadowfell  
- **Hunter** – master predator of the natural world

---

### **Level 4: Ability Score Improvement**
Gain an **Ability Score Improvement feat** or another feat of your choice.  
Gained again at levels 8, 12, and 16.

---

### **Level 5: Extra Attack**
You can attack **twice** whenever you take the **Attack Action**.

---

### **Level 6: Roving**
You gain a **+10 ft Speed bonus** while not wearing Heavy Armor.  
Gain **Climb** and **Swim Speeds** equal to your walking Speed.

---

### **Level 9: Expertise**
Gain Expertise in **two more skills** of your choice.

---

### **Level 10: Tireless**
You’re sustained by primal endurance.

- **Temporary HP:** As a **Magic Action**, gain `1d8 + WIS mod` temporary HP.  
  Uses = Wisdom modifier (min 1), regain after Long Rest.  
- **Recover From Exhaustion:** Reduce your Exhaustion level by 1 after each Short Rest.

---

### **Level 13: Relentless Hunter**
You cannot lose Concentration on *Hunter’s Mark* from taking damage.

---

### **Level 14: Nature’s Veil**
As a **Bonus Action**, become **Invisible** until the end of your next turn.  
Uses = Wisdom modifier (min 1), regain on Long Rest.

---

### **Level 17: Precise Hunter**
You have **Advantage** on attacks against the creature marked by *Hunter’s Mark*.

---

### **Level 18: Feral Senses**
Gain **Blindsight (30 ft)** through your connection to nature.

---

### **Level 19: Epic Boon**
Gain an **Epic Boon feat** (recommended: *Boon of Dimensional Travel*).

---

### **Level 20: Foe Slayer**
Your *Hunter’s Mark* damage die increases from **d6 → d10**.

## 🐾 Ranger Subclasses

---

### **Beast Master**
> *“My companion and I are one with the wild.”*

#### **Level 3: Primal Companion**
Summon a **Primal Beast** — a magical manifestation of nature’s bond.  
Choose: *Beast of the Land*, *Sea*, or *Sky*.

- Acts on your turn (takes **Dodge** unless commanded as Bonus Action).  
- You may forgo one attack to command the beast to take its **Beast’s Strike**.  
- If you die, the beast vanishes.  
- **Restore:** Use a spell slot and Magic Action within 1 hour of its death to revive it after 1 minute.  
- **Resummon:** Summon a new beast after each Long Rest.

#### **Level 7: Exceptional Training**
When you use a **Bonus Action** to command the beast, it can also take **Dash, Disengage, Dodge, or Help**.  
Its attacks may deal **Force damage** instead of normal type.

#### **Level 11: Bestial Fury**
Your beast’s **Beast’s Strike** can attack **twice**.  
The first time each turn it hits a creature marked by your *Hunter’s Mark*, it deals **extra Force damage** equal to that spell’s bonus.

#### **Level 15: Share Spells**
When you cast a spell that targets yourself, your beast is also affected if within 30 ft.

---

### **Fey Wanderer**
> *“Joy and dread dance hand in hand within me.”*

Blessed (or cursed) by the Feywild, you wield both beauty and terror with grace.

#### **Level 3: Dreadful Strikes**
When you hit with a weapon, deal **+1d4 Psychic damage** once per turn (increases to **1d6** at level 11).

#### **Level 3: Fey Wanderer Spells**
| **Ranger Level** | **Spells** |
|-------------------|------------|
| 3 | *Charm Person* |
| 5 | *Misty Step* |
| 9 | *Summon Fey* |
| 13 | *Dimension Door* |
| 17 | *Mislead* |

#### **Level 3: Otherworldly Glamour**
Add **Wisdom modifier** to all **Charisma checks** (min +1).  
Gain proficiency in **Deception**, **Performance**, or **Persuasion**.

#### **Level 7: Beguiling Twist**
You have Advantage on saves vs *Charmed* or *Frightened*.  
When any creature within 120 ft succeeds such a save, you may use a **Reaction** to force another creature within 120 ft to make a **WIS save** or be *Charmed* or *Frightened* (your choice) for 1 minute.

#### **Level 11: Fey Reinforcements**
You can cast *Summon Fey* without material components or slots once per Long Rest.  
Optionally remove Concentration; if you do, duration = 1 minute.

#### **Level 15: Misty Wanderer**
You can cast *Misty Step* a number of times equal to **Wisdom modifier** per Long Rest, and may bring one willing creature within 5 ft to your destination.

---

### **Gloom Stalker**
> *“The darkness is my ally.”*

#### **Level 3: Dread Ambusher**
- **Ambusher’s Leap:** +10 ft Speed at the start of combat (first turn only).  
- **Dreadful Strike:** Once per turn, add **2d6 Psychic damage** (uses = Wisdom modifier).  
- **Initiative Bonus:** Add **Wisdom modifier** to Initiative rolls.

#### **Level 3: Gloom Stalker Spells**
| **Ranger Level** | **Spells** |
|-------------------|------------|
| 3 | *Disguise Self* |
| 5 | *Rope Trick* |
| 9 | *Fear* |
| 13 | *Greater Invisibility* |
| 17 | *Seeming* |

#### **Level 3: Umbral Sight**
Gain **Darkvision (60 ft)** (or +60 ft if already have it).  
While in total darkness, you are **Invisible** to creatures relying on Darkvision.

#### **Level 7: Iron Mind**
Gain proficiency in **Wisdom saving throws** (or INT/CHA if already proficient).

#### **Level 11: Stalker’s Flurry**
Dreadful Strike damage increases to **2d8**.  
Choose one per use:
- **Sudden Strike:** Make another attack against a different creature within 5 ft.  
- **Mass Fear:** Force all within 10 ft of target to WIS save or become *Frightened*.

#### **Level 15: Shadowy Dodge**
When attacked, use **Reaction** to impose **Disadvantage** and teleport up to 30 ft to a visible space.

---

### **Hunter**
> *“Nature’s wrath, my weapon.”*

#### **Level 3: Hunter’s Lore**
While a creature is affected by *Hunter’s Mark*, you know all its **Immunities, Resistances, and Vulnerabilities**.

#### **Level 3: Hunter’s Prey**
Choose one option (change after a Short or Long Rest):

- **Colossus Slayer:** Deal +1d8 damage to wounded foes (once per turn).  
- **Horde Breaker:** Once per turn, attack a second creature within 5 ft of the first target.

#### **Level 7: Defensive Tactics**
Choose one (change after a Rest):

- **Escape the Horde:** Opportunity Attacks have Disadvantage against you.  
- **Multiattack Defense:** When hit, that attacker has Disadvantage on further attacks this turn.

#### **Level 11: Superior Hunter’s Prey**
Once per turn when dealing *Hunter’s Mark* damage, also deal it to another visible creature within 30 ft.

#### **Level 15: Superior Hunter’s Defense**
When taking damage, use **Reaction** to gain **Resistance** to that type until turn’s end.

# 🌿 Summary

> **Core Identity:** Primal warrior attuned to nature’s magic and survival instincts  
> **Primary Role:** Scout, skirmisher, adaptable striker  
> **Complexity:** Moderate  
> **Synergy With:** Druids, Rogues, Monks, Barbarians
