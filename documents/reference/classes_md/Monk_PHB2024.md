# 🥋 The Monk
> **“Perfection of body leads to harmony of soul.”**

Monks use rigorous combat training and mental discipline to align themselves with the universe and master their inner power — a force that can manifest as breath, spirit, life energy, or essence.  
Through this focus, Monks perform supernatural feats of agility, defense, and martial precision, turning even the simplest weapon into an extension of their will.

---

## 🧘 Core Monk Traits

| **Trait** | **Details** |
|------------|-------------|
| **Primary Abilities** | Dexterity, Wisdom |
| **Hit Point Die** | 1d8 per Monk level |
| **Saving Throws** | Strength, Dexterity |
| **Skill Proficiencies** | Choose 2: Acrobatics, Athletics, History, Insight, Religion, Stealth |
| **Weapon Proficiencies** | Simple weapons; Martial weapons with the **Light** property |
| **Tool Proficiencies** | Choose one: Artisan’s Tools or a Musical Instrument |
| **Armor Training** | None |
| **Starting Equipment** | Choose:<br>**A)** Spear, 2 Daggers, chosen Tool or Instrument, Explorer’s Pack, 11 GP<br>**B)** 50 GP |

> **Note:** Monks cultivate inner power — called *ki*, *focus*, or *essence* — which fuels their martial arts. Some train in solitude, while others seek enlightenment through experience in the chaotic world.

## 🧩 Becoming a Monk

### As a Level 1 Character
- Gain all traits from the **Core Monk Traits** table.  
- Gain the Monk’s level 1 features from the **Monk Features Table**.

### As a Multiclass Character
- Gain the **Hit Point Die** trait from Core Monk Traits.  
- Gain the Monk’s level 1 features.

## 🥊 Class Features

### **Level 1: Martial Arts**
Your training grants mastery over combat styles using unarmed strikes and Monk weapons.

**Monk Weapons:**  
- Simple melee weapons  
- Martial melee weapons with the **Light** property

You gain the following benefits while unarmored and not using a shield:
- **Bonus Unarmed Strike:** After the Attack action, you can make one Unarmed Strike as a **Bonus Action**.  
- **Martial Arts Die:** Use your Martial Arts die in place of normal weapon or unarmed damage.  
- **Dexterous Attacks:** Use **Dexterity** instead of **Strength** for attack and damage rolls with Monk weapons and unarmed strikes.  
- **Grapple/Shove:** When using Unarmed Strike to Grapple or Shove, you may use **Dexterity** instead of **Strength** for the DC.

---

### **Level 1: Unarmored Defense**
While unarmored and not wielding a shield, your AC = `10 + DEX modifier + WIS modifier`.

---

### **Level 2: Monk’s Focus**
Your training channels inner energy known as **Focus Points**, used to empower your techniques.

- **Focus Points:** Equal to your Monk level (see table).  
- **Recovery:** Regain all Focus Points after a Short or Long Rest.  
- **Save DC:** `8 + Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier`

#### **Focus Techniques**
- **Flurry of Blows:** Spend 1 Focus to make **two Unarmed Strikes** as a Bonus Action.  
- **Patient Defense:** Take the **Disengage** action as a Bonus Action, or spend 1 Focus to take **both Disengage and Dodge** as a Bonus Action.  
- **Step of the Wind:** Take the **Dash** action as a Bonus Action, or spend 1 Focus to take **Dash and Disengage** and double your jump distance.

---

### **Level 2: Uncanny Metabolism**
When you roll Initiative, you may **regain all expended Focus Points** and **heal HP** equal to your Monk level + one roll of your Martial Arts die.  
Once used, this feature recharges on a **Long Rest**.

---

### **Level 2: Unarmored Movement**
While unarmored and unshielded, your Speed increases by 10 ft (scales with level).

---

### **Level 3: Deflect Attacks**
When hit by a Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing attack, you can use a **Reaction** to reduce damage by `1d10 + DEX mod + Monk level`.  
If reduced to 0, you can spend 1 Focus Point to redirect the attack:
- Melee: Choose a creature within 5 ft.  
- Ranged: Choose a creature within 60 ft that you can see.  
The target makes a DEX save or takes damage = `2 × Martial Arts die + DEX mod`.

---

### **Level 3: Monk Subclass**
Choose your monastic path:
- **Warrior of Mercy**  
- **Warrior of Shadow**  
- **Warrior of the Elements**  
- **Warrior of the Open Hand**

---

### **Level 4: Slow Fall**
As a **Reaction** when falling, reduce fall damage by `5 × Monk level`.

---

### **Level 5: Extra Attack**
When you take the **Attack** action, you can attack **twice** instead of once.

---

### **Level 5: Stunning Strike**
Once per turn when you hit a creature with a Monk weapon or unarmed strike, you may spend 1 Focus Point to **stun** it.  
The target must make a CON save or be **Stunned until your next turn**.  
On a success, its speed is halved and the next attack against it has Advantage.

---

### **Level 6: Empowered Strikes**
Your Unarmed Strikes may deal **Force damage** instead of their normal type.

---

### **Level 7: Evasion**
When you make a DEX save for half damage, take **no damage** on a success and **half** on a failure (unless Incapacitated).

---

### **Level 9: Acrobatic Movement**
While unarmored and unshielded, you may move along walls or across liquids on your turn without falling.

---

### **Level 10: Heightened Focus**
Your Focus techniques improve:
- **Flurry of Blows:** Make **three** Unarmed Strikes instead of two.  
- **Patient Defense:** Gain Temp HP = `2 × Martial Arts die`.  
- **Step of the Wind:** Move one willing creature (Large or smaller) with you without provoking opportunity attacks.

---

### **Level 10: Self-Restoration**
At the end of each turn, remove one of the following from yourself: **Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned.**  
You also ignore exhaustion from lack of food or water.

---

### **Level 13: Deflect Energy**
You can now use **Deflect Attacks** against *any* damage type.

---

### **Level 14: Disciplined Survivor**
Gain **proficiency in all saving throws**.  
If you fail a save, you may spend 1 Focus Point to reroll it.

---

### **Level 15: Perfect Focus**
When rolling Initiative and not using Uncanny Metabolism, regain Focus Points until you have at least 4.

---

### **Level 18: Superior Defense**
As a Bonus Action, spend 3 Focus Points to gain **Resistance to all damage except Force** for 1 minute (ends if Incapacitated).

---

### **Level 19: Epic Boon**
Gain an **Epic Boon feat** or another feat of your choice.  
> **Recommended:** Boon of Irresistible Offense.

---

### **Level 20: Body and Mind**
Your mastery reaches divine perfection.  
Increase **DEX** and **WIS** by 4 each (maximum 25).

## 🕯️ Monk Subclasses

---

### **Warrior of Mercy**
> *“To heal is mercy. To end suffering is mercy.”*

Manipulate the life force of others, balancing restoration and retribution.

#### **Level 3: Hand of Harm**
Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike, you may spend 1 Focus Point to deal extra **Necrotic damage** = `Martial Arts die + WIS mod`.

#### **Level 3: Hand of Healing**
As a Magic Action, spend 1 Focus to heal a creature for `Martial Arts die + WIS mod`.  
When you use *Flurry of Blows*, replace one attack with a *Hand of Healing* without spending Focus.

#### **Level 3: Implements of Mercy**
Gain proficiency in **Insight**, **Medicine**, and **Herbalism Kit**.

#### **Level 6: Physician’s Touch**
Your hands heal and harm more potently:  
- **Hand of Harm:** Also inflicts *Poisoned* until your next turn.  
- **Hand of Healing:** Can remove one condition: *Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, or Stunned.*

#### **Level 11: Flurry of Healing and Harm**
When using *Flurry of Blows*, you can replace each attack with *Hand of Healing* without spending Focus.  
When dealing damage, you may apply *Hand of Harm* without cost (once per turn).  
Uses = Wisdom modifier per Long Rest.

#### **Level 17: Hand of Ultimate Mercy**
As a Magic Action, touch a corpse dead within 24 hours, spending 5 Focus Points.  
The creature returns to life with `4d10 + WIS mod` HP and without negative conditions.  
Once per Long Rest.

---

### **Warrior of Shadow**
> *“From shadow, I strike unseen.”*

Masters of stealth and deception, harnessing the gloom of the Shadowfell.

#### **Level 3: Shadow Arts**
Gain the ability to manipulate darkness.
- **Darkness:** Spend 1 Focus Point to cast *Darkness*. You can see through it and move its area up to 60 ft each turn.  
- **Darkvision:** Gain 60 ft (or +60 ft if you already have it).  
- **Minor Illusion:** Learn the *Minor Illusion* cantrip (WIS is your spellcasting ability).

#### **Level 6: Shadow Step**
While in Dim Light or Darkness, use a **Bonus Action** to teleport up to 60 ft to another dimly lit space.  
Your next melee attack before the end of the turn has Advantage.

#### **Level 11: Improved Shadow Step**
You may spend 1 Focus Point to teleport regardless of light conditions.  
After teleporting, make one Unarmed Strike as part of the Bonus Action.

#### **Level 17: Cloak of Shadows**
As a Magic Action in Dim Light or Darkness, spend 3 Focus Points to shroud yourself in shadow for 1 minute.  
While shrouded:
- Gain **Invisibility**  
- Move through occupied spaces (Difficult Terrain)  
- Use *Flurry of Blows* without spending Focus  

Ends early if you enter Bright Light.

---

### **Warrior of the Elements**
> *“The elements are my fists; the storm, my spirit.”*

Harness elemental energy to empower attacks and movement.

#### **Level 3: Elemental Attunement**
As a Bonus Action, spend 1 Focus Point to gain elemental energy for 10 minutes:  
- **Reach:** Unarmed Strike reach +10 ft.  
- **Elemental Strikes:** Change damage type to **Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder**.  
  On hit, target must make a STR save or be moved 10 ft.

#### **Level 3: Manipulate Elements**
You learn the *Elementalism* cantrip (WIS is your spellcasting ability).

#### **Level 6: Elemental Burst**
As a Magic Action, spend 2 Focus Points to create a 20 ft sphere within 120 ft.  
Each creature makes a DEX save or takes `3 × Martial Arts die` of your chosen element (half on success).

#### **Level 11: Stride of the Elements**
While Elemental Attunement is active, you gain **Fly Speed** and **Swim Speed** equal to your Speed.

#### **Level 17: Elemental Epitome**
While attuned, gain:
- **Damage Resistance:** Choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder (change each turn).  
- **Destructive Stride:** When using *Step of the Wind*, Speed +20 ft; creatures within 5 ft take 1 Martial Arts die of elemental damage (once per turn).  
- **Empowered Strikes:** Once per turn, deal extra damage = 1 Martial Arts die of your elemental type.

---

### **Warrior of the Open Hand**
> *“My body is the weapon, and peace my purpose.”*

Perfection of body, mind, and technique.

#### **Level 3: Open Hand Technique**
When you hit a creature with *Flurry of Blows*, choose one:
- **Addle:** Target can’t make Opportunity Attacks until next turn.  
- **Push:** Target must succeed a STR save or be pushed 15 ft.  
- **Topple:** Target must succeed a DEX save or fall Prone.

#### **Level 6: Wholeness of Body**
As a Bonus Action, heal yourself for `Martial Arts die + WIS mod`.  
Uses = Wisdom modifier per Long Rest.

#### **Level 11: Fleet Step**
After taking a Bonus Action (other than *Step of the Wind*), you may immediately use *Step of the Wind*.

#### **Level 17: Quivering Palm**
When you hit a creature, spend 4 Focus Points to set up lethal vibrations lasting days = Monk level.  
As an Action, you can end the vibrations, instantly killing the target if on the same plane.

# 🧘 Summary

> **Core Identity:** Martial artist channeling inner energy through motion and discipline  
> **Primary Role:** Agile striker and control specialist  
> **Complexity:** High  
> **Synergy With:** Rogues, Rangers, and Clerics who complement mobility and wisdom-based play
