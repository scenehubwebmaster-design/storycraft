# Species Descriptions

Ten species are presented in this section in alphabetical order: **Aasimar**, **Dragonborn**, **Dwarf**, **Elf**, **Gnome**, **Goliath**, **Halfling**, **Human**, **Orc**, and **Tiefling**.

---

## Aasimar

Aasimar (pronounced _AH-sih-mar_) are mortals who carry a spark of the Upper Planes within their souls. Whether descended from an angelic being or infused with celestial power, they can fan that spark to bring light, healing, and heavenly fury.

Aasimar can arise among any population of mortals. They resemble their parents but live up to 160 years and have features that hint at their celestial heritage—metallic freckles, luminous eyes, a halo, or angelic skin tones such as silver, opalescent green, or coppery red. These features start subtle and become obvious when the aasimar learns to reveal their full celestial nature.

### Aasimar Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium (4–7 ft) or Small (2–4 ft), chosen when selected  
**Speed:** 30 ft

#### Special Traits

- **Celestial Resistance.** You have resistance to _necrotic_ and _radiant_ damage.
- **Darkvision.** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 ft.
- **Healing Hands.** As a Magic action, touch a creature and roll a number of d4s equal to your proficiency bonus. The creature regains hit points equal to the total rolled. Once used, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.
- **Light Bearer.** You know the _Light_ cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.
- **Celestial Revelation.** Starting at level 3, as a bonus action you can transform using one of the options below. The transformation lasts 1 minute or until you end it. Once you transform, you can’t do so again until you finish a long rest.

> **Revelation Options**
>
> - **Heavenly Wings.** Spectral wings sprout from your back. You gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed.
> - **Inner Radiance.** Light radiates from your eyes and mouth. You shed bright light in a 10-ft radius and dim light for another 10 ft. Each creature within 10 ft at the end of your turn takes radiant damage equal to your proficiency bonus.
> - **Necrotic Shroud.** Your eyes become pools of darkness and flightless wings sprout from your back. Creatures within 10 ft must succeed on a Charisma saving throw (DC = 8 + your Charisma modifier + proficiency bonus) or be frightened until the end of your next turn.

## Dragonborn

The ancestors of dragonborn hatched from the eggs of chromatic and metallic dragons, possibly blessed by Bahamut and Tiamat. Whether divinely or naturally created, dragonborn have made homes across the Material Plane.

They resemble wingless, bipedal dragons with scales, horns, and bright eyes. Their coloration and features reflect their draconic ancestry.

### Dragonborn Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium (5–7 ft)  
**Speed:** 30 ft

#### Special Traits

- **Draconic Ancestry.** Choose a dragon type from the Draconic Ancestors table.
- **Breath Weapon.** Replace one attack with an exhalation of energy (15-ft cone or 30-ft line). Creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + Constitution modifier + proficiency bonus). On a failed save, they take `1d10` damage of your ancestry’s type, or half on success. Damage increases at levels 5 (2d10), 11 (3d10), and 17 (4d10).
- **Damage Resistance.** You have resistance to your ancestry’s damage type.
- **Darkvision.** 60 ft.
- **Draconic Flight.** At level 5, you can sprout spectral wings for 10 minutes, gaining a fly speed equal to your speed. Once per long rest.

## Dwarf

Dwarves were raised from the earth by a forge deity such as **Moradin** or **Reorx**, granting them affinity with stone and metal and long life spans (about 350 years). They are sturdy, bearded folk known for craftsmanship and valor.

### Dwarf Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium (4–5 ft)  
**Speed:** 30 ft

#### Special Traits

- **Darkvision.** 120 ft.
- **Dwarven Resilience.** Resistance to _poison_ damage and advantage on saves against the _poisoned_ condition.
- **Dwarven Toughness.** Hit point maximum increases by 1 per level.
- **Stonecunning.** As a bonus action, gain tremorsense (60 ft) for 10 minutes while touching stone. Uses = proficiency bonus per long rest.

## Elf

Created by the god **Corellon**, elves once could shapeshift freely until cursed for aiding Lolth. Though their forms are now fixed, they are long-lived (up to 750 years), graceful, and connected to the Feywild.

### Elf Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium (5–6 ft)  
**Speed:** 30 ft

#### Special Traits

- **Darkvision.** 60 ft.
- **Elven Lineage.** Choose a lineage from the table below; grants spells and features at higher levels.
- **Fey Ancestry.** Advantage on saves vs. _charmed_.
- **Keen Senses.** Proficiency in _Insight_, _Perception_, or _Survival_.
- **Trance.** Meditate for 4 hours instead of sleeping; remain conscious.

## Gnome

Gnomes were created by gods of invention, illusion, and underground life. They are small, clever, and live around 425 years, known for curiosity and tinkering.

### Gnome Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Small (3–4 ft)  
**Speed:** 30 ft

#### Special Traits

- **Darkvision.** 60 ft.
- **Gnomish Cunning.** Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.
- **Gnomish Lineage.** Choose one:
  - **Forest Gnome.** Know _Minor Illusion_ cantrip; can cast _Speak with Animals_ PB times per long rest.
  - **Rock Gnome.** Know _Mending_ and _Prestidigitation_. Can craft a Tiny clockwork device (AC 5, 1 HP) producing one chosen _Prestidigitation_ effect. It lasts 8 hours and you can maintain up to 3 devices.

## Goliath

Descendants of giants, goliaths bear supernatural boons and can momentarily grow in size, embodying their giant kin’s might.

### Goliath Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium (7–8 ft)  
**Speed:** 35 ft

#### Special Traits

- **Giant Ancestry.** Choose one benefit (PB uses/long rest):
  - **Cloud’s Jaunt (Cloud Giant):** Teleport 30 ft as bonus action.
  - **Fire’s Burn (Fire Giant):** Add `1d10` fire damage to a hit.
  - **Frost’s Chill (Frost Giant):** Add `1d6` cold damage and reduce target speed by 10 ft.
  - **Hill’s Tumble (Hill Giant):** Knock prone on hit vs. Large or smaller.
  - **Stone’s Endurance (Stone Giant):** As reaction, reduce damage by `1d12 + CON`.
  - **Storm’s Thunder (Storm Giant):** As reaction, deal `1d8` thunder damage to attacker.
- **Large Form.** At level 5, become Large for 10 minutes; advantage on Strength checks and +10 ft speed.
- **Powerful Build.** Advantage on saves to end _grappled_; count as one size larger for carrying capacity.

## Halfling

Cherished by gods of home and hearth, halflings are small (2–3 ft), lucky, and community-oriented, often adventurous and cheerful. They live about 150 years.

### Halfling Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Small (2–3 ft)  
**Speed:** 30 ft

#### Special Traits

- **Brave.** Advantage on saves vs. _frightened_.
- **Halfling Nimbleness.** Move through spaces of larger creatures.
- **Lucky.** When you roll a 1 on a d20 test, reroll and use the new result.
- **Naturally Stealthy.** You can hide when obscured by a creature at least one size larger.

## Human

Found throughout the multiverse, humans are ambitious, adaptable, and diverse in appearance and culture.

### Human Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium (4–7 ft) or Small (2–4 ft)  
**Speed:** 30 ft

#### Special Traits

- **Resourceful.** Gain _Heroic Inspiration_ when finishing a long rest.
- **Skillful.** Gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.
- **Versatile.** Gain one origin feat of your choice (_Skilled_ recommended).

## Orc

Created by the god **Gruumsh**, orcs are hardy, determined wanderers of plains, caverns, and seas. They are broad, gray-skinned, and strong.

### Orc Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium (6–7 ft)  
**Speed:** 30 ft

#### Special Traits

- **Adrenaline Rush.** As a bonus action, Dash and gain temporary hit points equal to proficiency bonus. Uses = PB per short/long rest.
- **Darkvision.** 120 ft.
- **Relentless Endurance.** When reduced to 0 HP (but not killed outright), drop to 1 HP instead. Once per long rest.

## Tiefling

Tieflings are mortals linked to the Lower Planes, often through fiendish ancestry. Their legacy may be infernal, abyssal, or chthonic.

### Tiefling Traits
