# Character Origins

Each part of your character’s origin reflects facets of their life and the circumstances that set them on the path to adventure.

> **Note:** If you choose a background or species from an older book, see the sidebar **“Backgrounds and Species from Older Books”** in chapter 2 for guidance on integrating them with these updated options.

---

## Origin Components

### Character Backgrounds

Your character’s **background** represents the place and occupation that most shaped them before their adventuring life began.  
Each background includes a narrative suggestion — feel free to alter it to fit your story.

### Parts of a Background

- **Ability Scores.** A background lists three ability scores. Increase one by +2 and another by +1, or all three by +1. None of these increases can raise a score above 20.
- **Feat.** Each background grants one specified _Origin feat_ (see Chapter 5).
- **Skill Proficiencies.** You gain proficiency in two specified skills.
- **Tool Proficiency.** Each background grants proficiency with one tool — either a specific one or a choice from the _Artisan’s Tools_ category (see Chapter 6).
- **Equipment.** Each background offers a choice between an equipment package or 50 GP.

---

### Character Species

When choosing your character’s species, determine whether you’re a **human** or a member of a fantastical species such as **dragonborn** or **gnome**.

The peoples of the D&D multiverse hail from diverse worlds and include many forms of sapient life.  
A character’s species defines the set of traits granted by their physiology or magical heritage.

> **Note:** Members of most species live about 80 years, though many have unique lifespans described in their respective entries.

### Parts of a Species

- **Creature Type.** Defines the character’s type (all species in this chapter are _Humanoid_).
- **Size.** Determines whether the character is _Small_ or _Medium_, depending on the species.
- **Speed.** Defines the base movement speed.
- **Special Traits.** Unique features granted by species physiology or magical nature.

> _“You devoted yourself to service in a temple, performing rites in honor of the gods. Through devotion and study, you’ve learned to channel divine power in service of your faith.”_

**Ability Scores:** Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma
