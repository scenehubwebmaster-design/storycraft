# ✨ The Paladin
> **“By oath and light, I stand against the darkness.”**

Paladins are united by sacred oaths to stand against corruption and annihilation. Whether sworn before a god’s altar, beneath the open sky, or in the solitude of grief, a Paladin’s oath binds them to a higher cause.  
Through divine magic and martial skill, Paladins heal the wounded, smite evil, and protect the innocent — embodying the will of the divine through devotion and courage.

> **Note:** A Paladin’s oath is a sacred bond — a covenant between their soul and their purpose. From knightly paragons to divine avengers, every Paladin channels faith into action.

## 🕊️ Becoming a Paladin

### As a Level 1 Character
- Gain all the traits in the **Core Paladin Traits** table.  
- Gain the Paladin’s level 1 features listed in the **Paladin Features Table**.

### As a Multiclass Character
- Gain **Hit Point Die**, proficiency with **Martial Weapons**, and training with **Light and Medium Armor** and **Shields**.  
- Gain the Paladin’s level 1 features.

## ⚔️ Class Features

### **Level 1: Lay on Hands**
Your divine touch can heal wounds.  
You have a **pool of healing** equal to `5 × your Paladin level`, replenished after a **Long Rest**.

- **Bonus Action:** Touch a creature to restore HP up to your remaining pool.  
- **Cleansing:** Spend 5 HP from the pool to remove the *Poisoned* condition.  

> **Example:** A Paladin kneels over a fallen ally, light blooming from their hands as wounds knit closed.

---

### **Level 1: Spellcasting**
You channel divine energy into miracles and blessings.

- **Spell Slots:** See table above.  
- **Prepared Spells:** Begin with 2 Level 1 Paladin spells (recommended: *Heroism*, *Searing Smite*).  
- **Preparation:** Change prepared spells after a Long Rest.  
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Charisma  
- **Spell Focus:** Holy Symbol  

---

### **Level 1: Weapon Mastery**
You gain mastery over two weapons of your choice with which you are proficient.  
You may change these choices after a Long Rest.

---

### **Level 2: Fighting Style**
Gain one **Fighting Style feat** (see Chapter 5).  
> **Optional:** *Blessed Warrior* – learn two Cleric cantrips (e.g., *Guidance*, *Sacred Flame*). These count as Paladin spells using Charisma.

---

### **Level 2: Paladin’s Smite**
You always have the *Divine Smite* spell prepared.  
You can cast it **once without using a spell slot** per Long Rest.

---

### **Level 3: Channel Divinity**
You can channel divine power through sacred rites.

- **Uses:** 2 (regain 1 on Short Rest, all on Long Rest; +1 use at Level 11)  
- **Save DC:** Same as Spell Save DC  

#### **Divine Sense**
As a **Bonus Action**, detect **Celestials, Fiends, and Undead** within 60 ft for 10 minutes.  
You also sense consecrated or desecrated places or objects (as *Hallow*).

---

### **Level 3: Paladin Subclass**
Choose your sacred oath:
- **Oath of Devotion**  
- **Oath of Glory**  
- **Oath of the Ancients**  
- **Oath of Vengeance**

---

### **Level 4: Ability Score Improvement**
You gain the **Ability Score Improvement** feat or another feat of your choice.  
Gained again at levels 8, 12, and 16.

---

### **Level 5: Extra Attack**
You can attack **twice** whenever you take the **Attack Action**.

---

### **Level 5: Faithful Steed**
You always have *Find Steed* prepared.  
You may cast it **once for free** per Long Rest.

---

### **Level 6: Aura of Protection**
You and allies within 10 ft gain a bonus to **saving throws** equal to your **Charisma modifier** (min +1).  
Inactive while you are **Incapacitated**.

---

### **Level 9: Abjure Foes**
As a **Magic Action**, expend a Channel Divinity use to present your holy symbol and awe your foes.

- Affect creatures within 60 ft equal to your CHA modifier (min 1).  
- Each must succeed on a **WIS save** or become **Frightened** for 1 minute.  
- While Frightened, targets can only move, take an action, or take a bonus action.  

---

### **Level 10: Aura of Courage**
You and allies in your **Aura of Protection** gain **Immunity to the Frightened condition**.

---

### **Level 11: Radiant Strikes**
Your melee weapon and unarmed strikes deal an additional **1d8 Radiant damage**.

---

### **Level 14: Restoring Touch**
When you use *Lay on Hands*, you may also remove any of the following conditions:  
*Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, or Stunned.*  
Costs 5 HP per condition removed.

---

### **Level 18: Aura Expansion**
Your **Aura of Protection** expands to **30 ft**.

---

### **Level 19: Epic Boon**
Gain an **Epic Boon feat** (recommended: *Boon of Truesight*).

## 🕊️ Paladin Subclasses (Sacred Oaths)

> Each oath embodies a divine philosophy that guides a Paladin’s actions and grants them holy powers.

---

### **Oath of Devotion**
> *“Uphold justice, order, and the sanctity of the divine light.”*

#### **Oath of Devotion Spells**
| **Level** | **Spells** |
|------------|------------|
| 3 | *Protection from Evil and Good, Shield of Faith* |
| 5 | *Aid, Zone of Truth* |
| 9 | *Beacon of Hope, Dispel Magic* |
| 13 | *Freedom of Movement, Guardian of Faith* |
| 17 | *Commune, Flame Strike* |

#### **Level 3: Sacred Weapon**
As an **Action**, expend a Channel Divinity use to imbue a melee weapon for 10 minutes:
- Add CHA modifier (min +1) to attack rolls.  
- Change damage type to Radiant.  
- Weapon glows (Bright Light 20 ft, Dim Light 20 ft beyond).

#### **Level 7: Aura of Devotion**
You and allies in your aura are **Immune to the Charmed condition**.

#### **Level 15: Smite of Protection**
When you cast *Divine Smite*, you and allies gain **Half Cover** within your aura until your next turn.

#### **Level 20: Holy Nimbus**
Bonus Action: empower your aura for 10 minutes:
- Gain **Advantage** on saves forced by Fiends or Undead.  
- Enemies starting their turn in aura take `CHA mod + Proficiency Bonus` Radiant damage.  
- Area counts as **sunlight**.  
Recharge on Long Rest or by expending a 5th-level slot.

---

### **Oath of Glory**
> *“Deeds immortalize the brave.”*

#### **Oath of Glory Spells**
| **Level** | **Spells** |
|------------|------------|
| 3 | *Guiding Bolt, Heroism* |
| 5 | *Enhance Ability, Magic Weapon* |
| 9 | *Haste, Protection from Energy* |
| 13 | *Compulsion, Freedom of Movement* |
| 17 | *Legend Lore, Yolande’s Regal Presence* |

#### **Level 3: Inspiring Smite**
After casting *Divine Smite*, expend a Channel Divinity use to grant **Temporary HP** = `2d8 + Paladin level`, distributed among allies within 30 ft.

#### **Level 3: Peerless Athlete**
Bonus Action: expend Channel Divinity for 1 hour to gain:
- Advantage on Athletics & Acrobatics checks  
- +10 ft to jump distances  

#### **Level 7: Aura of Alacrity**
Your speed increases by 10 ft.  
Allies entering or starting their turn in your aura also gain +10 ft Speed for 1 turn.

#### **Level 15: Glorious Defense**
When an ally within 10 ft is hit, use your **Reaction** to add CHA mod to their AC.  
If the attack misses, you may make a weapon attack against the attacker.  
Uses = CHA mod per Long Rest.

#### **Level 20: Living Legend**
Bonus Action: become a **living legend** for 10 minutes:
- **Charismatic Presence:** Advantage on Charisma checks.  
- **Saving Throw Reroll:** Reroll a failed save (Reaction).  
- **Unerring Strike:** Once per turn, turn a missed attack into a hit.  
Recharge: Long Rest or by expending a 5th-level slot.

---

### **Oath of the Ancients**
> *“Preserve life, light, and joy wherever they bloom.”*

#### **Oath of the Ancients Spells**
| **Level** | **Spells** |
|------------|------------|
| 3 | *Ensnaring Strike, Speak with Animals* |
| 5 | *Misty Step, Moonbeam* |
| 9 | *Plant Growth, Protection from Energy* |
| 13 | *Ice Storm, Stoneskin* |
| 17 | *Commune with Nature, Tree Stride* |

#### **Level 3: Nature’s Wrath**
Magic Action: expend Channel Divinity to restrain enemies with spectral vines (15 ft radius, STR save negates).

#### **Level 7: Aura of Warding**
You and allies in your aura gain **Resistance to Necrotic, Psychic, and Radiant** damage.

#### **Level 15: Undying Sentinel**
When reduced to 0 HP (not killed outright), drop to 1 HP instead and heal `3 × Paladin level`.  
Once per Long Rest.  
You also cease aging and cannot be aged magically.

#### **Level 20: Elder Champion**
Bonus Action: imbue your aura with primal power for 1 minute:
- **Diminish Defiance:** Enemies have Disadvantage on saves vs your spells and Channel Divinity.  
- **Regeneration:** Regain 10 HP at start of each turn.  
- **Swift Spells:** Cast Action spells as Bonus Actions.  
Recharge: Long Rest or expend a 5th-level slot.

---

### **Oath of Vengeance**
> *“The guilty shall not escape divine justice.”*

#### **Oath of Vengeance Spells**
| **Level** | **Spells** |
|------------|------------|
| 3 | *Bane, Hunter’s Mark* |
| 5 | *Hold Person, Misty Step* |
| 9 | *Haste, Protection from Energy* |
| 13 | *Banishment, Dimension Door* |
| 17 | *Hold Monster, Scrying* |

#### **Level 3: Vow of Enmity**
When you take the **Attack Action**, expend Channel Divinity to target one creature within 30 ft.  
You gain **Advantage on attacks** against it for 1 minute.  
If it dies, you may transfer the vow to another target within 30 ft (no action).

#### **Level 7: Relentless Avenger**
When you hit a creature with an **Opportunity Attack**, reduce its Speed to 0 and move up to half your Speed without provoking attacks.

#### **Level 15: Soul of Vengeance**
When a creature under your Vow attacks, you may use your **Reaction** to make a melee attack against it.

#### **Level 20: Avenging Angel**
Bonus Action: assume an angelic form for 10 minutes:
- **Flight:** Spectral wings (Fly Speed 60 ft, hover).  
- **Frightful Aura:** Enemies in your aura must make a WIS save or be **Frightened** for 1 minute.  
  Attacks against Frightened creatures have Advantage.  
Recharge: Long Rest or by expending a 5th-level slot.

# 🕊️ Summary

> **Core Identity:** Holy warrior empowered by sacred oath and divine will  
> **Primary Role:** Frontline smiter and protector  
> **Complexity:** Moderate  
> **Synergy With:** Clerics, Fighters, Bards, and Sorcerers (Charisma synergy)
