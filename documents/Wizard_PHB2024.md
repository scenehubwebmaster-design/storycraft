# 🧙‍♂️ **The Wizard — Master of Arcane Knowledge**

---

## 🪶 Core Wizard Traits

| **Trait** | **Description** |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Primary Ability** | Intelligence |
| **Hit Point Die** | `d6` per Wizard level |
| **Saving Throws** | Intelligence and Wisdom |
| **Skill Proficiencies** | Choose 2 from Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, or Religion |
| **Weapon Proficiencies** | Simple weapons |
| **Armor Training** | None |
| **Starting Equipment** | Choose (A) 2 Daggers, Arcane Focus (Quarterstaff), Robe, Spellbook, Scholar’s Pack and 5 GP; or (B) 55 GP |

---

> **Wizards** are defined by their exhaustive study of the inner workings of magic.  
> They shape fire, lightning, deception, and transformation through methodical research and boundless curiosity.

Their spells can conjure creatures from other planes, glimpse possible futures, or reshape matter itself.  
From the legendary halls of study to the crumbling ruins of forgotten empires, Wizards relentlessly seek lost arcane knowledge to expand the boundaries of mortal understanding.

---

## ✨ **Becoming a Wizard**

### As a Level 1 Character
- Gain all features in the *Core Wizard Traits* table.  
- Gain the Wizard’s Level 1 features from the *Wizard Features* table.

### As a Multiclass Character
- Gain the Hit Point Die from the Core Wizard Traits.  
- Gain the Wizard’s Level 1 features listed in the *Wizard Features* table.  
- See chapter 2 for multiclass spell slot rules.

## 🧙‍♀️ **Level 1: Spellcasting**

Your study of arcane theory grants mastery of spellcraft.

### Cantrips
You know three Wizard cantrips of your choice.  
**Recommended:** *Light*, *Mage Hand*, *Ray of Frost*.  
Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you may replace one cantrip with another Wizard cantrip.

At levels 4 and 10, you learn an additional cantrip.

---

### Spellbook
Your wizardly apprenticeship culminated in creating a unique spellbook — a Tiny object (3 lb, 100 pages) that only you can read or someone using *Identify*.

It begins with six 1st-level Wizard spells of your choice.  
**Recommended:** *Detect Magic*, *Feather Fall*, *Mage Armor*, *Magic Missile*, *Sleep*, *Thunderwave.*

Whenever you gain a Wizard level after 1st, add two spells of your choice to your book (up to your available slot levels).

---

### Spell Slots
Your spell slots per level are shown in the table above.  
All expended slots are regained after a Long Rest.

---

### Prepared Spells of Level 1 and Higher
You prepare a number of spells from your book equal to the Prepared Spells column of the table.  
Prepared spells can be any combination of levels for which you have slots.

> **Example:** At Level 3, you may prepare 6 spells of 1st and 2nd level in any combination.

If a feature gives you spells always prepared, they don’t count against this limit.

Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you may re-prepare your list.

---

### Spellcasting Ability
**Intelligence** is your spellcasting ability.

### Spellcasting Focus
You may use an **Arcane Focus** or your **Spellbook** as your focus.

---

## 🕯️ **Level 1: Ritual Adept**
You can cast any spell as a **Ritual** if it has the ritual tag and is in your spellbook.  
It need not be prepared, but you must read from your book to cast it.

---

## 📖 **Level 1: Arcane Recovery**
Once per Short Rest, you can recover expended spell slots equal to half your Wizard level (rounded up, max 5th level).  
You cannot recover slots 6th level or higher.

> **Example:** At Level 4, you may recover two levels of slots (either two 1st or one 2nd).

---

## 🎓 **Level 2: Scholar**
Choose one skill you are proficient in (Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, or Religion).  
You gain **Expertise** in that skill (double proficiency bonus).

---

> ### 🪶 *Expanding and Replacing a Spellbook*
> - When you find a Wizard spell on a scroll or in a tome, you may copy it into your book if it’s of a level you can prepare.  
> - Copying takes **2 hours and 50 GP per spell level**.  
> - Copying from your own book to another costs **1 hour and 10 GP per level**.  
> - If you lose your book, you can recreate it from prepared spells using the same process.  
> - Many Wizards keep backup spellbooks.

---

## 🔮 **Level 3: Wizard Subclass**
Choose a specialization that defines your arcane focus.  
(*Abjurer*, *Diviner*, *Evoker*, or *Illusionist*).  
You gain its features as you reach the specified levels.

---

## 🪄 **Level 4: Ability Score Improvement**
You gain the **Ability Score Improvement** feat or another feat of your choice.  
Repeat at levels 8, 12, and 16.

---

## 📘 **Level 5: Memorize Spell**
When you finish a Short Rest, you can study your book and replace one prepared Wizard spell with another of any level you can cast.

---

## 🧠 **Level 18: Spell Mastery**
Choose one 1st-level and one 2nd-level spell in your book that have a casting time of an action.  
You always have them prepared and can cast them at will without expending a slot.  
Casting at a higher level still requires a slot.  
After a Long Rest, you may replace either spell with another of the same level.

---

## 🏆 **Level 19: Epic Boon**
Gain an **Epic Boon** feat or any feat for which you qualify.  
*Boon of Spell Recall* is recommended.

---

## 📜 **Level 20: Signature Spells**
Choose two 3rd-level spells in your book as **Signature Spells**.  
You always have them prepared and can cast each once at 3rd level without expending a slot.  
You regain this use after a Short or Long Rest.

# 🧭 **Wizard Subclasses**

> Each Wizard specializes in one school of magic — refining and reshaping how they understand the multiverse.

---

## 🛡️ **Abjurer — Shield Companions and Banish Foes**

### Level 3: Abjuration Savant
Add two free 2nd-level Abjuration spells to your spellbook.  
Each time you gain a new spell level, add one Abjuration spell for free.

### Level 3: Arcane Ward
When you cast an Abjuration spell, create a magical **ward** around yourself (HP = 2 × Wizard level + INT modifier).  
The ward absorbs damage before you do.  
Whenever you cast another Abjuration spell, the ward regains HP equal to twice the spell’s level.  
> You can recreate the ward only once per Long Rest.

### Level 6: Projected Ward
When a creature within 30 ft takes damage, use your Reaction to make your ward absorb that damage.

### Level 10: Spell Breaker
You always have *Counterspell* and *Dispel Magic* prepared.  
You can cast *Dispel Magic* as a **Bonus Action** and add your proficiency bonus to the check.  
If the spell fails to end magic, the slot isn’t expended.

### Level 14: Spell Resistance
You have **Advantage on saving throws against spells** and **Resistance to spell damage**.

---

## 🔮 **Diviner — Learn the Secrets of the Multiverse**

### Level 3: Divination Savant
Add two free 2nd-level Divination spells to your spellbook.  
Each time you gain a new spell level, add one Divination spell for free.

### Level 3: Portent
After each Long Rest, roll **two d20s**.  
You may replace any d20 test made by you or a visible creature with one of your Portent rolls (once per turn).

### Level 6: Expert Divination
When you cast a Divination spell using a 2nd-level or higher slot, regain one expended spell slot of lower level (max 5th).

### Level 10: The Third Eye
As a Bonus Action, gain one of the following until your next rest:
- **Darkvision** (120 ft)  
- **Greater Comprehension** (read any language)  
- **See Invisibility** (without a slot)

### Level 14: Greater Portent
Roll **three d20s** for Portent instead of two.

---

## ⚡ **Evoker — Create Explosive Elemental Effects**

### Level 3: Evocation Savant
Add two free 2nd-level Evocation spells to your spellbook.  
Add one free Evocation spell whenever you unlock a new spell level.

### Level 3: Potent Cantrip
Your damaging cantrips deal **half damage** even on a miss or successful save.

### Level 6: Sculpt Spells
When you cast an Evocation spell that affects allies, you may choose up to **1 + the spell’s level** of them to automatically succeed on saves and take no damage.

### Level 10: Empowered Evocation
Add your **Intelligence modifier** to one damage roll of any Wizard Evocation spell you cast.

### Level 14: Overchannel
Once per Long Rest, you may **maximize damage** for a Wizard spell of 1st–5th level.  
Using this feature again before resting deals **2d12 necrotic damage per spell level**.  
Each additional use increases the damage by `1d12` per level.

---

## 🌀 **Illusionist — Weave Subtle Spells of Deception**

### Level 3: Illusion Savant
Add two free 2nd-level Illusion spells to your spellbook.  
Each time you gain a new spell level, add one Illusion spell for free.

### Level 3: Improved Illusions
You can cast Illusion spells without Verbal components.  
If an Illusion spell has a range ≥ 10 ft, increase it by 60 ft.  
You also learn *Minor Illusion* (or another cantrip if you already know it).  
You can create **both image and sound** with it, and cast it as a **Bonus Action**.

### Level 6: Phantasmal Creatures
You always have *Summon Beast* and *Summon Fey* prepared.  
You can cast spectral Illusion versions without slots (half HP).  
Once per Long Rest, you can do so for each spell.

### Level 10: Illusory Self
When hit by an attack, use a Reaction to create a duplicate — the attack misses automatically.  
Recharge: Short/Long Rest or by expending a 2nd-level+ slot.

### Level 14: Illusory Reality
When casting an Illusion spell, as a Bonus Action you may choose one inanimate object in the illusion to make **temporarily real** for 1 minute.  
It cannot deal damage or impose conditions.
