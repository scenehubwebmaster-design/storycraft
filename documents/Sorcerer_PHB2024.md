# 🪄 **The Sorcerer**

> **Core Identity:** Innate arcane power shaped by bloodline or cosmic anomaly  
> **Primary Role:** Blaster / Utility Caster  
> **Complexity:** Moderate to High  
> **Power Source:** Internal (Bloodline / Wild Magic / Planar Essence)

Sorcerers are **born with magic in their veins**—their power a reflection of something extraordinary in their ancestry or fate. Whether a dragon’s bloodline, a touch of planar energy, or an echo of wild chaos, Sorcerers bend reality by instinct rather than study. Their power flows naturally, but mastery requires precision and will.

## ✨ Becoming a Sorcerer

**At Level 1:**  
- Gain all Core Sorcerer Traits.  
- Gain all Level 1 features from the class table.  

**As a Multiclass Character:**  
- Gain Hit Point Die (d6) per Sorcerer level.  
- Gain Level 1 features, including *Spellcasting*.  
- Determine available spell slots per multiclass rules.

## 🔮 Class Features

### Level 1 — Spellcasting

You learn to channel and shape the innate magic within you into spells. You cast spells from the **Sorcerer spell list** using **Charisma** as your spellcasting ability.

**Cantrips Known:** 4  
**Prepared Spells:** Two 1st-level spells of your choice.  
**Spellcasting Focus:** You can use an **Arcane Focus**.  
**Spell Save DC:** 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier.

> **Note:** Your connection to raw magic allows flexible preparation. You may replace one known spell whenever you gain a Sorcerer level.

---

### Level 1 — Innate Sorcery

> **Innate Burst:** Once per Long Rest, you can tap directly into your inner well of power.

For 1 minute:
- You gain **Advantage** on spell attack rolls.  
- Your **Spell Save DC** increases by +1.

You may use this feature twice per Long Rest.

---

### Level 2 — Font of Magic

You possess a reservoir of magical energy represented by **Sorcery Points (SP)**.

| **Spell Slot Level** | **SP Cost** |
|----------------------|-------------|
| 1st | 2 |
| 2nd | 3 |
| 3rd | 5 |
| 4th | 6 |
| 5th | 7 |

You can:
- **Convert SP → Spell Slots** (using the table above).  
- **Convert Spell Slots → SP** (slot level × 2).  
- Regain all Sorcery Points after a **Long Rest**.

> **Tip:** Use this to adapt to any situation—more firepower in combat, or extra slots for utility.

---

### Level 2 — Metamagic

Metamagic allows you to modify spells in remarkable ways. You learn **two Metamagic options** at this level and more as you gain levels.

#### Example Options
- **Careful Spell (1 SP):** Choose allies to automatically succeed on saves against your spell.  
- **Quickened Spell (2 SP):** Change a 1 Action spell to a Bonus Action.  
- **Twinned Spell (1+ SP):** Target an additional creature with a spell.  
- **Subtle Spell (1 SP):** Remove Verbal and Somatic components.  

> **Limit:** You can apply only **one Metamagic** per spell unless otherwise specified.

---

### Level 3 — Sorcerous Origin

At 3rd level, choose your **Sorcerous Origin**, which describes the source of your power.  
Options include:
- **Aberrant Sorcery** – Mind-twisting psionics from alien influence.  
- **Clockwork Sorcery** – Mechanus-born order and precision.  
- **Draconic Sorcery** – Bloodline of ancient dragons.  
- **Wild Magic Sorcery** – Chaotic, unpredictable spellcraft.

Your choice grants features at levels **3, 6, 14, and 18**.

---

### Level 4 — Ability Score Improvement

Increase one ability score by +2, or two by +1.  
Alternatively, you may take a **Feat** for which you qualify.  
You gain this feature again at levels 8, 10, 12, and 16.

---

### Level 5 — Sorcerous Restoration

After a **Short Rest**, you regain Sorcery Points equal to **half your Sorcerer level (rounded down)**.  
Once used, this feature cannot be used again until after a Long Rest.

---

### Level 6 — Subclass Feature

Gain the next ability granted by your chosen **Sorcerous Origin**.

---

### Level 7 — Sorcery Incarnate

When no uses of **Innate Sorcery** remain, you can spend **2 SP** to activate it.  
While active, you may apply **two Metamagic options** to the same spell simultaneously.

---

### Level 8 — Ability Score Improvement

You gain another **ASI** or **Feat** of your choice.

---

### Level 9 — No New Features

At this level, your magic continues to grow in potency, increasing available **Sorcery Points** to 9.

---

### Level 10 — Metamagic Expansion

You learn **two additional Metamagic options** of your choice.

# 🧠 **Sorcerer — Advanced Features & Subclasses**

---

## 🔮 Higher-Level Class Features

### Level 11 — Unyielding Power
Your connection to magic becomes nearly unbreakable.  
Whenever you fail a **Concentration check** to maintain a spell, you can spend **1 Sorcery Point** to succeed instead.

---

### Level 12 — Ability Score Improvement
Increase one ability score by +2, or two by +1.  
Alternatively, choose a Feat. You gain this feature again at level 16.

---

### Level 13 — Greater Magical Potency
Your sorcerous might empowers your lower-level magic.  
When casting a 1st–3rd level spell using a slot of that level, add **+1 to the spell’s damage per damage die** (to a maximum of +10 per spell).

---

### Level 14 — Subclass Feature
Gain the next ability granted by your chosen Sorcerous Origin.

---

### Level 15 — Arcane Resilience
As a Reaction when you take damage from a spell or magical effect, you can spend **2 SP** to gain **Resistance** to that damage type until the start of your next turn.

---

### Level 16 — Ability Score Improvement
As before, increase your ability scores or gain a Feat.

---

### Level 17 — Advanced Metamagic
You learn **two additional Metamagic options**.  
Additionally, once per Long Rest, you may apply a **Metamagic option without spending SP**.

---

### Level 18 — Subclass Feature
Gain the final, capstone feature of your Sorcerous Origin.

---

### Level 19 — Epic Boon
Gain one **Epic Boon Feat** (see Chapter 5).  
Recommended: *Boon of Dimensional Travel* or *Boon of Arcane Mastery.*

---

### Level 20 — Arcane Apotheosis
Your mastery of sorcery reaches its pinnacle.  
While **Innate Sorcery** is active:
- You regain **2 Sorcery Points** at the start of your turn.  
- Once per turn, you can apply **one Metamagic** to a spell **without SP cost**.

# 🧩 **Sorcerous Origins (Subclasses)**

---

## 🧠 Aberrant Sorcery — *Wield the Power of the Mind*

Your mind has been altered by alien influence, psionic mutation, or the touch of the Far Realm.  
You channel **psychic and aberrant energy** through your innate magic.

### Level 3 — Psionic Spells
These spells are always prepared for you:

| **Level** | **Spells** |
|------------|------------|
| 3 | Arms of Hadar, Dissonant Whispers |
| 5 | Hunger of Hadar, Sending |
| 7 | Evard’s Black Tentacles, Summon Aberration |
| 9 | Telekinesis, Rary’s Telepathic Bond |

### Level 3 — Telepathic Speech
As a Bonus Action, choose a creature within 30 ft.  
You and that creature can communicate telepathically for minutes equal to your Sorcerer level (range: 1 mile per Charisma modifier).

### Level 6 — Psionic Sorcery
You can cast any spell from your *Psionic Spells* feature using **Sorcery Points instead of slots**, requiring **no components**.

### Level 6 — Psychic Defenses
Gain **Resistance to Psychic damage** and **Advantage on saves** against being *Charmed* or *Frightened*.

### Level 14 — Revelation in Flesh
As a Bonus Action, spend **1+ SP** to transform for 10 minutes.  
Each SP grants one of the following:
- **Swim Adaptation:** Swim speed 2× walking; breathe underwater.  
- **Wings:** Fly speed = walking speed (hover).  
- **Infravision:** See invisible creatures within 60 ft.  
- **Elastic Form:** Move through 1-inch spaces; escape grapples with 5 ft. movement.

### Level 18 — Warping Implosion
As a Magic Action, teleport up to 120 ft.  
Each creature within 30 ft of your previous location makes a Strength save (DC = Spell Save DC) or takes **3d10 Force damage** and is pulled to where you were.  
> **Recharge:** Long Rest or spend 5 SP to reuse.

---

## ⚙️ Clockwork Sorcery — *Channel the Power of Order*

Magic from Mechanus or the cosmic gears of the universe has taken root in your soul.  
You embody precision, rhythm, and inevitability.

### Level 3 — Clockwork Spells

| **Level** | **Spells** |
|------------|------------|
| 3 | Alarm, Protection from Evil and Good |
| 5 | Dispel Magic, Protection from Energy |
| 7 | Freedom of Movement, Summon Construct |
| 9 | Greater Restoration, Wall of Force |

### Level 3 — Restore Balance
Use your Reaction when a creature within 60 ft gains Advantage or Disadvantage to **cancel that effect**.  
Uses = Charisma modifier (min 1), regained after Long Rest.

### Level 6 — Bastion of Law
As a Magic Action, spend **1–5 SP** to shield an ally within 30 ft.  
The ward absorbs **1d8 damage per SP spent**, lasting until a Long Rest or until replaced.

### Level 14 — Trance of Order
As a Bonus Action, enter a 1-minute trance:
- Attack rolls against you can’t have Advantage.  
- Any d20 Test roll of 9 or lower counts as 10.  
> Recharge: Long Rest or spend 5 SP.

### Level 18 — Clockwork Cavalcade
Summon spirits of order in a 30-ft cube:
- **Heal:** Distribute 100 HP among creatures in the area.  
- **Repair:** Instantly repair objects.  
- **Dispel:** End spells of 6th level or lower in the area.  
> Recharge: Long Rest or spend 7 SP.

## 🐉 Draconic Sorcery — *Blood of Dragons*

Your bloodline or fate is bound to dragons.  
Scales shimmer under your skin, and your breath carries elemental force.

### Level 3 — Draconic Resilience
- Increase maximum HP by 3 + 1 per Sorcerer level.  
- When not wearing armor, your AC = 10 + DEX + CHA.

### Level 3 — Draconic Spells

| **Level** | **Spells** |
|------------|------------|
| 3 | Chromatic Orb, Command |
| 5 | Fear, Fly |
| 7 | Arcane Eye, Charm Monster |
| 9 | Summon Dragon, Legend Lore |

### Level 6 — Elemental Affinity
Choose a damage type: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison.  
Gain Resistance to that type and add your **CHA modifier** to one damage roll of a matching spell.

### Level 14 — Dragon Wings
As a Bonus Action, sprout spectral wings granting **Fly speed equal to your walking speed**.  
You can dismiss or manifest them at will.

### Level 18 — Draconic Presence
As a Magic Action, exude a powerful aura of awe or fear (your choice).  
Each creature of your choice within 60 ft must make a Wisdom save or be **Charmed or Frightened** (your choice) for 1 minute.  
> Recharge: Long Rest or spend 5 SP.

---

## 🎲 Wild Magic Sorcery — *Embrace the Chaos*

Your magic surges unpredictably, bending reality in strange ways.  
Every spell risks an explosion of chaos.

### Level 3 — Wild Magic Surge
When you cast a Sorcerer spell of 1st level or higher, roll a d20.  
On a 1, roll on the **Wild Magic Surge Table** for a random magical effect.

> **Example Surge Effects:**
> - A burst of pink fog fills a 20 ft radius.  
> - You teleport 30 ft in a random direction.  
> - You regain 5 HP.  
> - You cast *Fireball* centered on yourself.

### Level 3 — Tides of Chaos
Gain Advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw.  
After using it, you must trigger a Wild Magic Surge to regain this feature.

### Level 6 — Bend Luck
As a Reaction, spend **2 SP** to alter fate.  
Roll 1d4 and add or subtract it from another creature’s d20 roll.

### Level 14 — Controlled Chaos
When rolling on the Wild Magic Surge Table, roll **two times** and choose which result to apply.

### Level 18 — Spell Bombardment
When you roll damage for a spell and roll the highest number possible on any die, you may roll one additional die of the same type and add it to the total.

# 🪄 Summary

> **The Sorcerer is a vessel of pure magic — instinctive, creative, and volatile.**  
> Where Wizards study and Warlocks bargain, Sorcerers simply *are*. Their inner energy is both a gift and a burden — one that, in the right hands, can reshape worlds.
