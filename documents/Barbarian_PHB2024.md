# Barbarian

> **“Fueled by primal fury, the barbarian channels raw emotion into unmatched might.”**

Barbarians are mighty warriors powered by primal forces that manifest as **Rage** — a force of nature more than mere emotion.  
Their fury is the storm, the predator’s hunger, and the ocean’s turbulence made flesh.

Some Barbarians see their Rage as a spirit or ancestral force; others view it as the anguish of the world, the pulse of untamed magic, or an expression of their truest self.  
Whatever the source, it grants them extraordinary power, speed, and resilience.

Barbarians often serve as protectors and leaders, rushing headlong into danger to shield others.  
Their courage makes them natural adventurers.

---

## Core Barbarian Traits

| **Trait**                | **Details**                                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary Ability**      | Strength                                                                                        |
| **Hit Point Die**        | `1d12` per Barbarian level                                                                      |
| **Saving Throws**        | Strength, Constitution                                                                          |
| **Skill Proficiencies**  | Choose 2: _Animal Handling_, _Athletics_, _Intimidation_, _Nature_, _Perception_, or _Survival_ |
| **Weapon Proficiencies** | Simple and Martial weapons                                                                      |
| **Armor Training**       | Light armor, Medium armor, and Shields                                                          |
| **Starting Equipment**   | (A) Greataxe, 4 Handaxes, Explorer’s Pack, and 15 GP — or — (B) 75 GP                           |

## Becoming a Barbarian

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all the traits from the **Core Barbarian Traits** table.
- Gain the Barbarian’s level 1 features listed in the **Barbarian Features** table.

### As a Multiclass Character

If you multiclass into Barbarian:

- Gain Hit Point Die (`1d12`), proficiency with Martial weapons, and Shield training.
- Gain the Barbarian’s level 1 features.

## Barbarian Class Features

As you level up, you gain the features listed below.

### Level 1: Rage

As a **Bonus Action**, you can enter a **Rage**, channeling primal energy for strength and resilience.  
You can Rage the number of times shown for your level in the Barbarian Features table.

You regain **1 Rage use** after a Short Rest and **all uses** after a Long Rest.

#### While Raging:

- **Damage Resistance.** You have resistance to _Bludgeoning_, _Piercing_, and _Slashing_ damage.
- **Rage Damage.** When making a Strength-based attack (weapon or unarmed), add bonus damage (see table below).
- **Strength Advantage.** You have advantage on Strength checks and saving throws.
- **No Spells or Concentration.** You can’t cast or concentrate on spells.
- **Duration.** Rage lasts until the end of your next turn, extending each round if you:
  - Make an attack roll,
  - Force a saving throw, or
  - Take a Bonus Action to extend your Rage.  
    You can maintain Rage for up to **10 minutes**.  
    Rage ends early if you don Heavy Armor or become _Incapacitated_.

---

### Level 1: Unarmored Defense

While not wearing armor, your **AC = 10 + Dexterity mod + Constitution mod**.  
You may use a Shield and still gain this benefit.

---

### Level 1: Weapon Mastery

Your training lets you exploit special properties of weapons.  
Choose **two** kinds of Simple or Martial melee weapons (e.g., _Greataxe_, _Handaxe_).  
You can change one of these after each Long Rest.

As you level, you gain mastery with more weapon types.

### Level 2: Danger Sense

Gain an uncanny awareness of impending threats.  
You have **Advantage on Dexterity saving throws** unless _Incapacitated_.

---

### Level 2: Reckless Attack

When you make your first attack each turn, you can choose to attack **recklessly**:

- Gain advantage on Strength-based attack rolls this turn.
- Attack rolls against you have advantage until your next turn.

---

### Level 3: Primal Knowledge

You gain proficiency in one additional skill from the Barbarian skill list.

While Raging, you can make Strength-based checks for:

> _Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth,_ or _Survival_  
> representing primal power enhancing your instincts.

---

### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

Gain an **Ability Score Improvement** or a feat of your choice (repeats at levels 8, 12, 16).

---

### Level 5: Extra Attack

When you take the Attack action, you can attack **twice** instead of once.

### Level 5: Fast Movement

Increase your walking speed by **10 feet** while not wearing Heavy Armor.

---

### Level 7: Feral Instinct

You have **Advantage on Initiative rolls**.

### Level 7: Instinctive Pounce

When you enter a Rage (Bonus Action), you can move up to **half your Speed** immediately.

---

### Level 9: Brutal Strike

When using Reckless Attack, you can forgo its advantage on one Strength attack.  
If that attack hits, add `1d10` damage and apply one **Brutal Strike** effect:

> **Brutal Strike Effects**
>
> - _Forceful Blow:_ Push the target 15 ft away; you may move half your Speed toward it (no opportunity attacks).
> - _Hamstring Blow:_ Reduce target’s Speed by 15 ft until your next turn.

---

### Level 11: Relentless Rage

When you drop to 0 HP (but not killed outright) while Raging:  
Make a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw** — on success, drop to HP = `2 × Barbarian level`.  
Each subsequent use increases DC by 5 (resets on rest).

---

### Level 13: Improved Brutal Strike

Gain new **Brutal Strike** options:

> - _Staggering Blow:_ Target has disadvantage on next saving throw and can’t make opportunity attacks until your next turn.
> - _Sundering Blow:_ The next attack against that target gains +5 to hit (only once per target).

---

### Level 15: Persistent Rage

When you roll initiative, regain all expended **Rage uses** (once per Long Rest).  
Rage now lasts **10 minutes** automatically and ends only if you become _Unconscious_ or don Heavy Armor.

---

### Level 17: Improved Brutal Strike (Enhanced)

Your Brutal Strike bonus damage increases to `2d10`, and you can apply **two** different Brutal Strike effects.

---

### Level 18: Indomitable Might

If your total for a Strength check or save is less than your **Strength score**, use the score instead.

---

### Level 19: Epic Boon

Gain an **Epic Boon** feat (recommended: _Boon of Irresistible Offense_).

---

### Level 20: Primal Champion

You embody primal power itself.  
Increase **Strength and Constitution by +4**, up to a maximum of **25**.

## Barbarian Subclasses

Barbarian subclasses are called **Paths** — specializations representing different primal callings.

Available Paths:

- **Path of the Berserker** – Rage into violent fury
- **Path of the Wild Heart** – Kinship with beasts and nature
- **Path of the World Tree** – Channel cosmic vitality
- **Path of the Zealot** – Rage as divine ecstasy

---

### Path of the Berserker

> _“Channel rage into unrestrained violence.”_

**Level 3 – Frenzy**  
When you use Reckless Attack during Rage, the first hit deals extra damage equal to your Rage Damage in `d6s`.

**Level 6 – Mindless Rage**  
Immune to _Charmed_ and _Frightened_ conditions while Raging.  
If affected when entering Rage, those conditions end.

**Level 10 – Retaliation**  
When damaged by a creature within 5 ft, take a **Reaction** to make a melee attack against it.

**Level 14 – Intimidating Presence**  
As a **Bonus Action**, unleash primal terror:  
All chosen creatures within 30 ft must make a Wisdom save (DC = 8 + Strength mod + PB).  
On fail, they are _Frightened_ for 1 minute (repeat save each turn).  
You can use this once per Long Rest, or expend one Rage use to recharge it.

---

### Path of the Wild Heart

> _“Walk in communion with the animal world.”_

**Level 3 – Animal Speaker**  
You can cast _Beast Sense_ and _Speak with Animals_ as rituals (Wisdom = spellcasting ability).

**Level 3 – Rage of the Wilds**  
When you Rage, choose one primal aspect:

- **Bear.** Resistance to all damage types except _Force_, _Necrotic_, _Psychic_, _Radiant_.
- **Eagle.** When you Rage, take _Dash_ and _Disengage_ as part of that Bonus Action.
- **Wolf.** Allies gain Advantage on attacks against enemies within 5 ft of you.

**Level 6 – Aspect of the Wilds**  
Choose one aspect (changeable after a Long Rest):

- **Owl:** Gain or extend Darkvision by 60 ft.
- **Panther:** Gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.
- **Salmon:** Gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

**Level 10 – Nature Speaker**  
You can cast _Commune with Nature_ as a ritual (Wisdom = spellcasting ability).

**Level 14 – Power of the Wilds**  
When you Rage, choose one:

- **Falcon.** Gain Fly Speed equal to your Speed (if unarmored).
- **Lion.** Enemies within 5 ft have Disadvantage attacking anyone but you (or another Barbarian with this option).
- **Ram.** When you hit a Large or smaller creature with a melee attack, you can knock it _Prone_.

---

### Path of the World Tree

> _“Tap into Yggdrasil’s life force and cosmic branches.”_

**Level 3 – Vitality of the Tree**  
When you Rage:

- Gain Temporary HP = your Barbarian level.
- Each turn, grant another creature within 10 ft Temp HP equal to your Rage Damage in `d6s`.

**Level 6 – Branches of the Tree**  
As a **Reaction**, summon spectral branches when a creature starts its turn within 30 ft:  
The target must make a Strength save (DC = 8 + Strength mod + PB) or be teleported to a space within 5 ft of you.  
Reduce its Speed to 0 until end of turn.

**Level 10 – Battering Roots**  
Your reach extends by 10 ft with Heavy or Versatile melee weapons.  
When you hit, you can apply _Push_ or _Topple_ mastery properties in addition to another mastery.

**Level 14 – Travel Along the Tree**  
When you Rage, or as a Bonus Action during Rage, **teleport up to 60 ft**.  
Once per Rage, increase range to 150 ft and bring up to six willing allies within 10 ft of you.

---

### Path of the Zealot

> _“Rage in ecstatic union with divine power.”_

**Level 3 – Divine Fury**  
Once per turn while Raging, your first hit deals extra damage = `1d6 + ½ Barbarian level` (_Necrotic_ or _Radiant_).

**Level 3 – Warrior of the Gods**  
You have a pool of `4d12` healing dice.  
As a Bonus Action, expend any number to heal that many HP.  
Regain all dice after a Long Rest.  
Gain additional dice at levels 6 (5d12), 12 (6d12), and 17 (7d12).

**Level 6 – Fanatical Focus**  
Once per Rage, reroll a failed saving throw and add your Rage Damage bonus.

**Level 10 – Zealous Presence**  
As a Bonus Action, unleash a divine cry.  
Up to 10 creatures of your choice within 60 ft gain **Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws** until your next turn.  
Recharge after a Long Rest, or expend a Rage use to restore.

**Level 14 – Rage of the Gods**  
When you Rage, assume a divine form for 1 minute (or until 0 HP).  
While transformed:

- **Flight.** You gain Fly Speed equal to your Speed and can hover.
