# D&D Campaign Starters & Templates

This document contains pre-built campaign starters, adventure hooks, and opening scenes that the AI DM can use to begin adventures. These templates are indexed in the RAG system for retrieval during campaign creation.

---

## 🎭 Classic Opening Scenes

### 1. **The Tavern Meeting** (Most Classic)

**Setting:** A cozy tavern in a frontier town  
**Hook:** Strangers brought together by fate, rumor, or a mysterious patron  
**Opening Narration:**

> "The warm glow of the hearth illuminates worn wooden tables as travelers from distant lands find themselves sharing space in the [Tavern Name]. Outside, rain patters against shuttered windows. Each of you has come to this frontier town for your own reasons, but tonight, destiny has other plans..."

**Common Variations:**

- Mysterious hooded figure offers a job
- Overheard conversation about treasure/danger
- Bar fight breaks out, forcing party to work together
- Innkeeper pleads for help with local problem

---

### 2. **The Caravan Guards**

**Setting:** On the road, escorting merchants  
**Hook:** Hired to protect valuable cargo, bonds form during travel  
**Opening Narration:**

> "The morning sun rises over the dusty trade road as your caravan prepares for another day's journey. You've been hired as guards, each for your own skills. Three days into the journey to [Destination], you've begun to learn each other's names. Today, that familiarity may save your lives..."

**Common Variations:**

- Bandits attack the caravan
- Mysterious cargo reveals itself
- Another guard is a traitor
- Caravan master has secret mission

---

### 3. **Prison Break**

**Setting:** Dark dungeon, wrongfully (or rightfully) imprisoned  
**Hook:** Must work together to escape, then clear names  
**Opening Narration:**

> "Cold stone. The drip of water. The rattle of chains. You awaken in darkness, your head pounding. How long have you been here? Days? Weeks? Around you, others stir in their cells. Above, you hear the guards changing shift. This may be your only chance..."

**Common Variations:**

- Innocent prisoners frame by villain
- Gladiator slaves planning escape
- Political prisoners of corrupt regime
- Wrongfully convicted, real culprit still free

---

### 4. **The Festival Disaster**

**Setting:** Bustling town festival or celebration  
**Hook:** Celebration turns to chaos, heroes must respond  
**Opening Narration:**

> "The Festival of [Event] fills the town square with music, laughter, and the smell of roasted meats. Colorful banners stream from every building. Children chase each other between market stalls. You've come to enjoy the festivities, each drawn by different attractions. Then, the screaming begins..."

**Common Variations:**

- Monster attacks during celebration
- Assassination attempt on noble
- Magical accident unleashes chaos
- Thieves use festival as cover for heist

---

### 5. **The Summons**

**Setting:** Various locations, characters receive mysterious messages  
**Hook:** Each character independently called to a meeting  
**Opening Narration:**

> "The letter arrived three days ago, sealed with an unfamiliar sigil. It knew your name. It knew your skills. Most unsettling, it knew things you've never told anyone. The message was clear: Come to [Location] at sunset. Come alone. Refuse, and someone you love will suffer. Now, as twilight paints the sky orange, you arrive at the appointed place. You are not alone..."

**Common Variations:**

- Powerful wizard needs heroes for quest
- Dying mentor calls former students
- Mysterious benefactor has job offer
- Ancient organization recruits new members

## 📜 Campaign Types & Templates

### **ONE-SHOT ADVENTURES** (1-2 Sessions)

#### **The Haunted Manor**

**Level:** 1-3  
**Type:** Mystery/Horror  
**Synopsis:** Local noble's abandoned manor is cursed. Villagers report strange lights and screams. Party investigates.  
**Key Elements:**

- Undead servants protecting a dark secret
- Hidden treasure vault
- Tragic backstory of the manor's fall
- Restless spirit that needs help moving on

**AI DM Prompt Template:**

```
You are the DM for "The Haunted Manor" one-shot adventure. The party has been hired by the village elder to investigate the abandoned Blackwood Manor on the hill.

Setup: The manor has been empty for 20 years since the Blackwood family disappeared one stormy night. Recently, villagers have seen lights in the windows and heard screams. Three villagers who approached the manor never returned.

Key NPCs:
- Elder Thornwick (quest giver, fearful but desperate)
- Ghost of Lady Blackwood (trapped, needs help)
- Corrupted butler (undead, guarding the vault)

Story Beats:
1. Investigation of the manor exterior
2. First encounter with minor undead
3. Discovery of family journals revealing tragedy
4. Confrontation with the corrupted butler
5. Resolution: Lay Lady Blackwood's spirit to rest

Treasure: 500 gp, +1 weapon, scroll of protection
```

---

#### **The Goblin Raiders**

**Level:** 1-2  
**Type:** Combat/Rescue  
**Synopsis:** Goblins have kidnapped villagers. Track them to their lair and rescue the prisoners.  
**Key Elements:**

- Tracking through forest
- Goblin warren with multiple chambers
- Boss fight with goblin chief
- Rescue mission complications

**AI DM Prompt Template:**

```
You are the DM for "The Goblin Raiders" combat-focused adventure. The party must rescue kidnapped villagers from a goblin lair.

Setup: Last night, goblins raided the village of Millbrook, kidnapping the mayor's daughter and three farmers. The party tracks them to a cave system in the nearby hills.

Lair Layout:
- Entrance: 2 goblin guards
- Main chamber: 6 goblins + 1 goblin boss
- Prison area: Hostages held in cages
- Treasure room: Stolen goods from raids

Combat Encounters:
1. Forest ambush (4 goblins)
2. Cave entrance (2 goblin guards + wolf)
3. Main chamber (goblin chief + 6 warriors)

Rescue Complication: Hostages are weak, one is injured and needs healing

Treasure: Stolen items worth 200 gp, goblin chief's magical dagger (+1)
```

### **SHORT ADVENTURES** (3-6 Sessions)

#### **The Cult of the Crimson Eye**

**Level:** 3-5  
**Type:** Investigation/Mystery  
**Synopsis:** A sinister cult is performing dark rituals. Party must uncover their plan before a blood moon ritual.  
**Key Elements:**

- Urban investigation
- Multiple suspects and red herrings
- Race against time
- Ritual must be stopped before completion

**AI DM Prompt Template:**

```
You are the DM for "The Cult of the Crimson Eye" short adventure. The party investigates a death cult planning a catastrophic ritual.

Campaign Arc:
Session 1: Discovery of first victim, ritual markings
Session 2: Investigation in city, gathering clues
Session 3: Infiltration of cult meeting
Session 4: Discovery of ritual site
Session 5: Final confrontation at blood moon ritual

Key NPCs:
- Captain Ironfist (city guard, overwhelmed)
- Brother Aldric (helpful priest with knowledge of cults)
- Magistrate Blackthorn (secretly cult member)
- Cult Leader (charismatic, powerful warlock)

Mystery Elements:
- Victims all have crimson eye burned into forehead
- Ritual requires sacrifice under blood moon (5 days away)
- Cult has infiltrated city government
- Plan: Summon demon to possess city leader

Locations:
- City Watch headquarters
- Temple of Light
- Seedy tavern (cult meeting place)
- Abandoned cathedral (ritual site)

Treasure: 1,500 gp, +1 armor, spell scrolls, cult leader's staff
```

#### **The Lost Mine of Phandelver** (Inspired by D&D Starter Set)

**Level:** 1-5  
**Type:** Classic Adventure  
**Synopsis:** Find a lost mine, rescue a kidnapped dwarf, defeat bandits and monsters.  
**Key Elements:**

- Dungeon crawling
- Town base (Phandalin)
- Multiple quest hooks
- Epic final dungeon

**AI DM Prompt Template:**

```
You are the DM for a classic D&D adventure inspired by "Lost Mine of Phandelver." The party seeks a legendary lost mine and rescues a kidnapped dwarf.

Campaign Structure:
Arc 1 (Sessions 1-2): Travel to Phandalin, goblin ambush, rescue dwarf from Cragmaw Hideout
Arc 2 (Sessions 3-4): Phandalin as base, multiple side quests, dealing with Redbrand bandits
Arc 3 (Sessions 5-6): Finding Wave Echo Cave, confronting the Black Spider, claiming the mine

Key NPCs:
- Gundren Rockseeker (dwarf quest giver, kidnapped)
- Sildar Hallwinter (noble fighter, rescued alongside Gundren)
- Halia Thornton (merchant, has side quests)
- Glasstaff (Redbrand leader, wizard)
- The Black Spider (main villain, drow mage)

Major Locations:
1. Cragmaw Hideout (goblin cave, session 1-2)
2. Phandalin (town hub)
3. Redbrand Hideout (bandit base under manor)
4. Cragmaw Castle (goblin fortress, mid-adventure)
5. Wave Echo Cave (final dungeon, the lost mine)

Story Progression:
- Party hired to escort supplies to Phandalin
- Gundren goes ahead, gets captured
- Party rescues Gundren, learns of lost mine
- Redbrand bandits terrorizing Phandalin
- Black Spider also seeks the mine
- Final confrontation in Wave Echo Cave

Treasure Progression:
- Early: Basic weapons, 100-200 gp
- Mid: +1 weapon, spell scroll, 500 gp
- Final: Legendary mine claim, +1 armor, choice of magic items, 2,000+ gp
```

### **EPIC CAMPAIGNS** (10+ Sessions)

#### **Tyranny of Dragons** (Inspired)

**Level:** 1-15  
**Type:** World-threatening Evil  
**Synopsis:** Cult of the Dragon attempts to summon Tiamat. Party must stop dragon cult across multiple story arcs.  
**Key Elements:**

- Dragon encounters at various levels
- Political intrigue
- Epic battles
- World-traveling adventure

**AI DM Prompt Template:**

```
You are the DM for an epic dragon-themed campaign. The Cult of the Dragon plots to summon Tiamat, Queen of Evil Dragons. This is a multi-arc campaign spanning levels 1-15.

Campaign Overview:
Arc 1 (Level 1-4): "Hoard of the Dragon Queen"
- Cult raids town for treasure to summon Tiamat
- Party tracks cult caravan to hidden camp
- Infiltrate cult stronghold

Arc 2 (Level 5-8): "Rise of Tiamat"
- Cult gathers dragon masks
- Political councils decide world's response
- Party hunts down high-ranking cultists

Arc 3 (Level 9-12): "Dragon Hunts"
- Adult dragons allied with cult terrorize regions
- Party must stop rituals empowering dragons
- Gather allies for final battle

Arc 4 (Level 13-15): "The Summoning"
- Cult attempts to summon Tiamat
- Epic battle at the Well of Dragons
- Fate of the world hangs in balance

Key NPCs:
- Leosin Erlanthar (monk spy, early ally)
- Ontharr Frume (paladin, council member)
- Severin Silrajin (cult leader, charismatic)
- Various dragon allies and enemies

Dragon Encounters (scaled by level):
- Level 2: Young white dragon
- Level 5: Adult black dragon
- Level 8: Adult green dragon
- Level 11: Adult red dragon
- Level 15: Tiamat herself (avatar)

Major Locations:
- Greenest (starting town)
- Cult stronghold in Wyrmspeaker's lair
- Council of Waterdeep (political center)
- Flying castle (cult mobile base)
- Well of Dragons (final dungeon)

Progression:
Sessions 1-10: Discovering the cult's plan
Sessions 11-20: Building alliances, hunting cult leaders
Sessions 21-30: Preparing for final battle
Sessions 31-40: Epic confrontation, saving the world
```

## 🎲 Opening Scene Templates by Party Size

### **Solo Adventure** (1 Player)

```
You are [Character Name], a [Class] seeking [Goal] in the [Setting]. Your journey has brought you to [Location], where whispers speak of [Hook]. As you [Activity], you notice [Inciting Incident]...
```

### **Duo Adventure** (2 Players)

```
[Character 1 Name] and [Character 2 Name] have been traveling together for [Duration]. Your partnership began when [Backstory], and now you find yourselves in [Location]. The uneasy peace is shattered when [Inciting Incident]...
```

### **Standard Party** (3-5 Players)

```
Fate, coincidence, or perhaps destiny has brought you all to [Location]. Some of you know each other, others are strangers. But when [Inciting Incident] occurs, you must choose: Stand together, or face the danger alone?
```

### **Large Party** (6+ Players)

```
The [Organization/Event] has assembled you, a diverse group of skilled individuals, for [Purpose]. As you meet in [Location], it's clear this won't be a simple task. The true challenge reveals itself when [Inciting Incident]...
```

## 📋 Campaign Tone & Theme Options

### **Heroic High Fantasy**

- Clear good vs evil
- Epic quests to save the world
- Powerful magic is common
- Dragons, demons, and gods as enemies
- Inspiration: Lord of the Rings

### **Gritty Dark Fantasy**

- Morally grey choices
- Survival is a victory
- Magic is rare and dangerous
- Realistic consequences
- Inspiration: Game of Thrones, Warhammer

### **Political Intrigue**

- Court politics and backstabbing
- Social encounters > combat
- Information is power
- Noble houses and factions
- Inspiration: Dune, House of Cards

### **Dungeon Crawl**

- Focus on exploration and combat
- Mega-dungeon with many levels
- Treasure hunting
- Minimal roleplaying
- Inspiration: Classic D&D

### **Mystery & Investigation**

- Solving crimes and puzzles
- Gathering clues
- Social deduction
- Plot twists and red herrings
- Inspiration: Sherlock Holmes, Ace Attorney

### **Horror & Dread**

- Atmosphere of fear
- Sanity mechanics
- Cosmic horrors
- Survival horror elements
- Inspiration: Call of Cthulhu, Bloodborne

### **Lighthearted Comedy**

- Silly and fun
- Minimal character death
- Pop culture references
- Not taking itself seriously
- Inspiration: Pratchett's Discworld

## 🗺️ Setting Templates

### **Forgotten Realms** (Classic D&D)

Major cities: Waterdeep, Baldur's Gate, Neverwinter  
Regions: Sword Coast, Cormyr, Thay  
Themes: Classic high fantasy, gods are active, magic is common

### **Eberron** (Pulp Action)

Major cities: Sharn, Stormreach, Flamekeep  
Themes: Magic technology, noir mystery, post-war politics  
Unique: Warforged, airships, dragonmarked houses

### **Ravenloft** (Gothic Horror)

Major locations: Castle Ravenloft, Barovia  
Themes: Trapped in mist-shrouded domains, dark lords, gothic horror  
Unique: Domains of dread, mist travel, Strahd von Zarovich

### **Spelljammer** (Space Fantasy)

Major locations: Rock of Bral, various crystal spheres  
Themes: Sailing between planets, cosmic adventure, weird aliens  
Unique: Spelljamming ships, wildspace, phlogiston

### **Homebrew Custom**

DM creates unique world with player input  
Themes: Anything goes  
Benefit: Tailored to party interests

- Serial killer stalks the city

- Recover lost spellbook

- Scout enemy positions

- Negotiate peace between factions
- Convince noble to join alliance

## 💡 Random Adventure Generator Elements

### **Villains**

- Evil wizard seeking immortality
- Corrupt noble plotting coup
- Monster threatening village
- Cult summoning demon
- Bandit king terrorizing roads
- Dragon demanding tribute
- Undead lord raising army

### **MacGuffins**

- Ancient artifact of power
- Kidnapped person of importance
- Rare magical ingredient
- Treasure map to lost hoard
- Sacred relic of a god
- Royal crown (legitimate claim)
- Spell scroll of forbidden magic

### **Locations**

- Abandoned castle
- Deep forest grove
- Underground city
- Floating tower
- Volcanic cavern
- Frozen wasteland
- Desert pyramid

### **Twists**

- Quest giver is the real villain
- MacGuffin is cursed/fake
- Villain is sympathetic
- Greater evil behind everything
- Party member is traitor
- Quest was test of character
- Time loop trap

## 🎪 Session Zero Considerations

Before starting the campaign, discuss:

1. **Campaign Length**: One-shot, short, or epic?
2. **Tone**: Serious, comedic, dark, heroic?
3. **Content Warnings**: What topics to avoid?
4. **Player Agency**: Railroad vs sandbox?
5. **Lethality**: Easy, normal, deadly?
6. **House Rules**: Any modifications?
7. **Session Length**: How long per session?
8. **Frequency**: Weekly, biweekly, monthly?
9. **Party Dynamics**: Pre-existing relationships?
10. **Character Secrets**: Allowed backstory hooks?
